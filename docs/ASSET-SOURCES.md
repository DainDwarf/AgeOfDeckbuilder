# Asset sources

Where free art, sound, music and fonts come from when the look is built (v0.0.6 on the
[roadmap](ROADMAP.md)). The dogmas set the constraints: no generative AI in any asset, and every
pack entering `public/assets/` gets its entry in `public/assets/LICENSES.md` the same moment.
The art-style call — pixel art first, flat vector second — is made against what these sites
actually hold in depth.

Licence shorthand: **CC0** needs nothing; **CC BY** needs the author credited in the licence
file; **OFL** is the open font licence, fonts embed freely. Anything else is read per pack.

## Art

- [Kenney](https://kenney.nl/assets) — the deepest free supply, everything CC0. Strong on flat
  vector, UI and board-game packs; thinner on hex-strategy pixel art, though hex packs exist.
- [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org) — community archive, mixed quality and licences. Filter
  by licence before browsing. The hunting ground for hex terrain tilesets and unit sprites.
- [itch.io free game assets](https://itch.io/game-assets/free) — many small pixel-art packs by
  individual artists. Licences vary per pack; several forbid redistributing the raw files.
- [Game-icons.net](https://game-icons.net) — several thousand flat monochrome icons, CC BY 3.0.
  Resource chips and card symbols.

## Sound

- [Freesound](https://freesound.org) — sound effects, CC0 and CC BY, filterable by licence.
- [Kenney audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/category:Audio) — CC0 UI and impact sounds.

## Music

- [Incompetech](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/) — Kevin MacLeod's catalogue, CC BY
  with a fixed attribution string.
- [OpenGameArt music](https://opengameart.org/art-search-advanced?field_art_type_tid%5B%5D=12) —
  many CC0 and CC BY loops.

## Fonts

- [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com) — OFL faces, including pixel faces such as Press
  Start 2P and Silkscreen.
