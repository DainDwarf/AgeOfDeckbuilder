# Asset licences

Every pack in this folder is recorded here the moment it enters the repository, the store's packs among them: its name, where it came from, its licence, and what the licence requires (attribution text, share-alike, a no-resale clause). A pack with no entry here does not ship.

## Hex and Tile Terrain Sample Set

- **What:** David Baumgart's free sample set, its hex half, in `store/hex-and-tile-samples/`, one folder per set the samples draw from, his readme beside them; the square tiles left out.
- **From:** David Baumgart, <https://dgbaumgart.itch.io/>, version 1.0.1 of December 2024, the files as released.
- **Licence:** his own terms, in the readme: a free or commercial game may use them, modified and remixed at will; no training of an image model on them; never repackaged, sold or given away on their own. Credit to "David Baumgart" is asked, a link to <https://dgbaumgart.com> welcome. The files sit in the store, never in this repository.

## Source Sans 3

- **What:** the UI typeface, regular and bold, in `fonts/source-sans-3/`.
- **From:** Adobe, <https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-sans>, release 3.052R, the files as released.
- **Licence:** SIL Open Font License 1.1, the text in `fonts/source-sans-3/LICENSE.md`, Reserved Font Name "Source". The licence ships with the files; a modified version may not be called Source; no credit is required.
