# Asset licences

Every pack in this folder is recorded here the moment it enters the repository, the store's packs among them: its name, where it came from, its licence, and what the licence requires (attribution text, share-alike, a no-resale clause), and, where the pack takes a line on the Credits window, its credit: what it gave, then who gave it. A pack with no entry here does not ship.
