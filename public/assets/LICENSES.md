Every pack in this folder is recorded here the moment it enters the repository, the store's packs among them: its name, where it came from, its licence, and what the licence requires (attribution text, share-alike, a no-resale clause). A pack with no entry here does not ship.

Credit to "David Baumgart" is asked, a link to <https://dgbaumgart.com> welcome. The files sit in the store, never in this repository.
