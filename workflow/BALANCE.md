# Balance findings

The balance issues met in play, kept here until the headless simulator is good enough to inform a decision on them; the balance pass rung reads this file when its turn comes, and an entry leaves when the pass settles it. An entry is what was seen, where, and the lead if there is one — no numbers proposed, no fix designed. Nothing here is a board line.

## Nomadic Age

- **Military off the Hearth, onto flint**: the Hearth's yield loses its military and flint gives military instead of production. Tried before and rejected; the Nomadic Age reads chill enough now to carry it.
- **The rival band is rough**: the band itself needs balancing, and its event's answers are no real choice — "fight them" was never taken.

## Stone Age

- **The neutral gets pummelled by the barbarians**: seen in two games out of two. Lead: a neutral that starts on desert is weak by construction, which may be the whole cause rather than the camps' strength.
- **Beast Bones**: the tools become a trophy as well, and more production.

## Across the ages

- **Production is under-valued**: a stock of 57 production on the first cold night. Card costs, event prices and capstones all price it too low.
