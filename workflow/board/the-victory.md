# The victory

**Line:** The victory — the Stone Age's victory is its achievement, Metalworking its technology, needing Settlement and unlocking nothing until the Bronze Age exists; each age names its victory among its achievements and the next age follows from the order of history; a victory technology's plate stands on the border at its ground's right end, after every other technology of the age, no link to it, the last ground washing into the colour of the age to come. Doc-impact: `docs/ages/STONE.md`, `docs/META.md`, `docs/META-SCREENS.md`.

**Scope:**

- In: the Stone Age's victory achievement, `metalworking`, counting 1 once the chronicle ends in victory toward a need of 1, earning the technology `metalworking`, paying 5 influence (provisional, a start); the technology needs `settlement` and unlocks no card, no region, no building and no age. Its plate reads the name, Goal and the goal, Reward and the influence alone: no age line, since no next age exists to read. It is pinned the moment Settlement is learned, as the four first-tier technologies are, so a Stone chronicle reads it among its achievements and shows it pinned until the player takes the pin off; the ending ledger reads it on a Stone victory with its influence. Standing rules all; nothing on the chronicle screen or the ending screen changes.
- In, the mechanism, landed as an inert commit before the content: each age declares its victory, the id of one of its own achievements. The technology's own age-unlock declaration goes: the age a technology opens is derived, the age after the one whose victory's technology it is, none for the last age. Every reader of the old declaration reads the derived one: the ages a campaign has reached (the first, and every age whose previous age's victory technology is learned), the plate's reward line naming the age, the tree's borders, and the e2e helpers and specs that look an age's unlocker up. The catalogue refuses an age naming a victory it does not own; the four age-unlock refusals (the first age unlocked, an age unlocked by nothing, by two, by a technology of another age) go with the declaration they checked, their tests with them. The Nomadic Age names `first-shelter`, the Stone Age `metalworking`, each fixture age its own victory, the fixture's victory technologies unlocking nothing. The three identical victory counts (the Nomadic's, the fixture's, the Stone's) go through one rules helper beside the other achievement helpers.
- In, the layout: an age's victory technology stands in the column after the age's furthest other column, on the border, whatever it needs: the first age's at column 0 as today. An age's ground runs from the border before it to the middle of its own victory's column; the last ground ends there too, and the wash, centred on that end as on every border, runs from the ground's colour into the colour of the age after the last, a new entry of the look named for that meaning, bronze, provisionally `0x6b4a26`, in the register of the two grounds; from the wash's end to the tree's right end that colour fills the room. The tree's right end stands a lead (200) past the last column's middle, as today, so it stands 82 past the victory plate's right edge, the 82 that stand before Settlement's left edge. The screen opening on the tree's right end where no technology is available keeps its rule. No link is drawn to any victory technology, Settlement's from nothing as today, Metalworking's from Settlement not drawn.
- Out: the Bronze Age, its ground and anything it unlocks; the tree's column capacity (four plates fill the room at this plate height, three Stone columns hold four) is not this line's; a victory's failing condition; save compatibility, none needed before the Bronze Age.
- Reconcile: the victory count becomes one helper the three go through (become one); the technology's age-unlock declaration becomes the age's victory declaration (become one, in the projected form); the Nomadic's goal "Build [card:shelter]" and the Stone's "Pass the capstone" stay apart, meant; the link rule stays apart for victory technologies, meant.
- Corner cases decided: the Stone victory's reward reads the influence alone; the five pinned achievements a first Stone chronicle shows are the standing pin rule and change nothing; a chronicle of the first age reads nothing of Metalworking, since it is the Stone Age's.

**Traps:**

- Measured on the real screen (2026-10-15): the room is 672 tall, a plate 128 (five lines of text under the name), the row gap 22, plates 306 apart column to column, the lead 200; five plates in one column take 728 and overflow the room by 28 at each edge. That is why the victory stands in a column of its own, never in the first.
- The tree's door lookup (`src/ui/tree-layout.ts`) finds a border by the age a technology unlocks and throws "no technology unlocks the age" otherwise; with the declaration moved it reads each age's victory, keyed by the age the victory belongs to, not the one it opens.
- `src/rules/campaign.ts` reads the ages and the regions a technology unlocks through one helper over `unlocks[noun]`; the region half stays, the age half becomes the derived reading.
- `e2e/chronicle-screen.ts`'s plate reward helper, `e2e/tree.spec.ts`'s next age and `e2e/neutral.spec.ts`'s unlocker lookup all read the age-unlock declaration and must read the derived one, or the specs break at import.
- `src/content/catalogue.test.ts` already asks every achievement its goal text and every technology its name: the two text entries are proven there, nothing to add.
- `tools/` (the forge's `--list`, `learned-with-needs`) read the achievements' technologies, not the unlocks: untouched.
- The glossary lint reads `src/ui/text.ts`: "Pass the capstone" holds glossary words only; "Metalworking" collides with no row.
- A colour is named for its meaning: the wash's far colour is one new entry meaning the ground of the age after the last the game holds, never a value name and never the page colour reused.
- The comment hook flags a comment block over three lines under `src/` or `e2e/`; comments are for traps only.
- `docs/PHASER.md` for anything the tree's drawing raises; the wash is a Graphics gradient fill already in `src/ui/tree.ts`.
