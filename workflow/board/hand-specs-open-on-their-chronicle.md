# The hand's specs open on their chronicle

**Line:** The hand's specs open on their chronicle — press, recall and refuse, reviewed the same way: each test opened on the state it needs, built headlessly on the Nomadic content, its oracle read from the rules; the recall spec deleted, no age's content aiming a card at the discard pile. Done when `e2e/press.spec.ts` and `e2e/refuse.spec.ts` import nothing from `src/content/stand-in.ts` and open through the shared save opener on the Nomadic content; `e2e/recall.spec.ts` is gone; the report carries both specs' duration before and after; CI is green.

**Spec:** `DOGMAS.md` → _Testing_, the rule **A spec opens on the save it wrote, on the game's content**, read whole, and the rule **A spec rests before it presses or measures what just changed**. The sentences the tests assert are `docs/CHRONICLE-SCREEN.md` → _The hand and the aim_, _The piles_ and _The right click, the back key and the menu_, unchanged. No `docs/` sentence is added or changed. Player-facing sentences: none.

**Doc-impact:** none — the line changes the e2e suite alone; the rule it executes stands in `DOGMAS.md`, and no `docs/` page describes the suite.

**Scope:**

A later age's card aimed at the discard pile brings the spec back with its content.
- **Nothing in press or refuse is a stand-in remnant**: all twenty-five tests read against a sentence of the chronicle screen page, and every test keeps its gestures and its assertions. What changes is the opening — a save planted, the bare address, the capstone's window closed — and every number, which is read from the rules on the fixture.

Every seed's first hand holds a unit card the rules refuse for its cost and for the population and idle blocks at once; gather is the card aimed at a tile the city can pay for, and it admits no tile while no worker stands.   - _Worker on the city, turn 1_: the bare settle with the first worker played on the city's tile. From there the worker steps onto every neighbour in the heartland: plain for one move point, forest and hills for both.
  - _Worker stepped, turn 1_: the one above, the worker moved through the move command onto the neighbour the search chooses, by what the card under test admits after the step: gather admits any neighbour, every one lying outside the border, since the Nomadic city holds its own tile alone; march admits the worker once its move points are spent, on any neighbour; trapping admits a forest neighbour, and the city cannot pay its production on turn 1. Measured: seed 3's hand holds march and trapping with a forest at `1,0`; seeds 5 and 6 hold march too.
  - _Playable at nothing_: the first seed and turn, inside forty turns, whose hand holds a unit card the city can pay for and nothing blocks, no unit ever entered. **Traps:**

- The boot reads the save only on a bare address (`src/main.ts`): the opener goes to `/`, never to an address naming content or a seed. The save is planted through `addInitScript` before the navigation; the entry key is `SAVE_ENTRY` in `src/ui/save-entry.ts`. The shared module already holds the opener, `openSaved`, and the Nomadic fixture builder, `settledOn`; the seed search, `firstSeed`, and the headless turn, `endedTurn`, take the catalogue from the chronicle.
- The save names its deck by id, which the reader checks against the catalogue's decks, while the piles come from the chronicle: the doubled-deck fixture saves under the Nomadic deck's id and reads back with its twenty-card draw pile. `writeSave` validates by reading back: a fixture the reading refuses throws at the plant, which is the finding.
- The launch takes the deck as a value (`launched` in `src/rules/chronicle.ts`), not an id; `settledOn` takes the catalogue's first deck, so the doubled deck goes in beside it or through it, the implementer's call.
- Every command a fixture here takes — play, move, end-turn, take — goes through `apply`, which charts the snapshots on every stage; no fixture calls a rules helper outside `apply`, so no `charted` pass is needed. Should one come to, `charted` in `src/rules/sight.ts` is the pass, as the attack spec runs it.
- The chronicle screen raises the capstone's window on its first render, a resumed chronicle included (`src/ui/overlay.ts`); its card closes it, and the back key does not, being rebindable. Seed 1 at turn 5 is before its first deal and before the capstone's window of turns, so the fixture opens under the opening's window alone.
- The hand selects a card by its aim and never by its refusal (`select` in `src/ui/hand.ts`): a card aimed at a tile or at a unit is being aimed from the click, unaffordable or not, and the aim's catcher, point and line stand. The play on the selection is what the refusal turns down.
- The browse lays six cards to a row and its frame holds two rows at the design size (`layGrid` in `src/ui/overlay.ts`); twelve cards leave no overflow and thirteen do. The doubled deck's twenty are well past it; the spec reads the overflow off the grid and states no row count.
- `throughWorker` refuses `no-worker` before the tile's own reasons (`src/rules/cards.ts`): the city's own tile with no unit standing answers that block for gather, trapping and shelter alike, and every neighbour a worker stands on answers for the tile.
- The unit card's blocks come in the order `enters` declares them (`src/rules/cards.ts`): population, then idle, then the city's tile occupied; the note reads the unaffordable costs first, then those blocks, as `reasons` builds them now.
- The map emits `attack`, not `move`, for a drag onto a tile a unit of another faction stands on (`src/ui/map.ts`); the worker's drag here lands on an empty neighbour and is a move.
- CI runs the suite split by spec across four runners: press is the slowest spec in the suite as it stands and sets the slowest shard on its own.
- A spec's duration is measured with nothing else running on the machine: a run beside the user's play or another suite reads contention, not the spec. Measured today, one spec at a time: press 3.9 min across twenty tests of 7 to 18 s each, recall 58 s, refuse 34 s.
- The named-spec hook refuses a Playwright run that names no spec. `docs/PHASER.md` → _Under a Playwright spec_ says why a spec rests after a load, a scene start and a raise, and why a press in the frame an object appears falls through it.
