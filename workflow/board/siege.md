# The siege

**Line:** The siege — the temperate schedule names the siege beside the long winter and the monument: its landing enters a raid through the raid's door, warriors and archers led by a leader, a kind of its own that carries a bonus to the units of its faction beside it, the raid going for the player's city whatever city lies nearer, and the leader killed is victory on the blow, whoever strikes it; the catalogue's coherence test passes and the age page says so. Doc-impact: `docs/ages/STONE.md`, `docs/CHRONICLE.md`, `docs/GLOSSARY.md`.

**Spec:** `docs/CHRONICLE.md` _Units and combat_ and _Enemies and camps_, `docs/GLOSSARY.md`, `docs/ages/STONE.md` _The units_ and _The capstones_. The sentences to land, written out:

- `docs/GLOSSARY.md`, a row after **damage**: `| **bonus** | What a unit adds to the damage of the units of its faction standing beside it. | buff, boost, aura, rally, lead |`. The **feature** row's forbidden list drops `bonus` and reads `—`.
- `docs/CHRONICLE.md` _Units and combat_, the lead paragraph, after "lost health stays lost until a **heal** brings it back up, never above full": A unit's kind may carry a **bonus**, damage it adds to every unit of its faction standing beside it, read wherever a unit's damage is read, at the blow and on its card, and a unit beside several carries every one of them; a unit carries no bonus of its own.
- `docs/CHRONICLE.md` _Enemies and camps_, the paragraph "What an entry enters is content", its sentence "A raid draws its enemies' rows before its door, and its door is read on its first enemy." becomes: What enters a raid draws its enemies' rows before the door, and may put rows of its own in front of the drawn ones; the door is read on the first enemy, whichever way it came to be first.
- `docs/ages/STONE.md` _The units_, a bullet after the archer's: **The leader** holds more health and more damage than the warrior and carries a bonus: the units of its band beside it hit harder. No card enters it; it comes with the siege.
- `docs/ages/STONE.md` _The capstones_, a paragraph after the monument's: **The siege.** When it lands a raid enters through the raid's door, warriors and archers led by a **leader**, every one of them going for the player's city whatever city lies nearer and preparing the capture on its tile alone, attacking what they can on the way. The chronicle ends in victory the moment the leader is killed, whoever kills it, in the middle of the blow; the capstone names no second script, no span and nothing that fails it: the band at the city is the clock, and the schedule keeps dealing. So the siege is broken by reaching the leader through the band around it, which strikes the harder for standing beside it: the trial is whether the city has units enough, and the military to pay for them.

The player-facing entries, written out:

- The capstone: name `The siege`; rules `A [enemy:leader] enters as a raid with {band} [enemy:warrior] or [enemy:archer]`, `{band}` the number the capstone reads.
- The unit kind: `Leader`. Its card reads a row `Bonus` after `Damage`, its value `+1`; the label entry `Bonus`, its tooltip `The damage this unit adds to its faction's units beside it`. A unit standing beside a leader of its faction reads its damage row as its own number and the bonus, `2 + 1`, the way health reads `3 / 5`; a unit beside none reads its own number alone.
- The lore at the opening: `The elders say a great band is gathering beyond the hills under one leader. When it comes, kill the leader, and the Stone Age is won.` At the landing: `The band has come, a leader at its head, and those beside the leader strike the harder for it. Kill the leader, and the Stone Age is won.`
- The victory line: `The leader fell and the raid broke. Stone Age is over.`

The numbers, all provisional, the balance pass's: the leader 8 health, 3 damage, range 1, move 2, action 1, sight 2, bonus 1; the band 5 enemies drawn from warriors at a weight of 2 and archers at 1, the leader in front; every one of the raid, the leader included, carrying the siege's raider script.

**Scope:**

- In: the capstone `siege` on the temperate schedule beside `long-winter` and `monument`, drawn each alike; the unit kind the Stone Age brings, with its bonus, its name, its mark and its card's row; the bonus rule, read at every blow and on the card; the siege's raider script; the raid door taking rows, the drawing a helper of its own; the texts and the lore; the proof spec.
- Out: the archipelago's schedule, which keeps its stand-in until its own line; the fixture's siege, its placed camps and its span, which the stand-ins' doors rung reassesses; any number's tuning; a bonus to any stat but damage, or over any reach but beside; a bonus of the player's own.
- The pass is "the capstone has landed and no unit of the leader's kind stands on the map", read as the winter's is: after the landing group as a whole, then after every change. The leader killed by the neutral's units, by the player's, by anything, passes it on that change, mid enemy phase or mid-play; the capture of the player's city before then is the standing defeat, the leader preparing it as any raider does.
- The raid's door is the standing one: a camp still standing, likelier, or the outer ring, read on the leader as the first enemy; a door with no free tile for the leader is never drawn, so the leader enters wherever any raid enters.
- The siege's raider goes for the player's city alone: nearest by its own walk to that tile, on it it prepares the capture, off it it attacks the weakest it can and walks on; standing on the neutral's city's tile on its way it prepares nothing there. It crosses the water as the camp's raider does. Two decisions apart from the raider, each with its test beside the script.
- The bonus is the kind's, read off the catalogue through the unit's type as full health is, never carried on the unit: the save has no field for it, and a unit's own damage stays its own. A unit beside two leaders of its faction carries both; a leader beside another leader carries the other's. A unit of another faction beside a leader carries nothing. An embarked unit attacks nothing, so its bonus is read nowhere, and an embarked leader gives as any leader does.
- The reconcile: the raid door takes the rows and nothing else, the drawing out of a table by weights its own helper that the event answers and the camp's placing call before it, the answers unchanged in play; the raider becomes a factory as the guard is, taking which city it goes for, the camps' raider and the siege's made from it, with the neutral's units of no city going for the player's city alone through their faction as they do today; the damage read becomes one helper the blow and the card both go through; the pass goes through the winter's door; the kind through the archer's precedent; the face's number through the capstone's reading; the spec's walk through the shared helper.

**Traps:**

- The pass is read on the chronicle the landing group leaves and after every change after it (`conditionsRead` in `src/rules/chronicle.ts`), never inside the landing: "no leader stands" holding before the raid enters is not read. A landing that enters no leader would pass on the spot; the outer ring is always a door, so none does, and a `runtime-error` there is the only other outcome.
- The coherence test (`src/content/catalogue.test.ts`) calls every capstone's `lands` on a chronicle settled in each age and asks `passes` to be false there: the siege's landing must enter on a settled chronicle without throwing, and the pass must read `capstoneLanded` first, as the winter's does.
- Every blow, the player's and a script's, pays damage in one function (`blow` in `src/rules/chronicle.ts`) off `attacker.stats.damage`; the unit card (`readingOf` in `src/ui/infopanel.ts`) reads the same field. Both must read the one helper, or the card lies about the blow. `fullHealth` in `src/rules/catalogue.ts` is the precedent for a number read off the kind through the unit's type.
- `UnitStats` is copied onto the unit as it enters (`entered` in `src/rules/catalogue.ts`) and the save reads it field by field (`statsOf` in `src/rules/save.ts`): a bonus on the stats record would be written into every save and read back; it goes on the kind's declaration, beside the stats or as a table of its own, and the implementer says which and why.
- `raided` in `src/rules/enemies.ts` draws through `rowsDrawn`, reads the door through `raidEntry` on `drawn.rows[0].kind` and enters through `enteredAs`; `enteredAround` draws the same way for a camp's placing. The cut moves the drawing out to the callers: `src/content/nomadic.ts`'s answers, `src/content/stone.ts`'s grievance through `campedOrRaided` in `src/content/scripts.ts`, and the fixture's raid in `src/rules/fixtures.ts`.
- `RAIDER` in `src/content/scripts.ts` goes for the nearest city not its own through `otherCityOn` in both `moveTo` and `acts`; `guarding(radius)` is the factory precedent. The pillager, the guard, the neutral's guard and wave all fall back on `RAIDER`, and keep its nearest-city reading.
- The script ids are code identifiers the glossary lint reads: `besiege` is forbidden under **prepare**, so the siege's raider is named without it, `siege-raider` or the like.
- A text entry's `[enemy:…]` resolves to a unit kind, and the coherence test reads it; a unit kind needs its mark in `src/ui/marks.ts`, a flat polygon after the warrior's and the archer's, and its name entry in `src/ui/text.ts`; the unit card's rows are the closed list `STATS` in `src/ui/infopanel.ts`, each with a `label.` and a `tooltip.` entry.
- The temperate schedule's seeds now split three ways: `e2e/long-winter.spec.ts` and `e2e/monument.spec.ts` filter on the capstone id through `beforeTheLanding`, and the siege's spec filters the same way; `e2e/boot.spec.ts` reads whichever lands.
- For `e2e/`: `docs/PHASER.md`, a spec rests before it presses or measures; a chronicle built for a spec is built through the rules' helpers, a unit entered through `unitEntered`, health taken through `unitDamaged`, the enemies cleared through `cleared`, never written.
