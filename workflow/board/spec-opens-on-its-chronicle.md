**Line:** A spec opens on the chronicle it wrote — the planting helper moves into the shared module and takes any deck, the seed searches answer the chronicle they found, and the specs whose whole setup is turns open on it, on the game's content, each reviewed on the way, opened on the state it needs, shortened, and deleted where it tests a remnant of the stand-in or a code path that no longer exists: fall, attack, victory, deal, move, end-of-turn, play-out. Done when `e2e/fall.spec.ts`, `attack`, `victory`, `deal`, `move`, `end-of-turn` and `play-out` import nothing from `src/content/stand-in.ts` and open through the shared save opener on the Nomadic content; the victory spec's span test and the move spec's end turn are gone; `e2e/resume.spec.ts` plants through the shared helper; the report carries each of the seven specs' duration before and after; CI is green.

**Spec:** `DOGMAS.md` → _Testing_, the rule **A spec opens on the save it wrote, on the game's content**, read whole, and the rule **A spec rests before it presses or measures what just changed**. No `docs/` sentence is added or changed. Player-facing sentences: none.

**Doc-impact:** none — the line changes the e2e suite alone; the rule it executes already stands in `DOGMAS.md`, and no `docs/` page describes the suite.

**Scope:**

Out: every other spec, the stand-in catalogue, and the stand-in's own helpers in the shared module — `open`, `settle`, `launch`, `dealRun`, `workerRun`, `stepRun`, `fallRun`, `atTileRun`, `unaffordableRun` — which keep their remaining callers and die with the last of them, in a later line. No stand-in search is rewritten here: the seven specs stop calling them.
- The shared module holds two families until the last line: the stand-in's, which settles through the screen on the address, and the new one, which builds a chronicle headlessly on the Nomadic content and plants its save. The new builders take the catalogue they run on; the planting helper takes the save's content, region and deck, so `resume.spec.ts` plants on the stand-in through it and the seven specs on the Nomadic.
- **The opener on a save**: names read, the save planted, the bare address opened, the `ui` scene active, a rest, the capstone's window closed by a press on its card, the window gone, a rest. Every resumed chronicle opens under the capstone's window, an ended one under its ending screen; none of the seven is ended at its opening.
- **The Nomadic fixture**: the chronicle launched on the Nomadic catalogue, its first region, schedule and deck; the settle card played on the centre tile, which is plain on every seed; the other settle cards played on the city's tile where the spec wants them and left to go with the phase where it does not; the settle phase ended. The headless turn takes a deal's first entry, as the window's press does; a search that wants another answer takes it itself.

**Traps:**

- The boot reads the save only on a bare address (`src/main.ts`): the opener goes to `/`, never to an address naming content or a seed. The save is planted through `addInitScript` before the navigation, as `readNames` is; the entry key is `SAVE_ENTRY` in `src/ui/save-entry.ts`.
- The chronicle screen raises the capstone's window on its first render, a resumed chronicle included (`src/ui/overlay.ts`); its card closes it, and the back key does not, being rebindable.
- A rules helper called outside `apply` charts nothing: the snapshots stay as the last command left them. After its last helper the fixture is passed through `charted` in `src/rules/sight.ts`, which `apply` runs on every stage, so the save's snapshots agree with what stands. The map draws a tile in sight live, so the screen is right either way; the save is not.
- The map emits `attack`, not `move`, for a drag onto a target tile (`src/ui/map.ts`); a headless oracle for the attack applies the `attack` command.
- The entering helper (`entered` in `src/rules/catalogue.ts`) takes a unit kind, a faction, a tile, and a script id for an enemy; it takes no population, unlike the `worker` card's `enters`, which is blocked while the population is one. The heartland around the centre is plain, forest and hills, all of which the camp's unit stands on.
- `writeSave` validates by reading back: a fixture the reading refuses throws at the plant, which is the finding, not a silent skip. The screen rewrites the save after every command; a planted save overwritten is harmless.
- The city's fall is read in the enemy phase, before the next turn's events phase, so the capturing end of turn stops on no deal: `stoppedTurn` sees the ending.
- The claim command asks culture of twice the tiles held, two on the first claim; on every seed measured the city holds far more by the landing. The shelter's aim needs the tile inside the border, its slot free, a worker with action on it and a terrain it stands on: a claimed neighbour in the heartland meets all four.
- CI runs the suite split by spec across four runners: the slowest spec sets the slowest shard.
- A spec's duration is measured with nothing else running on the machine: a run beside the user's play or another suite reads contention, not the spec.
- The named-spec hook refuses a Playwright run that names no spec. `docs/PHASER.md` → _Under a Playwright spec_ says why a spec rests after a load, a scene start and a raise.
