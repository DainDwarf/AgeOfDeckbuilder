# The neutral's fall

**Line:** **The neutral's fall** — a unit of the player's standing on the neutral's city through the enemy phase captures it: the village leaves the map with the neutral's population, stocks and border, its tiles claimable like any other, its units hostile still and raiding, and the capture deals the neutral's rewards in the deal window, each reward a card in several copies. Doc-impact: `docs/CHRONICLE.md`, `docs/CHRONICLE-SCREEN.md`, `docs/GLOSSARY.md`, `docs/ages/STONE.md`.

**Scope:**

- In: a reward row naming a card and its copies, the camps' and sites' rows naming one, a reward of several copies drawn in the deal window as the browse draws a stack, the take adding every copy to the discard pile; the neutral's city captured by the player's units alone, the neutral's city row leaving the chronicle while the relations stay hostile; the Stone Age neutral's three rewards — three copies each of Capture (the Nomadic camp's card, reused as the Stone camp already reuses it), Their land and Their stores — their texts and the capture lore; the coherence test widened to the neutral's rewards and lore; one spec.
- Out: an enemy capturing the neutral's city (stays an idea), the reward card that pays influence at the ending (stays an idea), any change to the neutral's unit scripts, the neutral occupying or being occupied at income.
- Corner cases decided: the fall always finds the neutral hostile, since the only landing on its tile declares war; its surviving units keep acting by their scripts' hostile branches, so guard and wave alike raid the player's city; the neutral enters, claims and grows no more. Tiles in fog keep the neutral's ring until seen again, by the fog rule, and a tile of its a neutral unit stands on is not claimable while it stands there, by the standing claim rule. A chronicle that falls in the same enemy phase captures nothing, as today. Their land lights only the tiles the player's city may claim, so it never lands on a tile the neutral holds and declares no war; with no claimable tile it is a blank draw, refused over a pressed tile with the standing entry. A reward row naming copies below one or a card the catalogue does not hold is a content defect refused when the catalogue is built.
- Reconcile: the fall goes through the standing capture door, parameterised by who may capture the building (the player alone for the neutral's city) and what more leaves with it; Their people is the standing card Capture, three copies, no twin; a reward row declares copies as a technology's unlock list does; Their land walks the door the fixture's claim stand-in already walks, which stays as the fixture it is; a reward of several copies is drawn with the browse's stack and badge, a reward of one as today.

**Traps:**

- The neutral's relations live on its city's row today, and the one reader of them answers peace on a chronicle holding no neutral. After the fall the row's city, border, population and stocks are gone but the relations must still read hostile: the walk's passage, the attack rule, the occupation at income, the infopanel's Hostile line, the declare-war warning and the waves all read them through that one reader. Wherever the relations come to live, every step that today asks "is there a neutral" to mean "does its city stand" must still mean that.
- The capture step walks every building the catalogue names rewards for and credits whoever stands on it. A camp or a site falls to the player's or the neutral's units; the neutral's city must fall to the player's alone, or its own guard resting on its tile, which the guard script does while no other held tile is free, would capture it.
- The deal a chronicle waits on is saved and read back; if a standing deal comes to carry copies, the save's reading of a deal changes with it. Saves need no compatibility care at this stage.
- The scripts' hostile branches run first and need no city, but the neutral guard's peace branch stands still when no neutral row is there; the fall never reaches it while the relations hold hostile.
- The pillage spares the neutral's building by reading the age's content, and the terraform of a city's tile reads the neutral's city off the chronicle; both are sane once the city is gone.
- The deal window and the browse are both Phaser; the stack and the badge the browse draws are module-private to it. `docs/PHASER.md` holds what the window must wait out before it measures or takes a press.
- In `e2e/`, the standing neutral spec builds its chronicles on seed 1's turn 1, where the neutral has no units yet and no stock to enter one; a warrior of the player's standing on its tile with the war declared through the rules' own helper, then End turn pressed on screen, is the shape of the proof.
