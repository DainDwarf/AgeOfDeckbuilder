# City mode's unassigned tiles

**Line:** City mode's unassigned tiles — in city mode a held tile nobody is assigned to wears the assigned mark's disc empty and a dark of its own, as dark as the fog's and cooler; the yield glyphs of an assigned tile a unit occupies stand in grey and every other glyph in its colour, nothing drawn paler; `e2e/city-mode.spec.ts` proves it.

**Spec:** the mockup the picks were made on is https://claude.ai/artifact/Qr7JUvm3QbTaF9X6CjYyF1, the real city mode shot in layers; its picks are `Dark on a held tile nobody is assigned to: 70%, cool`, `Sign: the mark's disc, empty`, `Glyphs of a tile not giving: grey ink, full strength`, `Not giving is: only an assigned tile an enemy occupies`. No player-facing text entry is added or changed. The pages' sentences, written out:

**Doc-impact:** `docs/MAP-SCREEN.md`, `docs/LOOK.md`.

**Scope:** In: the colour of city mode's dark; the empty disc; the grey glyphs and the rule that says which glyphs wear it; the pale strength gone. Out: the yield's patch, the faded units, the assigned mark's own look and place, the claim marks, anything out of city mode, the neutral's tiles. Decided here:

- The dark's tint is `0x344d70` where the fog's is `0x4d4d4d`: the same green, less red, more blue. It stays one entry of the look, the one that holds it today, and goes through the dark as it stands; only its value moves.
- The empty disc is the assigned mark's own disc with its glyph left out, the same size, place and layer: one piece for the two, so an assign fills the disc where it stands. It is not the row's disc, which is wider and stands above the middle.
- Every held tile nobody is assigned to wears it: the city's own tile, a tile a unit of any faction stands on, a held tile in fog, and whether or not an idle population is left.
- The empty disc takes no press of its own: a click on its tile is the city's act as today, and a drag from a tile nobody is assigned to takes hold of nothing, as today.
- A glyph in grey is its own colour drained: each of its three channels set to `0.3 × red + 0.59 × green + 0.11 × blue` of the resource's colour, at full strength, its dark outline unchanged. It is derived from the glyph's colour where it is drawn and is no entry of the look; it stays apart from the flat grey of health lost and of a threshold not yet met, which would vanish on the patch.
- Grey is worn by a tile one population is assigned to that gives nothing at income, and by no other: a held tile nobody is assigned to, occupied or not, and every tile outside the border show their glyphs in colour. No glyph is drawn at less than its full strength, and the look's strength for a yield not given goes with its last use.
- The grey is city mode's alone: out of it the overlay shows an occupied tile in colour, as `docs/MAP-SCREEN.md` _The yield overlay_ already says of whatever occupies a tile.
- While a population is dragged, its mark is carried whole as today and the tile it left wears the empty disc; let go off a held tile, the mark comes home onto it.
- Unchanged and not to be touched: a held tile in fog wears the fog's dark alone; the dark is in the paintings and darkens no mark and no unit; the hex grid is left as it is by city mode's dark.

**Traps:**

- A Shape carries no tint (`docs/PHASER.md`): the mark's disc is an Arc painted through `asRowDisc`, the row's discs take a darkened fill and stroke through `darkened`, and the new tint is no longer a grey, so a shape darkened by it must come out as the paintings beside it do, channel by channel.
- A game object is made once and changed in place (`DOGMAS.md` _Stack_): the marks are pooled on the `assigned-marks` layer, which `e2e/map-order.spec.ts` reads for the map's order.
- The specs count the objects named `assigned` as the marks of the assigned tiles, on fourteen sites of `e2e/city-mode.spec.ts`, and `e2e/map.spec.ts` reads that name's place and waits on it: an empty disc under that name breaks every count.
- `e2e/chronicle-screen.ts` splits the glyphs by strength in `glyphsByStrength`, against the look's strength for a yield not given, and `e2e/city-mode.spec.ts` asserts today's rule by it in two tests, "…pales the glyphs of every tile not giving its yield at income…" and "…its glyphs stand paler": both titles and both oracles change with the rule, the oracle read from the rules as today (`givingYield`, `assignedTo`), and the reading becomes the glyph's colour, not its strength.
- `e2e/city-mode.spec.ts` reads the dark as a face's tint equal to the look's entry: it follows the value by itself.
- `src/ui/map-grab.ts` takes hold of the mark `assignedMarkOn` answers for an assigned tile and moves it with the pointer; the tile it left has no mark of its own while it is carried.
- The rules already answer which tile gives its yield (`givingYield`, `src/rules/city.ts`); the grey's rule is that answer beside the assignment, and no new rules function is owed.

**Plan:** `src/ui/look.ts`, the dark's value and the strength that goes; `src/ui/map-marks.ts` and `src/ui/map.ts`, the empty disc on every held tile nobody is assigned to, the drag leaving one, and the glyphs painted in colour or in grey; `src/ui/glyphs.ts` where the grey is derived, if that is where it reads best; then `e2e/chronicle-screen.ts` and `e2e/city-mode.spec.ts`, the readings and the two tests moved to the new rule, with one assertion that every held tile nobody is assigned to wears an empty disc, a held tile an assign has just emptied among them; then the two pages. The order: the look's value first, which leaves the dark cooler and every spec green; the glyphs' rule next, which leaves nothing paler; the empty disc last.
