# Events after the draw

**Line:** Events after the draw — the turn opens on its hand: the opening ticks, draws, runs the capstone's second script and then the events phase, so the deal and the capstone's landing stand over a drawn hand and the take of an answer resolves nothing after its landing; the rules tests state that order, `docs/CHRONICLE.md` and `docs/CHRONICLE-SCREEN.md` say so, and `e2e/strangers.spec.ts` is green.

**Spec:** `docs/CHRONICLE.md`, _The turn_ and _Events and the capstone_; `docs/CHRONICLE-SCREEN.md`, _The windows_. The sentences, written out:

- _The settle phase_, last sentence: "Turn 1 follows, its draw and its events phase as on any turn."
- _The turn_, items 1 and 2 become, in this order:
  - "1. **Draw.** The player draws a **hand** of **five** cards from the draw pile. 🔧 Buildings, a civilization or an effect may change the number. An empty draw pile is refilled by shuffling the discard pile into it."
  - "2. **Events.** On a due turn the schedule deals one of its events and that event deals its answers as cards; the player chooses one answer, which lands and resolves at once, on what the player's city has; play waits on the choice. On the capstone's turn the capstone lands instead, and nothing is dealt. Events land after the draw: an event turns on the player's city's stocks and on what stands on the map, which the hand does not change, so the turn opens on its hand every time and a card an answer adds to a pile waits there for its cycle. Events ahead of the draw, so the hand answered the map the event left, was rejected: the hand answers no event, and the draw after the answer shuffled what the answer had just added to the discard pile straight into the draw pile."
- _The schedule_, the deal's last sentence: "The turn stops on the deal, and the chronicle takes no command but the choice until every deal standing is chosen from — a capture's rewards, dealt before the turn ticks, then the turn's event, dealt onto the hand the turn drew."
- _The capstone_, first paragraph: "a second script it may carry, run on every turn after the one it lands on, after the draw and before whatever that turn deals" and "on its turn the events phase lands it alone, whatever else was due, its landing runs straight on the hand drawn as on any turn, and the next deal is due from its landing as from any".
- _The capstone_, the condition's paragraph: "and one already holding when the capstone lands ends it on the landing — the turn's tick, the draw and the timeline's roll on the capstone's turn read nothing, the landing is the first read".
- `CHRONICLE-SCREEN.md`, _The windows_, the deal's window: "the window closes on it with the landing playing out after — the next deal's window rising where one still waits, and where none does, the rest of the end of turn after a reward, the next turn's draw among it, and nothing after an answer".
- `CHRONICLE-SCREEN.md`, _The windows_, the capstone's window: "and when the capstone lands, where the end of turn's play-out ends on it over the hand that turn drew, and its landing plays out once it is closed".

No player-facing entry changes: the answer and capstone texts that say "top of the draw pile" stay true, and the age pages stay as they are.

**Doc-impact:** `docs/CHRONICLE.md`, `docs/CHRONICLE-SCREEN.md`.

**Traps:**

- The deal window rises when the play-out ends, not on the `dealt` stage: `src/ui/overlay.ts`'s play answers a resolved promise while a deal stands and the window is shown by the render the play-out ends on. With the draw ahead of the deal, the hand's draw animates and then the window rises over it; no UI code is expected to change, and the one proof spec says whether that holds.
- The timeline's own generator is stepped by the deal and the chronicle's by the shuffle and by an answer's landing. The shuffle now steps the chronicle's generator before an answer lands, so every seed search in `e2e/` lands on other seeds and the specs read their oracles from the rules, by design; a search that finds no seed is a finding, not a reason to loosen it.
- `e2e/chronicle-screen.ts`'s `landed()` ends turns up to the capstone's and expects the Shelter in hand there; after the change the Shelter is in the hand of the turn after the landing, so it ends one turn more, still with the ending unset and no deal standing. `e2e/capstone.spec.ts` asserts the hand empty under the landing window; it is full there now.
- The rules tests that state the old order, to restate and never delete: `src/rules/schedule.test.ts` — a deal due on turn 1 stopping the settle phase's end with no hand (line 250), the famine's hazard in the hand (301 and 310), the due turn dealing with no draw (322), the unmet need's turn drawing (358), the take drawing the hand (473), the capstone's turn landing and drawing (1458), the capture before the capstone's turn (1554), the condition holding before the landing (1861), and every staged list naming `drawn` after `answer` or `capstone-landing`; `src/rules/enemies.test.ts` at 218 and 1179; `src/rules/chronicle.test.ts` 623 holds as it is unless a deal is due on turn 1. The famine's hazard test becomes: taken on a due turn, Hunger is on the draw pile's top and in the next turn's hand.
- The settle phase's end on a timeline due on turn 1: the opening banishes the settle cards, draws the hand, then deals; the take then draws nothing.
- A capture's reward stands before the turn ticks, on an empty hand; a turn's event stands on a full one. Both are deals, and the take's resume differs only in what it goes on to: the opening after a reward, nothing after an answer.
- `docs/PHASER.md` for anything the specs ask of the screen; the dossier restates none of it.
