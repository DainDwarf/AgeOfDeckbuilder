# Tooltips follow a scrolled window

**Line:** Tooltips follow a scrolled window — a tooltip or a small card raised from a card on a window's grid moves with the thing that raised it when the grid scrolls, and goes down as any hover does once the scroll takes that thing out from under a pointer that holds still; `e2e/browse.spec.ts` proves it on a browse scrolled by the wheel.

**Spec:** `docs/INTERFACE.md` → _What stands over what_: "The pointer is on the one thing that stands topmost under it, across every surface, at every moment: a thing rising, falling or coming live under a pointer that holds still is hovered or left as one the pointer moved onto or off." And → _The presses_, the name paragraph (a small card stays up while the pointer is on it or on the name that raised it, and goes down once the pointer is on none of them) and the kind-label paragraph (the pointer leaving the label takes the bubble down). The code breaks the first sentence on a window's grid: which name or kind label the pointer is on is read only when the pointer moves, so the wheel or a fling moving the cards under a still pointer leaves the small card and the bubble standing where they were raised, and still counted as hovered. The infopanel's rows already behave as the spec says: the map camera carries their bubble, and the per-frame hover read takes it down once a pan moves the row off the pointer. No sentence is added or changed, and no player-facing text.

**Doc-impact:** none — the behaviour is already the spec; the code catches up with it.

**Scope:**

- In: every window that lays out a grid — the browse, the aim window, the deal window, the capstone's window — for both things a grid's card raises: the small card a name raises, and the overlay's kind bubble a label raises.
- **Carried.** Whatever a grid's card raised moves with that card as the grid scrolls, frame by frame, the wheel's notches and a fling's travel alike. A small card keeps the side of its name it rose on, above or below, for as long as it stands. It is not re-placed or flipped partway through a scroll, and it may leave the frame or stand over the title while it follows.
- **Left like any hover.** As the grid moves, it is read again what the pointer is on, with no pointer move needed. A label scrolled out from under the pointer takes its bubble down at once. A name scrolled out from under it takes its small card down after the handover, unless the pointer is on the small card. A name or label the scroll brings under a still pointer is hovered as if the pointer had moved onto it: its rest, or the handover straight onto it, applies.
- **Down the chain.** A small card raised from a name on a small card, and the bubble raised from a small card's own kind label, follow as well. They are carried because what raised them is carried.
- **A drag is unchanged.** Starting a drag of the grid takes the small card and the bubble down, as it does now. Neither is raised while the grid is being dragged. The fling that follows the release is a scroll like the wheel's and falls under the two rules above.
- Out: the hand, the resource bar, a card shown large and the infopanel, which do not scroll or already follow.

**Traps:**

- The grid scrolls by moving its container (`scrollTo` in `src/ui/overlay.ts`). The kind bubble (`src/ui/tooltip.ts`, `beside`) reads its anchor once, when it is painted. The small card (`src/ui/small-card.ts`, `raise`) is placed once, from its raiser's `where()`. Neither is on the grid's container: they stand on their own strata of the overlay scene, over the grid and unmasked. Nothing about them moves unless something tells them the grid moved.
- The grid's names and labels are not interactive objects. `followPointer` (`src/ui/design-space.ts`) re-reads only objects every frame. The grid's name and label under the pointer come from the frame zone's `pointermove` handler, which Phaser fires only on a pointer move (`docs/PHASER.md` → _The pointer's readings_, first entry). A re-read under a still pointer has to be the game's own. It runs only while the frame zone is what the pointer is on: a small card, the title or anything else standing over the frame hides the grid's names beneath it, as `topOnly` hides them from the `pointermove` today.
- The rest timers in `tooltip.ts` and `small-card.ts` restart only on a pointer move beyond `JITTER`. A name arriving under a still pointer starts a fresh rest through the path it already takes when hovered. It needs no timer of its own.
- The wheel is heard scene-wide (`scene.input.on('wheel')` in `overlay.ts`), so it scrolls the grid while the pointer is on a small card too. The small card then follows its name away from the pointer, and `followPointer` already reads the pointer off it.
- A position is read after a drawn frame (`docs/PHASER.md` → _Under a Playwright spec_): the spec rests after each wheel before it measures.

**Plan:**

1. `src/ui/overlay.ts` and the two raisers it drives, `src/ui/small-card.ts` and `src/ui/tooltip.ts`. First, whatever stands raised off the grid is re-placed from its raiser each time the grid moves, the chain and the bubble included; the small card keeps the offset from its name it was raised at. Second, a grid move under a still pointer re-reads the name and label under the pointer, as the frame's `pointermove` does, and feeds the result through the same paths. This leaves the behaviour in Scope standing on all four windows. How the move is signalled and where the re-placing lives is the implementer's call.
2. `e2e/browse.spec.ts`, one new test, on the file's own `PH_TallDeck` browse, whose cards carry names. With the pointer at rest on a name until its small card stands, a wheel small enough to keep the name under the pointer leaves the small card standing and moved with the name: its offset from the name is the same before and after. A wheel that takes the name off the pointer takes the small card down once the handover has passed. The same two steps are run on a kind label and its bubble. The small wheel's size is read off the name's and the label's height on screen, never hard-coded.

**Verify:** `npm run check`, `npm run lint`, `npm test`. The one spec that proves the line: `npx playwright test e2e/browse.spec.ts`. CI proves on the push the specs that walk the same raisers and the grid: `e2e/reference.spec.ts`, `e2e/hover.spec.ts`, `e2e/recall.spec.ts`, `e2e/press.spec.ts`. Listed for the hand-back and not run locally.
