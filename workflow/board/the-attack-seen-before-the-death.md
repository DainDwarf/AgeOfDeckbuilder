# The attack seen before the death

**Line:** The attack seen before the death — a stage on units plays where any of its tiles is drawn live on the map as it stands or as the stage leaves it, and the map draws what the stage leaves once it has played, so an attack that kills the unit whose sight alone held its tiles is seen before the fog covers them.

**Scope:** In: the one gate the map holds on whether a stage on units plays — a move, an arrival, a unit damaged or healed, a unit killed, a unit prepared, and the attack group — reads the tiles drawn live on the chronicle the map stands on as well as on the one the stage leaves, and the pan that holds the stage's tiles comes with it. A kill no attack raises, a terraform leaving the player's own unit on ground it cannot stand on or a hazard that kills it, plays its shrink by the same rule and gets no case of its own. Out: an attack from a tile the map does not draw live still plays nothing and is not foreseen — every enemy has range one and attacks only what its own sight reaches, so no content reaches it; the motions themselves, their durations and their look, stay as they are. Decided here: what appears on an arrival stays what is live on the chronicle it enters on, so an enemy walking from fog into sight still appears as it does now and one walking out of sight still slides out. Reconciled: the tile changes — a terraform, a charting — already play on both faces, the one the map stands on and the one the change leaves, and the units' gate joins that shape; the arrivals stay apart, since what enters is read on the chronicle it enters on, a difference that is meant.
