# The animation speed setting

**Line:** The animation speed setting — Settings lists Graphics, Graphics lists the Animation speed row reading ×1, ×2 or ×4 and Back, a press on the row moving to the next speed and ×4 back to ×1; every tween on the chronicle screen's three scenes and the piles' wait play that many times faster, the hover rests do not; the speed set is kept in the browser beside the controls and read as the chronicle screen opens; a change made over a play-out in the air takes at once. Doc-impact: `docs/INTERFACE.md`, `docs/CHRONICLE-SCREEN.md`, `docs/PHASER.md`.

**Spec:** [`docs/CHRONICLE-SCREEN.md`](../../docs/CHRONICLE-SCREEN.md) → _The chronicle screen_, the paragraph "Every motion on the chronicle screen plays at the speed set in Settings" is the design of the speed itself and stands; its first clause becomes "**Every motion on the chronicle screen plays at the speed set in the Graphics window of Settings**". [`docs/INTERFACE.md`](../../docs/INTERFACE.md) → _The menu_: in the first paragraph, the sentence "**Settings** is where everything the player sets lives, and **Controls** is its first entry; the speed the chronicle screen's motions play at is set there too ([`CHRONICLE-SCREEN.md`](CHRONICLE-SCREEN.md)), and kept in the browser as the controls are." becomes "**Settings** is where everything the player sets lives, and lists **Controls** and **Graphics**." After the Controls paragraph, a new paragraph: "**Graphics** lists **Animation speed**: a row reading how fast the chronicle screen's motions play ([`CHRONICLE-SCREEN.md`](CHRONICLE-SCREEN.md)), ×1, ×2 or ×4 the speed they were designed at, a press moving it to the next and ×4 back to ×1; a change takes at once, a motion in the air included, and a motion of the hand's own — a scrolled panel's fling, a held key's pan — is no motion of the chronicle's and keeps its pace. **Back** closes the window as it does Controls, and what is set here is kept in the browser from one launch to the next." [`docs/PHASER.md`](../../docs/PHASER.md) → _Scenes and stacking_, two entries, each cited by file and line as the page's others are: "**A scene carries two time scales, and its clock's `now` follows neither.** The tween manager's scales every tween it owns (`src/tweens/TweenManager.js:99`, `src/tweens/tween/Tween.js:671`); the clock's scales every timer event it holds (`src/time/Clock.js:377`), while `scene.time.now` is the step's time, unscaled (`Clock.js:370`). A wait that is to follow the tweens runs on the tween manager's time, and one that is not stays on the clock." and "**The tween manager's time scale goes back to 1 at every scene start, and the clock's never does.** `TweenManager.start` resets it (`src/tweens/TweenManager.js:236`); `Clock.start` resets nothing of its own (`src/time/Clock.js:169-177`). A scale a scene is to run at is written after its start, at every start, since `overlayAhead` restarts the overlay at every screen change." The player-facing entries: the Settings window's new button reads **Graphics**; the Graphics window's title reads **Graphics**; its row's label reads **Animation speed**; its button reads **×1**, **×2** or **×4**, one entry `×{speed}`; its Back button reads the standing `control.back` entry, **Back**.

**Doc-impact:** `docs/INTERFACE.md`, `docs/CHRONICLE-SCREEN.md`, `docs/PHASER.md`.

**Scope:** In: the Graphics window under Settings, listing the Animation speed row and Back, closing back to Settings as Controls does, on the back key, the scrim and Back alike; the three speeds ×1, ×2, ×4, ×1 the speed every motion was designed at and the one a browser that kept nothing, or kept anything that is not one of the three, stands at; the speed kept in the browser in an entry of its own beside the controls' entry, read once and cached as the controls are; the tween manager's time scale of the `map`, `ui` and `overlay` scenes set to the speed as the chronicle screen opens and again at every press of the row while the chronicle screen stands, reached through the scene manager by key at the moment of the press; the piles' wait for a block of cards to land running on the tween manager's time, so it follows the speed and a change mid-wait, and is still let go of by a render as today; a Vitest test on the pure settings rule — parse, serialise, cycle. Out: the clock's time scale, which stays at 1 on every scene, so the tooltip's and the small cards' rests and hand-overs keep their spans; a scrolled panel's fling and a held key's pan, which are per-frame readings of the hand and not tweens; the overlay under the collection and launch screens, which restarts at every screen change and so stands at 1 there, as the page says the chronicle screen's motions alone; a Default button on Graphics, since one row wraps on its own; the suite's speed and the turn budget, the next line's; any other row of Graphics, each its own line when it comes. Corner cases decided here: the Graphics window stands over whatever the chronicle screen is doing, and a press on the row while an end of turn plays out speeds the rest of it at once, Phaser scaling each frame's delta; a speed kept from a previous launch is applied before the chronicle screen's first motion, so the opening animations run at it. Reconcile: the Animation speed row is drawn as the Controls window's wheel rows are — label left in the row's label style, a button right of the wheel rows' width and height reading the state, a press turning it — through the standing row shape as it is; the Graphics window goes through the standing window layout as Controls and Manage Save do, its Back the same button; the kept speed goes through the standing storage door and follows the controls' tolerant parse, in an entry of its own since the controls' entry is what the Controls window sets and Default there leaves the speed alone.

**Traps:** Phaser's two scales and the reset at start are the `docs/PHASER.md` entries above; the implementer writes them and works by them. The `overlay` scene is started before `map` and `ui` by `openChronicle`, so a scale written from the overlay's own create cannot know the chronicle screen is opening; the `ui` scene's create runs last of the three and sees all three running. `scene.time.now` stays unscaled, so the hand-over comparisons in `src/ui/tooltip.ts` and `src/ui/small-card.ts` and the fling's window in `src/ui/panel.ts` change meaning under no setting. A tween promised through `ended` in `src/ui/card-motion.ts` settles only at complete or stop, never at destroy, and the piles' wait today is let go of by `render` removing its timer event and resolving its promise by hand: a wait moved onto the tween manager's time must still be let go of the same way, or a render while a block is in the air leaves the end of turn hanging. The menu scene outlives every chronicle: it reaches the three scenes by key through the scene manager at the moment of the press, never through a handle taken at create (`docs/PHASER.md` → _Across a restart_). Player-facing text is keyed data in `src/ui/text.ts`; a window's buttons and title read text keys through the `WINDOWS` table in `src/ui/menu.ts`, and the Controls window's rows are laid by a body of their own in that file. A spec finds a window by its name, `standing(page, 'graphics')`, and a button by `${which}-${press}` as `settings-graphics` and `graphics-back`; `e2e/menu.spec.ts` and `e2e/controls.spec.ts` walk into Settings and assert nothing about what else it lists. The e2e helper `waitGameClock` waits on the clock, unscaled, and keeps meaning under any speed.
