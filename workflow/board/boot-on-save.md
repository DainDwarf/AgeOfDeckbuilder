# The game boots on the save

**Line:** The game boots on the save — the chronicle screen writes the save when a chronicle begins and after every command, on the outcome before the play-out; the bare address resumes the chronicle in progress where it stood, the capstone's window rising again, or opens the launch page where no save stands or the save is dropped; and nothing is written into the address any more. Done when `e2e/resume.spec.ts` passes: a chronicle played on the developer's door and reopened on the bare address stands where it stood, and a save that cannot be read is dropped and the launch page boots; and `e2e/boot.spec.ts` asserts the address stays what the boot read.

**Spec:** `docs/META.md` _The save_: the save is written whenever it changes, the chronicle in progress after every command, there is never a Save button, the game boots on the save, a reload of a chronicle in progress resumes it where it stood, a chronicle's save the boot cannot read whole is refused at the boot and dropped and the boot says why on the browser's console alone, and the address that opens a chronicle straight is a launch like any other. `docs/INTERFACE.md` _The launch page_: the bare address boots on the save, the chronicle in progress where it stood and the page where none stands; an address naming a deck opens the chronicle straight, a launch like any other; any other address that names something opens the page with what it names already chosen, whatever the save holds; the chronicle screen writes nothing into the address; a chronicle's save keeps the choices it was launched on beside it, and the menu's New chronicle deals the same choices a fresh seed. `docs/CHRONICLE-SCREEN.md` _The capstone's window_ and _The ending screen_: the window rises when the chronicle screen opens, on a chronicle begun and on one resumed alike; a chronicle opened on a deal opens on the deal's window; an ended chronicle stands on its ending screen. **Scope:**

- In: the storage. The save is one entry of `window.localStorage`, its text the save module of `src/rules/` writes and reads; the entry's name is a constant exported from `src/`, since the proof spec plants a save under it. It holds the chronicle's save alone: the campaign rung adds its part when that part exists, and no envelope is built for it now.
- In: the writing. The chronicle screen writes the save when a chronicle begins — a launch from the page, the address's straight opening, the menu's New chronicle — and after every command, from the outcome the rules answered and before the play-out walks its stages, so a reload mid-animation lands on the command's outcome. A write that fails — storage full, storage refused — is caught, said on the browser's console, and play goes on.
- In: the boot. An address that names nothing the boot reads — none of content, region, schedule, deck, seed — is bare, and boots on the save: the entry's text read whole against the catalogue its content names resumes the chronicle screen on that chronicle and those choices; no entry opens the launch page on its defaults as today; an entry that cannot be read — a content version no catalogue is named by, or the save module's refusal — is removed from storage, its error's words go to the browser's console as a warning, and the launch page opens on its defaults. Storage that cannot be reached at all counts as no save. An address naming a deck opens the chronicle straight as today, and that chronicle's begin writes the save over whatever stood; an address naming something but no deck opens the page as today, the save untouched.
- In: the address. The chronicle screen's rewriting of it goes, whole: after a launch from the page the address stays bare, after the straight opening it stays what it was, so a reload of the developer's door replays the launch as it does today.
- In: the first render of a resumed chronicle. One that runs opens on the capstone's window, as a begun one does; on its close the screen stands as the chronicle stands, and where the chronicle waits on a deal the deal's window rises then. An ended chronicle's screen opens on its ending screen and the capstone's window does not rise. Today's screen cannot be handed either state at its opening, and its render raises the capstone's window ahead of everything on the first call.
- In: the menu's New chronicle after a resume deals the same region, schedule and deck a fresh seed, the choices read from the save.
- In: `e2e/resume.spec.ts`, the proof: a chronicle opened through the suite's door on the stand-in, settled and one turn ended, then the bare address opened on the same page; the chronicle screen stands, the capstone's window is up and closes on its card, and the chronicle read through the screen equals the one read before the reload. A second test plants text that is no save under the entry's name, opens the bare address, and finds the launch page standing and the entry gone, the run's problems empty — a warning is not an error.
- In: `e2e/boot.spec.ts`: the test on the address following every chronicle begun becomes one on the address staying bare through Launch and New chronicle, its seed read through the screen and no longer off the address; the console test reads the seed it typed through the screen. Neither is weakened: what changed is the rule.
- Out: `e2e/controls.spec.ts`. Its reload stands on the suite's door, which names a deck, so the reload replays the launch and the capstone's window waits under the menu as its comment says; nothing changes there.
- Out: a console entry for the seed, two tabs on one save, the meta's part of the save, the campaign screen and Continue.
- Corner decided: a save on the stand-in content resumes like any other; the boot resolves the version through the catalogues the game holds, the stand-in among them.
- Corner decided: a resumed chronicle on the settle phase with no city yet is resumed like any other; the screen already draws that state.
- Corner decided: the failed-boot page is never reached through a save. Every read of the save at the boot is caught.

**Traps:**

- `docs/PHASER.md` _Scenes and stacking_ and _Across a restart_, which the boot in `src/main.ts` and `newChronicle` in `src/ui/chronicle-scene.ts` already cite: the ui scene reaches into the console's, the menu's, the overlay's and the map's as it is created, so the start order at boot is load-bearing, and a restart with no data keeps the data of the last start. If the resumed chronicle rides in the scene's start data, the restart New chronicle makes must hand data that carries no chronicle, or the restart resumes the old one again.
- `src/failed-boot.ts` watches from the first import and turns any error thrown during the boot into the failed-boot page, which `e2e/failed-boot.spec.ts` proves. The save is read inside a catch.
- `src/rules/` never touches the DOM: the storage lives in `src/ui/` or the boot, the text in the rules module. `src/ui/bindings.ts` is the precedent, one named constant for the entry and `window.localStorage` behind one reader and one writer.
- `src/content/catalogues.ts`'s `catalogueOf` throws on a version no catalogue holds, and the save module refuses through `refuse` of `src/rules/map-kinds.ts`; both are errors whose words the warning carries.
- In `src/ui/chronicle-scene.ts`, `playOut` moves `this.current` on at every leaf and settles it on `outcome(stages)` in its `finally`; the save is written from `outcome(stages)` right after `apply`, before the walk. `begin` draws the fresh seed through `Math.random`, the one place entropy enters, and rewrites the address through `history.replaceState`: the second goes, the first stays.
- In `src/ui/overlay.ts`, the first render raises the capstone's window ahead of the ending screen and the deal window (`announced`), and `closeCapstone` for the opening's raising closes and renders nothing after; the deal window and the ending screen rise only at a render.
- The spec helpers in `e2e/chronicle-screen.ts` open through the suite's door with a deck on the address, and `open` closes the capstone's window itself; a spec reopening the bare address waits for the ui scene as `openOnCapstone` does, rests, and closes the window through its card, never the back key.
- Playwright gives every test a fresh browser context, so no save leaks between tests; within one test the storage persists across a `goto`. `page.addInitScript` runs before every page the test loads from then on, so a script that plants a save re-plants it at every load in that test.
- A spec rests before it presses what just rose (`DOGMAS.md` _Testing_, `docs/PHASER.md` _Under a Playwright spec_).
