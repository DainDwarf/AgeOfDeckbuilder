# A card face read in place, and the hand

**Line:** **A card face read in place, and the hand** — a card face re-reads another card, refusal and tone in place through its objects' setters, its cost chips, its rules glyphs and its name zones pooled, its ring and its aim point made once; the chronicle screen holds one face per card key, made for every card of the chronicle when the screen opens and for every card gained at a render, destroyed at the render that finds the card gone and with the screen, and shared by the hand and the piles; the hand lays out the faces of the cards it holds, every one written in place at every render and taking the pointer only while its card lies in the hand; a card leaving the hand flies on its own face and turns over in place into the card it lies in the pile as, a card drawn rides a back from a pool; a render of the hand destroys nothing. Doc-impact: `docs/PHASER.md`.

**Spec:** `DOGMAS.md` _Stack_, "A game object is made once and changed in place", is the rule; its clause "what has an identity and is few is made when it enters and destroyed when it leaves" is the face's shape, and "what comes and goes in numbers is pooled, hidden and reused" the backs', the chips', the glyphs' and the zones'. `docs/CHRONICLE-SCREEN.md` _The hand and the aim_ states what the hand shows and answers — the lift, the ring, the point, the line, the drag — and _The piles_ that a card exhausted or discarded flies from the hand, "turning over into the card it became on the way". `docs/INTERFACE.md` _The presses_ states that the pointer "is right the moment that thing rises, falls or comes live under a pointer that holds still", and _What stands over what_ that a scrim rising sends a dragged card home. None of it moves. No player-facing sentence: the player sees the same hand.

`docs/PHASER.md` _Under a Playwright spec_ gains one entry after "An object made interactive is hit-tested only from the next frame", written out:

> - **Input re-enabled on an object is live at once.** `enable` on an object that already carries an `input` sets its `enabled` flag and queues nothing (`src/input/InputPlugin.js:934-938`), and `disable` clears the flag and takes the object out of every pointer's over and drag lists (`:861-893`); only the first `setInteractive`, which makes the component, waits a frame (`:2434`). `setInteractive` and `disableInteractive` on the object are those two calls (`src/gameobjects/GameObject.js:560-590`).

**Doc-impact:** `docs/PHASER.md`, the entry above. The dogma states the rule and the chronicle screen page states what the hand shows, which does not change.

**Scope:**

In, by part:

- The face's re-read: one face takes another `Face`, `Refusal` and tone and writes every object in place — the paper and its edge redrawn in the palette the refusal picks, the cost chips from a pool of the face's with their marks and amounts, the name, the kind label and the influence number and diamond set and shown or hidden, the rules text set so its wrap callback lays the run out again, the glyphs from a pool, the `names` data rewritten, the name zones from a pool for a face made with presses, the art box redrawn under the rules' new height, the ring kept and hidden, the aim point one polygon made with the face and shown while aimed. The width and whether names answer presses stay the face's for life. The tone is a re-read parameter: one face lies plain in the hand and worn on the discard pile.
- The chronicle's faces: one per card key, the scene's, built from the chronicle at create for every card of its four piles and handed to the hand and the piles in the bundle the foot bubble and the small cards already ride in. At every render the faces are the chronicle's cards: made for every key the chronicle holds and no face does, destroyed for every face whose key is in no pile. The scene's restart destroys them all with the scene, as today; nothing is kept across chronicles. A face not shown by the hand stands hidden; the discard pile's top goes on drawing its own worn face until the piles' line.
- The hand's render: for every card of the hand, its face found by key, its motion stopped and its end state written — position, rotation, scale, alpha, visibility, layer, depth, name `hand-<index>`, the ring off, the face re-read with the card's refusal and the city's building — and for every face that left the hand, hidden, its name cleared and its input disabled; a face entering the hand gets its input enabled, the first time through `setInteractive` with its hit rectangle and `draggable`, after that through `enable`, which is live at once. The compare of the hand reference and the refusals that kept the faces goes with its comment: its reason was the rebuilt card's lost frame, which a retained face does not lose. The hover behaves as today: the pointer reading of each frame finds the same object and keeps the card lifted across a render, and a card the pointer rests on after a card before it left is the card now under it, lifted.
- The handlers: hover, click, right click, drag start and move are bound once per face, when the hand first meets it, and read the card's slot and refusal at the press from what the last render wrote; the records the hand keeps across presses — the selection, the drag, the card let go of, the card held by a small card — hold the face's record, keyed by the card.
- The flights: a card leaving for a pile flies on its own face, narrowing to an edge where it lies, re-read as the card the pile holds under its key, plain, widened again and carried to the pile's place; a card drawn rides a back taken from the hand's pool of backs, carried from the draw pile's place to the card's home, where the back narrows, hides, and the card's own face widens from the edge. The hand's list of what it has in the air goes: a render stops every motion of the faces and the backs it meets and writes their end state, the backs hidden back into their pool, the faces hidden or laid out as the chronicle stands.
- The turn-over: one motion in `src/ui/card-motion.ts` that narrows one object to an edge, runs what the caller hands it at the edge, and widens the object again; the hand's two turn-overs go through it, the first re-reading the face at the edge, the second swapping the back for the face there. The piles' shuffle goes through it in the piles' line.
- The pool helper and the reset at reuse leave `src/ui/map.ts` for a shared UI module, as they stand — make on demand, hide every object and stop its motion, hand the next one back shown, whole, at full strength — and the hand's pool of backs and the face's end state go through them; the map keeps using them from there.

Out: the piles' tops, the next line's — the draw pile's back retained, the discard pile showing its top card's own face, the shuffle through the shared turn-over. The faces on the scrim — the browse, the deal and capstone windows, the card shown large, the small card — made by id and destroyed with their window, the cards-and-windows rung's. The collection, deck and launch screens' faces. The map's units and tiles, retained already. The hand's layout, the fan and the lane, unchanged.

Corner cases decided here:

- A render while a face flies, slides home or turns over stops its motion and writes its end state; `e2e/broken-motion.spec.ts` asserts exactly one shown face per card of the hand and none past the hand's length after a dropped motion.
- A face re-read to the text it already holds costs nothing: `setText` compares first, and the glyphs and names lay out off the run the callback wrote last. A face re-read to another text reruns the callback inside `setText`, so the run is current before the glyphs and the names are laid out.
- A card that transforms on its way to a pile keeps its key, so `cardKeyed` finds it in the pile and the face re-reads the card it lies there as; a card banished leaves the hand where it lies with no flight, and the render that finds its key in no pile destroys its face.
- A face's names on the hand are read by the hand's own `nameAt` on the pointer's move, as today; the hand's faces are made without presses, so their zone pool stays empty.
- Nothing in the hand pools faces by slot: a slot is where a card's face stands this render, and the next render may stand another card's face there.

The reconcile, four likenesses: the per-card record and the map's per-unit record stay apart, the shape repeated and nothing shared; the map's pool helper and reset become the hand's too, lifted into a shared UI module unchanged; the hand's turn-over and the piles' shuffle become one turn-over taking what happens at the edge, the hand through it here and the shuffle in the piles' line; the chronicle's faces go through the bundle the scene already hands the hand and the piles.

**Traps:**

- `docs/PHASER.md` _Scenes and stacking_: a hidden object is neither drawn nor hit, so a face hidden under a stale name is not counted; a Text set to the text it holds does nothing, and the wrap callback runs inside `updateText` on a changed text (_Rendering under WebGL_), the constructor running it twice where the style carries padding; a Layer draws by depth first and add order among equal depths, so the hand's depth per slot is written at every render. _Rendering under WebGL_: `killTweensOf` destroys a tween silently and a promise waiting on it waits for ever; `stopMotion` stops, which resolves; `turnOver` today destroys the object it narrows, and the back it narrows now is a pooled object that hides instead.
- `docs/PHASER.md` _The pointer's readings_: `disableInteractive` leaves the canvas cursor standing; the game's own cursor is written by `followPointer` in `src/ui/design-space.ts` from what it finds under the pointer each frame, so a face disabled under a resting pointer is left on that frame and the cursor follows. `onHover` records live on the object and are deleted at its destroy, which a kept face never reaches; a second `onHover` on a face already carrying one adds a second record, so the hand binds once per face.
- The pile's press zone is added to the resting layer after the pill and stands over the pile's top card in the render list, so a face lying at the pile's place with its input left enabled would still lose the press to the zone; the hand disables it anyway, and the piles' line relies on that.
- `e2e/chronicle-screen.ts` readers prefer the first shown object of a name and fall back to the first found; `counted` and `standing` answer the shown; `part('ring')`, `part('rules')`, `kind-label`, `influence-label` and `aim-point` are found by name in the face's own list, so every pooled or retained child keeps its name; `names` is the root's data, rewritten at each re-read; `standing(page, 'aim-point')` reads shown, so the point made once is hidden while the card is not being aimed.
- `e2e/press.spec.ts` "a click on the top of a card of the hand while the end of turn plays out stops on the card" is the trap the retained face closes; `e2e/capstone.spec.ts`, `e2e/settle.spec.ts` and `e2e/broken-motion.spec.ts` assert `hand-<length>` not standing, so a face leaving the hand has its name cleared at that render.
- The hit rectangle's height is written per face in `settle` to hold a lifted card under the pointer; it is the face's own rectangle now, set at its first `setInteractive`.
- `src/ui/piles.ts` `topOf` makes its own worn face until the piles' line; the face it makes and the card's own face both stand at the pile's place for no frame, since the render that hides the flown face is the one the pile's landing calls.
- The chronicle's faces are built from `this.current` at create, which is every card of the four piles; a chronicle opened on a deal or on the capstone's window is the same chronicle.
- `src/ui/map.ts` is past 2,100 lines and its cut is a line of its own; this line takes the pool helper and the reset out of it and changes nothing else there.
- A hook flags a comment block longer than three lines touched under `src/` or `e2e/`; advisory.
- CI's browse flake comes back when the screen's frame cost rises (`workflow/LOOK.md`); this line adds hidden faces, which a Layer skips, and should move nothing, read off the run.

**Plan:**

1. A shared UI module: the pool helper and the reset at reuse out of `src/ui/map.ts`, the map reading them from there. Leaves the map as it stands on shared helpers.
2. `src/ui/card-face.ts`: the face re-reads another card, refusal and tone in place, its chips, glyphs and zones pooled, its aim point made once. Leaves every site making faces as today, the re-read unused.
3. `src/ui/card-motion.ts`: the turn-over takes one object and what happens at the edge. Leaves the hand's flights on the new turn-over, still on made-and-destroyed objects.

5. `src/ui/hand.ts`: the hand on the chronicle's faces — the render writing every face in place and enabling or disabling its input, the handlers bound once per face, the records by card, the flights on the card's own face and on pooled backs, the compare and its comment gone.
