**Scope:**

- In: the shortfall rule at the bottom of the rules, where the fall is born: a change that would leave a stock of either city below nothing is emitted with that stock at nothing, and one population of that city is taken right after it for each stock so left short, the fall then read on what that leaves; the shock helper no longer floors, the floor being the bottom's; Hunger's and Misunderstandings' strikes reduced to their shock; the fixture's Drought losing its shortfall clause; the shortfall's fixture tests moved onto the bottom rule; the two card texts; the three pages.
- Out: the resource bar's reading, the next line; any kind tooltip or card text naming the rule (the user's call, fork 1); the glossary, which needs no row — the sentence uses stock and population, and "short" is plain; a fall of the neutral for no population, which no rule names and no content reaches; saves and campaigns, which need no care before the Bronze Age ends.
- Corner cases decided here: no chronicle any stage carries ever holds a stock below nothing — the change is emitted already floored, the population change after it, so the play-out reads "takes three culture, one leaves" and the bar never shows a minus (fork 2, as recommended); depth does not matter, a stock at minus one and one at minus twenty each cost one population; a change leaving several stocks short takes as many population, one after another; a stock left at exactly nothing is not short and takes nobody, as today; a city with no population left to take has the stock set to nothing and nothing more; the player's city's last population taken falls the chronicle on that change, as today; the neutral's stocks are read like the player's, and a neutral taken to no population stands with nobody; a cost never reaches the bottom short, every cost being refused when unaffordable, and no guard is added for it.
- Reconcile, every likeness chosen by the user: the shock helper's floor and the three strikes' own shortfall clauses (Hunger's, Misunderstandings', the fixture's Drought) become the bottom rule and nothing else; the fixture's Drought then differs from the fixture's Hunger in nothing but its number, so the shortfall tests move onto one card and whether the second stays is the implementer's; Sickness takes a person outright and touches no stock, a difference that is meant, so it stays apart.
