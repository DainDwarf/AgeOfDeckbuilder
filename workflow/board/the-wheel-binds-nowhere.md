# The wheel binds nowhere, and under a scrim the screen hears no key

**Line:** The wheel binds nowhere, and under a scrim the screen hears no key — no slot of the Controls window takes a wheel notch, the wheel zooms the map and scrolls what scrolls frontmost under the pointer, `=` and `-` are the zooms' default keys, and while anything stands on a scrim the map and the tree under it hear no pan and no zoom, the campaign screen no longer raising the menu on a notch bound to the back key. Doc-impact: `docs/INTERFACE.md`, `docs/META-SCREENS.md`.

**Spec:** `docs/INTERFACE.md` and `docs/META-SCREENS.md`, the sentences below written as given; no other page speaks of the wheel or of a key heard under a scrim.

**Scope:**

- In: the wheel leaves the bindings. No slot takes a notch, a slot listening keeps listening through one, and a notch stored in a slot from a launch that still bound it leaves that slot empty, as a stored left or right click does. The zooms' defaults are `=` first slot and `-` first slot, second slots empty. The map zooms one notch per wheel notch as today, a notch being the same accumulation of the browser's deltas the wheel reader makes now, so a trackpad zooms as it does today; a wheel under Ctrl, Meta or Alt is the browser's and zooms nothing, as today. The wheel zooms the map wherever the pointer stands on the chronicle screen while nothing stands on a scrim, the hand and the bar included, as today; under the debug console the map still zooms, as `docs/INTERFACE.md` says of the console.
- In: under a scrim of the overlay — a browse, the deal window, the aim window, the capstone's window, a card shown large, the ending screen — the chronicle screen hears no key: the pans and the zooms join the keys the scrim already holds. A pan key held as the scrim rises pans no further, the way a held key stops when a window of the menu rises (`view.live(false)` at `src/ui/chronicle-scene.ts:582` is that precedent). On the campaign screen, under a card shown large, the tree hears no key either; the wheel keeps doing nothing there. The launch screen and the collection screen already hear no key under a scrim.
- In: the campaign screen's defect closes by construction — a notch is no key, so no binding of the back key can raise the menu on one — and every guard that read a notch specially (`src/ui/browse.ts`, `src/ui/collection-screen.ts`, `src/ui/overlay.ts` twice, `src/ui/tree.ts`) goes with the wheel's key reading.
- Out: the pan keys scrolling panels and browses; a setting inverting the wheel; a minimize button on the scrim's windows. Each is a board line or an idea of its own.
- Corner cases decided here: the Controls window says nothing about the wheel; a `=` or `-` bound elsewhere by the player moves as any key does; the wheel over the collection screen's navbar or bar scrolls nothing and does nothing.
