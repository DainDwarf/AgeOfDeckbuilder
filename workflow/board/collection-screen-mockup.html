The collection screen in three modes

One panel on the right that grows leftwards: the collection, then a deck being edited, then the civilization alone. Every press works, and in the deck editing mode a card drags across. The Nomadic cards, their costs and their counts are the game's; every price, every card past the Nomadic Age and every civilization but Nomadic is a placeholder.

Collection mode

  • Every card owned with its copies, and a price button that buys one more.
  • A press on a civilization's pile opens its deck for editing.

Deck editing mode

  • A press on a stack puts one copy in; a press on a row takes one out.
  • A stack dragged onto the right panel, or a row dragged onto the left, does the same.
  • A copy put in one deck is still free for every other deck.

Civilization mode

  • − takes a copy out. + puts one in: a copy owned and not yet in this deck where there is one, a copy bought where there is none, and then the button reads its price.
  • A card taken down to none stays where it stood, at half strength, until the mode is left.

Placeholders

  • Every price is 10.
  • The words on the three mode buttons and under the stacks.
  • "Card 9" and up, "Civilization 2" and "Civilization 3".
