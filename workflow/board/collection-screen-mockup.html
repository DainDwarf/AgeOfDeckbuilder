The collection screen in three modes

One panel on the right that grows leftwards: the collection, then a deck being edited, then the civilization alone. Every press works, and in the deck editing mode a card drags across. The Nomadic cards, their costs and their counts are the game's; every card past the Nomadic Age and every civilization but Nomadic is a placeholder, and so is every base price past the Stone Age.

The civilization mode
A copy is dragged across

Collection mode

  • Every card owned with its copies, and a price button that buys one more.
  • A price is the base price of the card's age, doubled for every copy owned past the first. The Nomadic Age and the Stone Age have a base price of 1.
  • A press on a civilization's pile opens its deck for editing.

Deck editing mode

  • A press on a stack puts one copy in; a press on a row takes one out.
  • A stack dragged onto the right panel, or a row dragged onto the left, does the same.
  • A copy put in one deck is still free for every other deck.

A copy is dragged across

  • A press held anywhere on a panel still drags it. On a card that can land, the first move past the slack decides: mostly sideways carries the card, mostly up or down scrolls the panel. Try it on Four ages, 48 cards.
  • A card lands anywhere on the other side of the line between the panels, head included. Let go on its own side, on the bar or the navbar, and it comes home with nothing changed.
  • The carried card is held where it was grabbed, at full strength, and the stack or row it left stands as it was until the release.

Civilization mode

  • The first line ships it read only: the name over the room, centred, the two sections with their counts beside their words, seven stacks to a line, and Collection », which brings the collection back beside the civilization.
  • The second line adds the buttons, on the panel's paper. − takes a copy out. + puts in a copy owned that this deck does not hold; where the deck holds them all the button reads the price alone, and a press buys a copy and puts it in.
  • A card taken down to none stays where it stood, at half strength, until the mode is left: + puts it back. Set to go at once, the stack leaves and the ones after it close up under the pointer.
  • Between the two buttons the reading is the two numbers alone, held over owned, centred in the room the buttons leave: the words "In deck" do not fit beside two buttons.
  • A card face answers the right click and nothing else; set to remove, a press on it does what − does.

Placeholders

  • The base prices past the Stone Age: 2, then 4.
  • The words on the three mode buttons and under the stacks.
  • "Card 9" and up, "Civilization 2" and "Civilization 3".
