# The neutral's grievance

**Line:** **The neutral's grievance** — the Stone Age's quarrel with the neutral, dealt on every map, no need named: _Escalate_ declares war while the neutral's city stands at peace, places a camp with warriors near the player's city otherwise and raids where no tile takes one, its card reading the text of whichever case stands; _Pay tribute_ adds the challenge **Tribute** to the discard pile, whose strike takes food, production and money and sets its own counters higher for the next; _Send gifts_ pays culture once, more on a later turn. A strike that sets its card's counters, an answer whose card reads the text of its case, and the camp-or-raid shape Make room shares are the mechanisms, each in its own inert commit. Done when the event stands on both Stone schedules, `npm test` is green with the coherence test and the mechanism tests among it, `e2e/grievance.spec.ts` passes, and the three pages say so. **Scope:**

- In, three mechanisms, each one inert commit ahead of the content, in this order or any:
  - **A strike that sets its card's counters.** A strike may leave its own card in the hand carrying new values under the counters its content declares; the card goes on in the hand at those values, so the next cycle strikes at them and the face reads them; a counter the card does not declare is refused as it is everywhere. The hand is the row moved; where no change name of the closed set says it, one is new and every listener takes it. One test on the fixture: a fixture challenge whose strike takes a stock by a counter and sets it higher, in the hand at the end of a turn, takes the counter's value and leaves the card in the hand at the new one; through a second cycle it takes the new value; the save written after the first strike reads the card back at the new value.
  - **An answer whose card reads the text of its case.** An answer may declare its cases, a closed list of its content's own, and name one of them on the chronicle as it stands, through no step of the generator; the deal draws the text of that case for its card, under one entry per case; an answer declaring none reads its one entry as today. One test on the fixture: a fixture answer declaring two cases names the one its chronicle meets, and the other on a chronicle that does not. The coherence tests in `src/content/catalogue.test.ts` widen so that an answer declaring cases has a text found for every case it declares, not the one the settled chronicle meets, and every name a case's text marks is in its table.
  - **The camp or the raid.** One content shape both slices compose: a camp placed near the player's city, through the standing camp-placing door with its spans, and warriors entered on and around it as guards, as Make room does; and where no tile takes a camp, the same count of warriors entered as raiders through the standing raid door, which always enters. Make room goes through it, its behaviour unchanged where it is reached: its need keeps the raid branch out of its reach until the line that removes the need. One test beside the shape, the real closure on the fixture's ground: a ground with a tile places the camp with its guards, and a ground with none enters the raid.
- In, the content, one commit with its measurement: the event `grievance` in the Stone slice's events, answers declared in the order _Escalate_, _Pay tribute_, _Send gifts_. The count n = one and one more for every ten turns, the event's own arithmetic as each event keeps its own. _Escalate_ costs nothing, is refused by nothing, reads `{ warriors: n }`, declares the cases `war`, `camp` and `raid`, names `war` while the neutral's city stands and relations are at peace, `camp` otherwise where the camp-placing door finds a tile at the rival band's spans, written out in the Stone slice, and `raid` otherwise; it lands the war declared through the standing door in the `war` case, and the camp-or-raid shape with n warriors in the others. _Pay tribute_ costs nothing, reads `{ food: 2, production: 2, money: 1 }` and lands one copy of `tribute` on the discard pile through the reward helper. _Send gifts_ costs `{ culture: 6 + floor(turn / 5) }`, reads the same, and lands the chronicle unchanged. **Traps:**

- `warDeclared` in `src/rules/city.ts` is the one `relations` change, nothing where relations are not at peace; `cityRows(chronicle, 'neutral')` in `src/rules/state.ts` is undefined once the neutral's city has fallen, whoever took it, and `relations` stays what it was. The `relations` change has so far come inside a move, an attack or a play; here it comes inside the `answer` group, and the listeners in `src/ui/hand.ts`, `src/ui/map.ts` and `src/ui/piles.ts` switch over the change by name, not by its group.
- `struck` in `src/rules/cards.ts` walks the hand and hands each strike `counterOf(catalogue, held)`, a reader; the `Strike` type in `src/rules/catalogue.ts` is `(catalogue, chronicle, counter) => Landed`, and which card of the hand is striking is known to the loop and not to the strike. `cardMade` there refuses a counter the content does not declare; `src/rules/save.ts` reads every declared counter back and refuses a card lacking one.
- The change names are the closed sets in `src/rules/stages.ts`, and every listener switches over them with no default: `src/ui/hand.ts`, `src/ui/map.ts`, `src/ui/piles.ts`, `src/ui/overlay.ts`, `src/ui/resource-bar.ts`; a name added fails the typecheck in each until it is taken. No change name today says a card's counters moved.
- `answerFace` in `src/ui/face.ts` reads one entry per answer through `answerRules` in `src/ui/text.ts`, naming the city's building and the age's camp beside the reading; the fixture's answers have no text entries, so a case's text is proven on the real content alone, in `src/content/catalogue.test.ts` (the entries test near its top and the every-answer test near its end), which read one settled chronicle and so meet `war` alone unless widened. `src/ui/face.test.ts` is where the pure face is tested in Node.
- `campsPlaced` and `campPlaceable` in `src/rules/schedule.ts` are the camp-placing door, `enteredAround` and `raided` in `src/rules/enemies.ts` the entries; `campsPlaced` is a `runtime-error` where no tile fits and refuses a chronicle whose city stands nowhere, and `raided` is one for a count of none. `RIVAL_CAMP` in `src/content/nomadic.ts` holds the spans, `[3, 4]` from the city and `3` apart; the Stone slice writes its own values out, schedules and constants never being factorized across slices. `src/content/scripts.ts` is the standing shared content module.
- `shocked` in `src/rules/cards.ts` is the stock harm that takes one population past nothing, once per stock; `populationTaken` raises the fall on the last; nothing of play resolves after the fall, `docs/CHRONICLE.md` _Units and combat_.
- Every event must deal one answer costing no stock whatever the chronicle and blocked by nothing: _Escalate_ is it, and the validator in `src/rules/catalogue.ts` refuses the slice without it. The merge refuses an answer id two events deal and a card id two slices bring; `escalate`, `pay-tribute`, `send-gifts`, `grievance` and `tribute` are new to the tree, and an answer id and a card id share no namespace.
- The glossary lint hook reads forbidden synonyms in `src/ui/text.ts`: "Declare war" is the glossary's verb; "attacked by" is Fight them's phrasing; "tribute", "gifts", "escalate", "grievance" and "amends" are no glossary words and no forbidden ones.
- The specs' seed search (`firstDealtAlone`, `offeredOn`, `firstEntriesTaken` in `e2e/chronicle-screen.ts`) takes the first of a thousand seeds whose first deal is the event alone, and the first answer the rules admit of every deal, so Stone chronicles ended headlessly take _Escalate_ from the first grievance on and run hostile from there; adding an entry to the schedule shifts which event a seed deals, so every Stone spec's seed search finds other seeds, and none names one. `warDeclared` is a helper a card composes, so a spec may make its chronicle hostile through it before writing the save, as `e2e/neutral.spec.ts` does.
- The resource bar reads culture as a count over the culture threshold, `barReadingOn` in `e2e/chronicle-screen.ts`; the spec reads it so and never a literal. `unit-state.hostile` in `src/ui/text.ts` is the line a unit of the neutral's wears on its infopanel card while hostile.
