# A copy is added or bought from the deck

**Line:** **A copy is added or bought from the deck** — in the civilization mode each stack reads its copies held over owned between two buttons: − removes a copy, + adds a copy owned that the deck does not hold, and where the deck holds them all the button reads the card's price and one press buys a copy and adds it, greyed while unaffordable; a card taken down to none stands dimmed until the mode is left; `e2e/civilization-mode.spec.ts` removes, adds and buys, each read from the rules and written to the save; the ship deletes the mockup and the Lines section's paragraph about it.

**Spec:**

- The look is the mockup's, [collection-screen-mockup.html](collection-screen-mockup.html), with Mode on Civilization, "Shown as it stands after" on "The second line: a copy added or bought", and every control on its recommended option; where the mockup and this dossier disagree, the dossier holds.
- `docs/META-SCREENS.md`, _The collection screen_, the civilization mode's paragraph. Its sentence "Each card the civilization holds stands once in its section, as a stack of the copies the deck holds, seven stacks to a line, the lines centred in the room, in the order of the collection, and under a stack reads the copies the deck holds over the copies owned; a deck holding no card says so." becomes: "Each card the civilization holds stands once in its section, as a stack of the copies the deck holds, seven stacks to a line, the lines centred in the room, in the order of the collection, and under a stack reads the copies the deck holds over the copies owned, between two buttons on the panel's paper; a deck where no stack stands says that it holds no card."
- Same paragraph, its last three sentences — "A card there answers the right click and the rest as a card does anywhere. A copy is added and removed from there. A card added takes a copy owned that the deck does not hold, where there is one; where there is none, the one press buys a copy and adds it." — become: "A card there answers the right click and the rest as a card does anywhere, and no left click. The button at the left of a stack's reading removes one copy from the deck, and the one at its right adds one: it takes a copy owned that the deck does not hold, where there is one; where there is none, the button reads the card's price, as the price reads in the collection mode, and the one press buys a copy and adds it. A button that can do nothing stands greyed and answers no press: the one that removes under a card the deck holds no copy of, the price of an unaffordable card. A card taken down to no copy stands where it stood, dimmed, until the mode is left; a card the deck held no copy of when the mode opened does not stand, and is added in the deck editing mode."
- Nothing else on the page moves: "Buying raises no warning", "a card added or removed [leaves the panels where they stood], and a copy bought" already stand.
- Player-facing text, three new entries in `src/ui/text.ts`: `'collection.held-of-owned': '{held}/{copies}'`, `'collection.remove-copy': '−'` (U+2212), `'collection.add-copy': '+'`. The price on the right button reads the existing `collection.price`; the section words, the counts, the title and the empty deck's sentence are the existing entries. `collection.in-deck` stays the deck editing mode's.

**Doc-impact:** `docs/META-SCREENS.md`.

**Traps:**

- `edit` in `src/ui/collection-screen.ts` lays the screen again in the same mode after every move, and `deckRowsOf` answers only the cards the deck holds: a card taken down to none is in no row the second time. Which cards stand has to outlive a relay and not a fresh entry into the mode; `lay({ shows: 'civilization', … })` is called for both. Where that is kept is the implementer's.
- The buy and the add are one move handed to `edit`, so one `keepCampaign`: `bought` deals the copy into no deck, and `addedTo` then takes the one free copy.
- `addedTo` refuses a card the deck holds every copy of, `removedFrom` one it holds none of, `bought` an unaffordable one: a greyed button hands no press (`Held.press` undefined), as `priceButtonOf` does with no `buy`; the panel reads the hand from a press being there (`src/ui/panel.ts`).
- `stackOf` takes a stack's copies for the cards under its face; at no copy it draws a face alone. The dimming is `Reading.dimmed`, the colours mixed towards the page, never a container's alpha (`src/ui/collection-stack.ts`).
- `priceButtonOf` lays the price at the line's right end and joins the stack's root; the − and the + are its siblings on the same line. What is shared and what is repeated is the implementer's, under _Single source of truth for facts; repetition for shape_.
- A text's width includes its padding: the reading is centred, and the signs too, from `ownBoxOf`, as the price is placed beside its diamond.
- `priceOf` and `unaffordableIn` refuse a card the collection owns no copy of; every card that stands here is owned.
- A piece the scene builds receives the catalogue as an argument (`DOGMAS.md` _Stack_); the mode is a closed union switched with a `never` check.
