# The personas and the neutral guard their incomes

**Line:** The personas and the neutral guard their incomes — no gambit of either persona claims, assigns, reassigns, unassigns or places, and the neutral's script neither assigns nor builds, in a way that takes any resource's income below nothing or lower while it is, each guard held by one test on the fixture, the design pages saying so. Doc-impact: `docs/SIMULATOR.md`, `docs/ages/STONE.md`.

**Scope:**

- In: the guard on the five gambits of both personas — the idle assign, the reassign, the warlord's unassign for a unit, the claim, the place through a worker — and on the goal gambits' claim and play through a worker; the two walks that weigh a play through a worker to decide where a worker goes, the builder's and the goal's, read the guard too, so a worker never walks to a play the guard refuses. The guard reads the income alone, yields of the worked tiles less the upkeep, the shortfall counted past nothing; the strikes in hand are no part of it. "Takes below nothing or lower while it is" means: the command is dropped where some resource's income after it is below nothing and below what it was before it; one that leaves an income below nothing where it stands, or raises it, passes. The guard is the persona's, said once in its paragraph as the no-war check is; the gambits' sentences stay as they are.
- In: the neutral's villager lays its population upkeep first — for each resource its upkeep takes, in the resources' order, the unworked held tile giving the most of it, until the income covers that resource or no tile left gives any — then food first, most of everything of equals, as today; its worker builds nothing whose build, with the population laid over the tiles as the villager would lay it on the chronicle the build leaves, takes a resource's income below nothing or lower while it is.
- In: the "less given" gambit of the builder reads the income through the same reading as the guard, so a stock at nothing no longer reads an income of nothing where it is below it.
- In, the reconcile's choice: the screen's reckoning and the guard's reading become one reading of what a run of stages took of each stock, past nothing included, the reckoning reading it over income and strikes, the guard over income alone; the stages read are the parameter.
- Out: the deal, the settle, the hazard, the unit cards, the walks that weigh no play, the attacks and the end of turn carry no guard: forced, or moving no income. Out: any gambit that covers an uncovered upkeep actively — the line is the guard; thin is answered after play. Out: the idle persona, which places nothing.
- Corner cases decided: an enemy occupying a worked tile makes it yield nothing while its building's upkeep stays, which is why the guard compares after against before and not against nothing alone; a tile the fallen neutral built on brings its building's upkeep in with the claim, which is why the claim carries the guard; the goal's play would build for the count on a city with no production, which is why the goal gambits carry it.
