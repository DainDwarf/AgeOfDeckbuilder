# The painted cave

**Scope:**

In: the site as a declaration of the age's — its building and its rewards, by id — and the region naming which sites it deals and how far they keep, from the centre and from one another, in two variables of the sites' own; the generator placing the sites after the camps, one each, on the ground the site's building names, among the tiles the age's camps are reached on (over the water too where the age's camps stand across it), each at least the region's distance from the centre and from every site placed before, a camp beside it allowed and a camp's tile never; a map short of its sites redealt as one short of its camps is; the capture of a site by a unit of the player's through the same door as a camp's; the site's rewards dealt in the capture's window under its name and lore; the pillage and the pillager leaving a site alone; the cave in both ages with its cards, text, lore, mark and colour; the fixture's site and the mechanism's tests; the proof spec. The mechanism lands in its own inert commit ahead of the content, with a fixture site reaching it and no real content.

Out: the neutral taking sites, the other two sites, the Nomadic city's culture, any number's tuning beyond the provisional ones here.

Corner cases decided:

- Only a unit of the player's captures; standing is enough, no prepare. Captures resolve in tile order with the camps', each its own group, each deal behind the deals standing.
- An enemy may enter on, walk over or stand on a site's tile; nothing comes of it, and while one stands there no unit of the player's can.
- A site's tile claimed takes one population and yields its terrain; the site's building yields nothing; a building card is refused there while the slot is filled, by the standing rule. A unit standing on a claimed site captures it as on any.
- A terraform of the player's on a site's tile is refused for nothing the site adds; the standing layer rule removes the site with a terrain its building does not name, and nothing is dealt. No card of either age terraforms today, so no spec reaches this; no mechanism is added for it.
- A pillage removes nothing of a site, as it removes nothing of a camp. The pillager does not go for a site for the site's sake; a worker of the player's standing on one is sought as any worker is.
- The site's building names hills alone, which no event of either age terraforms, so no event removes a site.
- The site's keep has no floor of the camps' kind: the catalogue refuses a region whose camps stand within the first steps, and asks nothing of where its sites stand.
- Saves written before this line carry no site and read as they did; a save written mid-deal before the deal's shape changes is not read, and needs no care until the Bronze Age.

Provisional numbers, tuning: both ages' regions keep their sites 7 from the centre and 4 from one another, the camps' numbers under the sites' own names. Measured on 100 seeds per region with the real generator, sites on hills placed after the camps: no map short on either temperate region; 8 of 100 short on the archipelago, which redeals; mean distance from the centre 8.7 to 9.7.

Reconcile, chosen:

- The camps' placing places the sites too, the two become one: what is placed (a count of one building for the camps, a list of buildings one each for the sites), what it may stand on, what it keeps from (camps from camps, sites from sites) and how far are its parameters; the reach is the age's camps' for both.
- The camp's capture and the site's are one door: a filled slot a unit of the player's stands on through the enemy phase is emptied and its rewards dealt; the camp's capture also frees its enemies of their camp, and that is the camp's difference. The group is named for the capture alone, `capture` carrying its tile, in place of the camp's name; it is a closed set, so every switch over the stage names follows.
- The deal is one shape for any capture, carrying the building captured and its rewards; the window heads with that building's name and reads a lore keyed on it, the camp's entry moving under that key.
- Rewards go through the standing door as it is: card ids, each made at its start and added to the discard pile when chosen.
- The reads of "a slot nobody's" stay apart from the city's side and join on the enemies': the claim and the terraform treat a site's tile as any tile and keep refusing a camp's; the pillage and the pillager spare a camp and a site alike.
