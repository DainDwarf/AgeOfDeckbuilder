Done when the catalogue holds no city record, a deck holds a city section naming its building, its sight, its idle population and its card, the chronicle carries the city section it was launched on and the save reads it, the settle puts the city section's city down and sight reads the city section's number, the catalogue refuses a region whose camps keep within the centre part's reach plus two, `npm run check`, `npm run lint` and `npm test` are green, the searches under _Verify_ find nothing, and CI is green.

- **The city section** is the deck's third: the city's building, by id; its sight; the idle population the chronicle opens with besides the one on the city's tile; and its card, by id. The Nomadic deck's city section holds the catalogue's city record as it stands today, building `city`, sight 2, idle 0, and its card is the Nomadic `settle` card, the one tile-aim that settles the city section's city. The catalogue's `city` record goes, and the merge takes the version and the slices alone.
- **The chronicle carries the city section** it was launched on, copied at the opening as the deck's cards are copied into its piles, and never changed after; the chronicle's `city` field stays the coordinates the city stands on and nothing more. The opening's hand is the city section's card first, then the settle section in the deck's order.
- **The settle** puts the city section's city: the card's effect settles through the rules' helper, which reads the section the chronicle carries — the building in the tile's slot, the tile held alone with one population on it, the section's idle besides, the chronicle's `city` set to the tile. The card's refusal reads the section's building's terrains off the chronicle it is asked on. No rule reads the section to decide the play; the card's closure calls the helper.
- **The standing city** reads its sight off the section the chronicle carries; every read of the chronicle's `city` as its tile stays a read of its coordinates. The terraform of the city's tile reads the terrains off the building standing in the tile's slot, no longer off a catalogue record.
- **The save** reads the chronicle's city section — its building as a building id the catalogue holds, its sight and its idle as integers, its card as a card id the catalogue holds; a save whose chronicle carries no city section is refused, dropped, the reason on the console — the third and last time this rung drops the chronicle in progress, and the hand-back says so. The save's own `deck` id stays as it is. No version bump.
- **Validation**, in the one place the catalogue is validated: every deck's city section names a building the catalogue holds, a sight and an idle at or above nought, and a card of the settle kind; the settle section keeps holding settle cards alone and at least one, as today; every age's regions keep their camps beyond the centre part's reach plus two, the two a constant of the rule, tuning. The three checks that exist today on the city record — the building held, the sight and the idle at or above nought, the camps beyond reach — keep their tests on the fixture, now on the fixture's deck's city section and on the margin; the card-of-the-settle-kind check gets one test. A second copy of the city's card in the settle section is not refused: a second settle is refused by the card's own reasons on a chronicle where the city stands, as today.
- **The fixture**: its deck's city section holds the city its record holds today, building `PH_City`, sight 2, idle 2, and its card `PH_Settle`; `PH_Settle` leaves the settle section, and every test deck naming `PH_Settle` in its settle section names it as the city section's card instead, or keeps a copy there where the test plays a second settle on purpose; the chronicles the fixture builds directly carry that section; the sight test that today narrows the catalogue's city to a sight of one narrows the carried section instead, and needs no catalogue of its own for it.
