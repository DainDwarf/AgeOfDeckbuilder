# The city is the settle card's

**Line:** The city is the settle card's — the catalogue's city record moves into the settle card that settles the city, its building, its sight and the population it opens with the card's content, and the standing city keeps what the rules read of it. Done when the catalogue holds no city record, a settle card declares its city as data and its effect settles that city, the chronicle's city carries its sight and the save reads it, the catalogue refuses a region whose camps keep within reach of any declared city and a deck whose settle section declares no city or two, `npm run check`, `npm run lint` and `npm test` are green, the searches under _Verify_ find nothing, and CI is green.

**Spec:** `docs/DESIGN.md` → _Launching a chronicle_, the civilization: _the settle card that settles the city is the civilization's, fixed in its settle section, and what the city is — its building, how far it sees, the population it opens with — is that card's content_ — this line lands the second half; the civilization and the fixing are later lines'. `docs/CHRONICLE.md` → _Cards_, the settle kind, one sentence changes: _The one that **settles** the city is aimed at a charted tile whose terrain takes a city and puts the city there, its building in the tile's slot, held alone with one population on it and the city's idle count besides; what it refuses beyond the charting is content, and it leaves the terrain as it stands._ becomes _The one that **settles** the city is aimed at a charted tile whose terrain takes a city and puts the city the card declares there, its building in the tile's slot, held alone with one population on it and the idle population the card names besides, and the standing city carries the sight the card gave it from then on; what it refuses beyond the charting is content, and it leaves the terrain as it stands._ `docs/CHRONICLE.md` → _Sight_: _the city has a sight of its own, a number on its content_, unchanged, the content now being the card's. `docs/MAP.md` → _The generator_, the camps, one clause changes: _the catalogue refuses a region whose camps keep no further from the centre than the centre part reaches plus what the city sees_ becomes _the catalogue refuses a region whose camps keep no further from the centre than the centre part reaches plus what any city a settle card declares sees_. `docs/META.md` → _The collection and the deck_: _the one that settles the city fixed in it and never moved out_, unchanged, a later line's, which reads the declaration this line adds. `docs/ages/NOMADIC.md` → _The settle_, unchanged: the building's yields are the building's, and the page names no number. `DOGMAS.md` → _Architecture_, **Data owns its behaviour**: the card's effect reads the card's own declaration, and no rule reads it to decide what a play does; the catalogue's validation and, later, the collection screen read it as they read a card's counters. Player-facing sentences: none; the settle card's rules entry names the city's building today and keeps doing so.

**Doc-impact:** `docs/CHRONICLE.md` → _Cards_, the settle kind; `docs/MAP.md` → _The generator_, the camps; the two sentences above.

**Scope:**

- In: `src/rules/catalogue.ts` for the card's declaration, the city record's removal and the validation; `src/rules/state.ts` for the chronicle's city; `src/rules/cards.ts` for the settle and the terraform of the city's tile; `src/rules/sight.ts`; `src/rules/save.ts`; the merge as the line before left it; `src/rules/fixtures.ts` and the rules tests that read the catalogue's city; `src/content/nomadic.ts` and the catalogue's module; `e2e/settle.spec.ts` and `e2e/reference.spec.ts`, which read the city's building off the catalogue; the two `docs/` sentences. Out: the civilization and the collection screen; the settle card's face, which shows nothing more; the city's yields, which stay the building kind's; `CHANGELOG.md`.
- **The declaration**: a settle card may declare a **city** — its building, by id; its sight; the idle population the chronicle opens with besides the one on the city's tile — as data beside its aim, the way a card declares its counters. A settle card that declares none enters a unit or does whatever else its effect does, as today. The Nomadic settle card declares the city the catalogue's record holds today, unchanged in every number; the catalogue's `city` record goes, and the merge takes the version and the slices alone.
- **The settle** puts the city the card declares: the card's effect settles through the rules' helper with its own declaration, the building in the tile's slot, the tile held alone with one population on it, the declared idle besides, and the chronicle's **city** set to the tile and the declared sight. No rule reads the declaration to decide the play; the card's closure hands it in.
- **The standing city** is the chronicle's city record: the tile it stands on and its sight, set once at the settle and never changed after, saved with the chronicle. Sight reads it from there. The terraform of the city's tile reads the terrains the city's building stands on off the building standing in the tile's slot, no longer off a catalogue record. Every other read of the city record as its tile — the held change, the enemies' reads, the screen's — stays a read of its coordinates.
- **The save** reads the chronicle's city as its coordinates and its sight, an integer; a save whose city lacks its sight is refused, dropped, the reason on the console — the third and last time this rung drops the chronicle in progress, and the hand-back says so. No version bump.
- **Validation**, in the one place the catalogue is validated: every declared city's building is a building the catalogue holds, its sight and its idle at or above nought; every age's regions keep their camps beyond the centre part's reach plus what any declared city sees; every deck's settle section holds exactly one card declaring a city — none, and the settle phase could never end; two, and the second settle would stand a second city. The three checks that exist today on the city record — the building held, the sight and the idle at or above nought, the camps beyond reach — keep their tests on the fixture, now on the fixture's settle card; the exactly-one check gets one test.
- **The fixture**: its settle card declares the city its record holds today, in its numbers; its chronicles' city record carries that sight; the sight test that today narrows the catalogue's city to a sight of one narrows the chronicle's city record instead, and needs no catalogue of its own for it.

**Traps:**

- The settle card's refusal reads the terrains of the building it will place through the catalogue's city record today (`src/content/nomadic.ts`, the settle card's `refuses`); it reads its own declaration now.
- The terraform of the city's tile (`src/rules/cards.ts`, the read of the city's building's terrains) fires with the city standing, so the building is in the tile's slot; the tile's building is what it reads.
- The camp-distance check loops over every age's regions since the line before this one, and now over every declared city besides; the fixture has one of each, and the test that narrows the sight to reach the camps sets the fixture's settle card's sight.
- The rules tests match `/^fixture: /` on every refusal, and the coherence test in `src/content/` plays every settle card of every deck on a launched chronicle already; a settle card declaring a city on a chronicle where one stands is refused by its own reasons, as today.
- `e2e/reference.spec.ts` reads the building the settle card names off the catalogue's city record and `e2e/settle.spec.ts` asserts the building in the tile's slot against it; both read the first settle card's declaration now, through the catalogue's card lookup on the deck's settle section.
