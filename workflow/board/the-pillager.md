# The pillager

**Line:** **The pillager** — an enemy's script may prepare on the tile it stands on, spending its action as an attack does, the tile wearing the enemy's colour and the unit's card saying what is coming; at the start of the next enemy phase, the enemy still standing there, the prepared act lands first, the city's tile captured and any other tile pillaged, everything the player built on it removed at once, and the city's capture goes through the prepare; the Stone camp names a third script, the pillager, that goes for the nearest of the player's workers and of what they built, attacks what it can on its way, prepares the pillage where it stands on something built, and raids with nothing to go for; each held by a test on the fixture, the pillager standing in the Stone content with the coherence test passing and the age page saying so, and the screen proving the hue and the card's line. **Scope:**

In:

- The prepare, a rule of the enemies: a script answers its act with an attack, a prepare or nothing; the prepare spends the action as an attack does, is carried on the unit through the player's turn and the end of turn's refresh, and is saved with it. At the next enemy phase's start, before neutrals and before any enemy acts, every enemy still carrying a prepare resolves it in unit order, the city's capture first since the fall ends everything: on the city's tile the city falls by capture; on any other tile every improvement and the building the player built go at once, and nothing where nothing is left. The prepare is gone with the resolution, or with the enemy's death. A prepare on a tile with nothing to take, neither the city nor anything built, and a prepare by an embarked enemy, are each a `runtime-error`.
- The city's capture goes through the prepare: the positional read at the phase's start goes, the raider prepares the capture on the city's tile where it attacked nothing, and a guard with no camp raids and so prepares there too.
- The pillager script in the Stone content, the Stone camp naming it as its third script, the Nomadic camp naming none: a camp names the scripts it has, and an entry asking for one it lacks is refused by the catalogue where the content names it and a `runtime-error` where the chronicle asks. The pillager's destination is the nearest, by its own walk from where it stands, of the tiles a worker of the player's stands on and of the tiles carrying a building or an improvement, the city's tile and a camp's never, and only tiles it stands on ashore; ties in tile order. On its way it attacks the weakest it can reach in its own sight, as the raider does; standing on a built tile with no target it prepares the pillage; a built tile it crosses on the way to a nearer target it does not prepare on. With no destination at all it walks to the city as a raider and prepares the capture there. Nothing in the game enters a pillager until the next line; the fixture test and the proof spec plant their own.
- The screen: a prepared tile wears the enemy colour at the target glow's values, fill 0.4 and outline 0.9, under a look role of its own; the unit card's head reads the kind and a line under the name reads "Raft", "Pillaging" or "Capturing", joined where two apply.
- Tests on the fixture: a prepare spends the action and rides on the unit through the end of turn; it resolves at the next phase's start as a capture on the city's tile and as a pillage elsewhere, the building and every improvement gone at once; a prepared enemy killed in the player's turn takes nothing; a prepare where nothing is to take, and one by an embarked enemy, are each a `runtime-error`; the capture tests keep their shape through the fixture scripts preparing on the city's tile; the save carries the prepare. The content's pillager gets its walk tests beside the raider's: the nearest target by walk, attack before prepare, the raid with nothing to go for.
- The content's checkable state: the pillager script in the Stone catalogue, named by the Stone camp, the coherence test passing, the age page's sentence.

Out:

- Who enters pillagers: the next line, **Camps that prepare**, whose title is reworded at its intake since prepare is now the enemy's word.
- The camp's capture by a unit of the player's: stays read off the unit standing there, shown by nothing.
- A pillage landing the phase the enemy arrives: rejected, recorded in the spec.
- An embarked pillage, so the fishery: out, an embarked enemy prepares nothing; a coast tile is never a pillager's destination.
- Pillage on arrival before attacking: rejected; the pillager attacks first.
- The console entry that enters a unit: its own line.

- The raider's walk to the city becomes one walk with its destination as parameter, the pillager walking it to its target and, with none, to the city; the two become one in this line. Whom it attacks goes through the raider's choice as it is.
