# The borders where the two cities touch

**Line:** **The borders where the two cities touch** — a city's line stands half its width inside each edge of the ground held, so where the two cities' tiles meet the two lines run side by side a full width apart and neither hides the other, the claim marks' ghost moved with them; the neutral spec reads the two lines no nearer than a width on a chronicle where the borders touch. Doc-impact: `docs/LOOK.md`.

**Spec:** `docs/LOOK.md`, _The map_, the _The borders_ paragraph, its first sentence changed and one sentence added after it, verbatim:

> **The borders.** A city's border is one line around the tiles it holds, in the city's colour, 1.5 units wide, running along each edge of the ground held, half its width inside the edge, and rounding every corner on its way, and a band of that colour inside the line, 60% at the line and fading bell-shaped over 7 units, a blur and not a gradient, so it has no inner edge. Where the two cities' tiles meet, the two lines stand side by side along their shared edges and round their shared corners a full width apart, each on its own city's side, so neither hides the other. A tile in fog keeps the stretch of border it wore when last seen, darkened by the fog as everything of it is, so the line runs on into the fog in the fog's dark. A city's own tile wears nothing more of it than the rest.

_The claim marks_ changes nothing: it calls the ghost the border's own line and band, which now carries the rule, and its meeting sentence — the ghost's line running on to the middle of a held tile's edge, where the border's line passes over it — stays true, the ghost's end lying under the moved border. `docs/CHRONICLE-SCREEN.md` changes nothing: its marks paragraph says which colour each border wears and that it is drawn from each tile as the map draws it, both untouched. **Traps:**

The map's lattice is squashed, rows three quarters of a tile's width apart (`docs/LOOK.md` _The terrain_, `cornerAt` in `src/ui/map-marks.ts`), so the direction from a point on an edge to its tile's centre is not perpendicular to the edge. The stretch moves along its own curve's normal, by 0.75, to the side its tile lies on; the tile only picks the side. Every stretch `src/ui/border-loops.ts` hands out carries its tile, and a corner's curve is two halves each with its own tile, split at the curve's point nearest the corner; both halves share the tangent at the split and consecutive roundings share the edge's direction at the edge's middle, so a move along the normal leaves the line continuous.
- A Graphics has no curve of its own: the border's curved stretch is a `Phaser.Curves.QuadraticBezier` sampled at even steps of its parameter and stroked as a polyline, 8 points per stretch today (`docs/PHASER.md`, _Rendering under WebGL_). Phaser is read, never remembered.
