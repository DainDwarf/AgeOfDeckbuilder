# The borders

**Line:** The borders — each city's border is one curved line around the tiles it holds with a band of its colour fading inside it, darkened where a tile is in fog, the ring inside every held tile gone, and the neutral spec reads it. Doc-impact: `docs/LOOK.md`, `docs/CHRONICLE-SCREEN.md`, `docs/PHASER.md`.

**Scope:**

- In: the per-tile border rings go — the `ring` each tile keeps, `ringMark`'s use for them, `RING` and `CITY_RING` — and with them the heavier ring on a city's own tile. The claimable ring in city mode keeps `ringMark` with its weight of 2 inline; it is the claim marks line's.
- In: the loop. For each holder, the edges of the tiles the map draws as held by that holder — a tile drawn live by the chronicle's holder, a tile in fog by its snapshot's, as `paintBorder` reads them today — whose neighbour the map does not draw as held by the same holder, each oriented the same way round its tile, chained at their corners into closed loops; a hole of unheld ground inside a border is a loop of its own. The lattice corners are the rules' (`cornersOf`, `cornerKey` in `src/rules/map.ts`) and stand on the surface where `cornerAt` puts them, as the rivers' do. The line runs through the middle of each edge and takes each corner as the control point of a quadratic curve to the next middle; the mockup's `trace` in https://claude.ai/artifact/1N9X2ThYMan8sCB1nRKgY3 is the geometry, settled on the user's picks: the line 1.5 wide, the band 10 deep, 60% at the line, no dark edge under the line.
- In: the band is the colour inside the loop only, strongest at the line and gone 10 units in, continuous round every corner, a concave corner included; it is the loop stroked inward, never a trapezoid per edge, so no corner shows a notch. The mockup draws it as fourteen strokes of the loop, each narrower and each adding a little, clipped to the loop's inside and to the holder's tiles.
- In: the fog. The stretch of the loop along a fogged tile's edges, band and line, is the holder's colour multiplied by the fog's tint (`darkened`, `multiplied`), the stretch along a live tile's edges the plain colour; the stretch splits at the corners and at the middles, each half edge belonging to the tile whose edge it is.
- In: the city's own tile wears no ring of its own any more; its building and its badge are what mark it, as before.
- In: the neutral's border drawn the same way in the neutral's colour; a tile the neutral holds and the player's city holds nothing of stands outside the player's loop as today.
- Decided here, the drawing: the band is painted on a canvas texture, as `drawSheet` in `src/ui/sheet.ts` paints the sheets at boot, since a Graphics can stroke a polyline but cannot clip to the loop's inside, and a mask is a filter with its own costs (`docs/PHASER.md`); the texture is one square of a power of two covering the disc at one pixel to the unit, so it mipmaps when the map zooms below 1 (the camera goes from 0.75 to 3.5), and a fade magnified at 3.5 is soft without harm. The line is a Graphics per holder, so it stays crisp at every zoom: the curve sampled into points and stroked, the stroke's colour set per stretch. Both stand in the `border` group of the terrain stratum, where the rings stood, so they draw over every face and under the lit tiles. The texture is redrawn, and refreshed, only when what it draws changed — the held tiles of either holder, or which of their edges' tiles are drawn live or in fog — never on every render: a refresh uploads the whole texture.
- Decided here: nothing moves. A claim redraws the border at the render after the change has played, as the rings appeared; motion is the Motion rung's.
- Out: the selection ring, the lit tiles, the glows, city mode's marks, the badge, every other line of the cut. The veils keep their meaning: with the fog veil off nothing is darkened; with the uncharted veil off every tile is drawn, so a border runs along every held tile.
- Reconcile, chosen: the loop's corners go through the rules' corner functions and `cornerAt`, shared with the rivers (the projected goes through the standing door); the edge walk stays apart from the shore's (`shoreOn` reads which edges meet land per tile for a painted overlay, the loop reads which edges change holder and chains them, a difference that is meant); the canvas texture goes through `createCanvas` as the sheets do, but its own drawing, since a sheet composes files and this paints; the fog's darkening goes through `darkened` as every mark's does.

**Traps:**

- A Graphics has `lineStyle(width, colour, alpha)`, `strokePoints`, `moveTo`, `lineTo` and `strokePath`, no quadratic curve and no clip (`node_modules/phaser/src/gameobjects/graphics/Graphics.js:301`, `:1118`); a curve is drawn through `Phaser.Curves.QuadraticBezier`, whose `draw(graphics, pointsTotal)` samples it into a polyline (`src/curves/QuadraticBezierCurve.js:150`), or its `getPoints`. Masks under WebGL are filters (`docs/PHASER.md`, _Rendering under WebGL_).
- A Shape carries no tint; an Image does (`docs/PHASER.md`). The band's Image is drawn in colour on the canvas, each stretch in its own colour, so it is tinted by nothing; the fog veil's switch redraws it.
- `CanvasTexture.refresh()` uploads the canvas; the texture mipmaps only at a power-of-two size, and every upload regenerates the levels (`docs/PHASER.md`, the mipmap entry). A texture the size of the disc is redrawn on a change of the border's inputs alone; `render` runs on every command and must not redraw it each time.
- The read of the corner order: `cornersOf` lists a tile's corners in the order of `ARMS` (`src/rules/map.ts:428`), and `neighbours` walks the six directions in its own order; which two corners bound the edge toward each neighbour is read off those two lists once, never assumed from the mockup's canvas order, whose `EDGE_CORNERS` is for the page's own hexagon.
- The lattice is squashed: a corner's surface position comes from `cornerAt` (`src/ui/map-marks.ts`), which applies `SQUASH` to y; the curve's control points are surface positions, so the curve is computed after the squash, never before.
- The e2e readings. `e2e/settle.spec.ts:55` and `:87` count the `border` group's shown children against 0 and the held count; `e2e/neutral.spec.ts:208-244` counts the rings' `strokeColor` per holder and per fog state. With the rings gone both read the new objects: the border standing for a holder once its city stands, and the colours of the line's stretches per holder with the fog's darkening. `DOGMAS.md` _Testing_: a reading the specs cannot make is a helper in `e2e/`, never an object kept, named or shaped for it. A Graphics keeps every command it was given in its `commandBuffer`, a `lineStyle` among them with its colour (`node_modules/phaser/src/gameobjects/graphics/Graphics.js:141`, `:305`), which is what a reading of the line's colours per stretch reads. `ringedTile` reads the selection ring, named `selected`, and is untouched.
- `paintBorder` runs inside `render` after `paintRivers`, and `tileObjects` is built once at the map's creation with a `ring` per tile: the ring's slot goes, and `paintedOnTiles` does not list it.
- `holderOf` and the snapshots: a fogged tile's holder is `charted.get(key)?.holder`, as `paintBorder` reads it today, and a drawn tile with no snapshot and not live is drawn as it stands (`faceIn`); the loop's edge test reads the neighbour the same way, so a neighbour the map does not draw at all counts as held by nobody and the loop runs along it.
- The disc's edge: a held tile on the rim has neighbours off the map; `tileAt` answers nothing for them and the edge is a border edge.
- `darkened` and `multiplied` live in `src/ui/map-marks.ts` and `src/ui/look.ts`; the fog's tint is `LOOK.fog`.
