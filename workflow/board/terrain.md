# The terrain

**Line:** **The terrain** — every tile the map draws wears David Baumgart's painted face of its terrain on his lattice, the Hex Basic Terrain Set and the Hex Rivers, Coasts & Seas pack in the store with their licence record, every hexagon the map draws squashed with the lattice. Doc-impact: `docs/LOOK.md`, `docs/PHASER.md`.

**Spec:** `docs/LOOK.md` _The map_, the terrain paragraph, is the spec as it stands: his hexes painted for rows three quarters of a tile's width apart, the map's rows that much closer and every hexagon it draws squashed by the same amount, the rules' geometry untouched; his art rising above its hex into the row behind, a tile's face drawn behind the row in front of it. Add to that paragraph, after its last sentence: "A terrain with several paintings spreads them over its tiles, each tile's painting picked by its place on the disc, so a face never changes under the player's eye and one seed always deals one picture." `docs/LOOK.md` _Near and far_ stays as it is: this line paints every zoom, and the far view line brings the threshold and the flat colours. `docs/PHASER.md` gains one entry per fact below under _Traps_ that the implementer verifies in the pinned package, each cited by file and line as the page's entries are: a scene with files queued runs `create` only after they load, so a scene started after it finds nothing of it until then; Phaser makes mipmaps for a texture only where `render.mipmapFilter` is set and the texture's sides are powers of two; a texture missing from the manager draws the missing texture and warns, and never throws, so the loader's own error is what a boot reads. No player-facing text: the line adds no word.

**Doc-impact:** `docs/LOOK.md` (the one sentence above), `docs/PHASER.md` (the loader and texture entries). The licence record `public/assets/LICENSES.md` gets two entries and loses one, below.

- The faces, by terrain, four paintings each, the file names his: plain `hexPlains00`–`03`, forest `hexForestBroadleaf00`–`03`, hills `hexHighlands00`–`03`, mountain `hexMountain00`–`03`, desert `hexDesertDunes00`–`03`, all in the basic set's `Tiles/`; coast `hexLake00`–`03` and ocean `hexOceanCalm00`–`03`, both in the rivers pack's `Tiles/`. Twenty-eight files. The table terrain → family and count is a UI constant beside the other marks; a terrain the content holds with no family is refused as a terrain with no colour is today, and the catalogue's coherence test reads every terrain through it, as it read the colour.
- Which of a terrain's paintings a tile wears is drawn from the tile's coordinates alone, by a hash in the UI, deterministic: no generator, no state. A tile keeps its painting through every render, and two seeds dealing one terrain on one tile show one painting.
- The lattice squash: the row pitch becomes three quarters of a tile's width, 1.5 × TILE_SIZE × (0.75 / (√3 / 2)); every hexagon the map draws, and the corners the rim and the rivers are stroked between, take the same vertical squash. Only the map's: the card back's emblem and the launch screen's hexagon stay regular. His 256 × 384 file is drawn at one scale on both axes, (√3 × TILE_SIZE × 1.04) / 256, its origin at the face's centre, 256 of 384 down the image; a hair wider than the cell, so no seam of the page shows between neighbours (4 % measured on the pitch; the implementer may re-measure). The face loses its one-unit outline: his edges are painted.
- Row order: within the ground layer a face's depth is its row, so a tile's art rising above its hex is drawn over the row behind and under the row in front. Every other mark, the rings, the glows, the scrims, the features and buildings, the units, stands in the layers and strata it stands in today, over the whole ground.
- Loading: the game's textures load through Phaser's loader before the first screen starts, from a scene that loads and then starts what the boot starts today; a texture that fails to load fails the boot as a font that fails to load does. The faces the game names, and only those, are then drawn into one texture sheet, a canvas texture whose sides are powers of two (2048 × 2048 holds forty of his cells, the twenty-eight here among them), each face one named frame of it, and the game config sets `render.mipmapFilter` so the sheet is minified through mipmaps: at the opening zoom a face is drawn about six times smaller than painted, which shimmers on a pan without them. One sheet is also one texture for the batch, which the boot pins to one texture at a time. The sheet is built by a helper that takes a list of files and hands back the sheet's key and its frames, so the glyph rung and the pieces rung can build theirs; no family abstraction beyond that helper.
- The infopanel's terrain card, which draws the terrain through the map's own face maker, shows the painted face fitted whole into its head box, overhang included; the cards rung redoes it.
- Every spec that walks the map as it stands keeps passing: a tile is still found by its name, the lattice axes are still measured from three tiles, and no spec reads a map tile's fill.

Corner cases decided:

- The press hit test, nearest centre within a tile's radius, is measured in the unsquashed space, the pointer's y divided by the squash, so a press near a diagonal edge lands on the tile whose drawn face it is inside; measured in the squashed space the cells differ from the drawn hexagons by about two units at the corners.

Reconcile: nothing found. The font loads before the game exists through the FontFace API and the textures through Phaser's loader once it does; both fail the boot, and that is the shared rule, not a shared door. The infopanel's terrain mark already comes through the map's face maker.

**Traps:**

- The scratch branch `Baumgart`, commit 348ff7c, carries a working throwaway of the squash, the row depth and the face as an Image (`src/ui/ground.ts`, the diff on `src/ui/map-marks.ts` and `src/ui/map.ts`), read for reference and never merged or cherry-picked: it predates the bundled font and the type floor.
- `asPainted` (`src/ui/pool.ts`) resets scale to one: an Image sized by scale is sized after a paint takes it, or it comes out at the file's 256 pixels.
- `fitMark` in `src/ui/infopanel.ts` reads `getBounds`, which for an Image is the whole footprint, overhang included.
- Phaser makes mipmaps only for power-of-two textures and only when `render.mipmapFilter` is set (`node_modules/phaser/src/renderer/webgl/wrappers/WebGLTextureWrapper.js:324`); his files are 256 × 384, so the sheet is what gets them. A canvas texture's `refresh` goes through the wrapper's update, which regenerates where the texture is mipmapped; verify before relying on it.
- Phaser 4 textures are Y-up and `setTintFill` is gone: the migration page, `node_modules/phaser/skills/v3-to-v4-migration/SKILL.md`, before frames are added to a canvas texture by hand.
- A scene whose `preload` queues files runs `create` after the load; the chronicle screen's `create` reaches into the map scene's strata, which is why the load belongs to a scene started before any screen and not to the map scene (`docs/PHASER.md` _Scenes and stacking_, "Every scene-plugin call is queued").
- The boot pins the batch to one texture unit (`src/main.ts`, `setMaxParallelTextureUnits(1)`); do not touch it: it is the rotated-text tear's workaround, and one sheet keeps the ground in one batch under it.
- A hidden object is neither drawn nor hit, and a Layer sorts by depth then add order (`docs/PHASER.md`): faces are given their row depth once, at their making, and pooled crossfade faces as they are taken.
- `e2e/chronicle-screen.ts` finds a named object through Layers and Containers and reads an Image's bounds through `getBounds` as it reads a Polygon's; `tileOnScreen` measures the lattice from three tiles, so the squash passes through it.
- `docs/PHASER.md` is cited, not restated, for the input and render facts a spec meets: a spec rests before it reads what a camera move changed.
- The store is a submodule: after the packs are committed and pushed there, the pointer on `main` moves in the ship's commit, and CI fetches the commit the pointer names.
