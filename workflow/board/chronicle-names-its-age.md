# The chronicle names its age

**Line:** The chronicle names its age — the catalogue gains its ages, each owning its schedule, its camp and its regions; the Nomadic module becomes the first age's slice, merged with an id two ages name refused; a chronicle names its age, the save carries it, and the schedule row becomes the age row with `age=` replacing `schedule=`. Done when the catalogue holds an `ages` table and no `schedules` table, the timeline names no schedule, a chronicle and its save name an age, the merge has its one test on fixture slices, the launch page's rows are age, region, deck and seed, `npm run check`, `npm run lint` and `npm test` are green, the searches under _Verify_ find nothing, and CI is green.

**Spec:** `DOGMAS.md` → _Architecture_, the rule **The catalogue is an argument**, its sentences _There is one catalogue, and an age is a slice of it in two halves: what the age owns — its schedule, its camp, its regions — and what it brings to the tables every age shares, so a deck holds cards of any age. The content is one module per age, merged into the catalogue as it is built, and an id two ages name is refused there; a chronicle names its age, and what the age owns is read through it._ — this line lands all of it. `DOGMAS.md` → _Testing_, the rule **A catalogue is coherence-tested, never trusted**: _asks every closure it carries its cheapest answer on a chronicle launched on each age_. `docs/CHRONICLE.md` → _The schedule_: _Each age has a schedule_, and the timeline is _one chronicle's roll of its schedule_, unchanged. `docs/MAP.md` → _The region_: _A region is an age's: each age has regions of its own, and what a region deals is that age's content_, unchanged. `docs/ages/NOMADIC.md` → _The events_, _The capstone_ and _The camps_, unchanged: they are what the Nomadic age owns. `docs/META.md` → _The save_, unchanged: a save the boot cannot read whole is refused and dropped, the reason on the console. `docs/INTERFACE.md` → _The launch page_, one sentence changes, as the line before this one leaves it: _The page is one row per choice — the region, the schedule, the deck and the seed — the first of each list chosen until another is pressed_ becomes _The page is one row per choice — the age, the region, the deck and the seed — the first of each list chosen until another is pressed_. Player-facing sentences: one text-table entry, the launch page's row label, `launch.age`: _Age_; the `launch.schedule` entry, _Schedule_, goes with its row.

**Doc-impact:** `docs/INTERFACE.md` → _The launch page_, the one sentence above.

**Scope:**

- In: `src/rules/catalogue.ts` for the ages, the merge and the validation per age; `src/rules/map-kinds.ts` for what the map reads; `src/rules/state.ts` for the chronicle's age and the timeline; `src/rules/chronicle.ts`, `src/rules/schedule.ts`, `src/rules/enemies.ts`, `src/rules/city.ts`, `src/rules/cards.ts`, `src/rules/map.ts` where they read the camp, the regions or the schedule; `src/rules/save.ts`; `src/rules/fixtures.ts` and every rules test that launches or hands in a timeline; `src/content/nomadic.ts` as the first slice, `src/content/scripts.ts`, the catalogue's module, `src/content/nomadic.test.ts` becoming the catalogue's coherence test, `src/content/scripts.test.ts`; `src/main.ts`, `src/ui/launch-page.ts`, `src/ui/text.ts`, `src/ui/save-entry.ts`, `src/ui/chronicle-scene.ts`, `src/ui/overlay.ts`; `e2e/chronicle-screen.ts`, `e2e/boot.spec.ts`, `e2e/capstone.spec.ts`, `e2e/resume.spec.ts` and any spec that names the schedule; the one `docs/` sentence. Out: the city, which stays the catalogue's one record, read as today, until the next line moves it into the settle card; the launch page's layout; a tag on any entry saying its age; `CHANGELOG.md`.
- **An age** is one entry of the catalogue's `ages` table, keyed by its id, `nomadic` for the first, the table's order the ages' order in history and the order the launch lists them. It owns its **schedule** — the spacing, the capstone with its window, the entries — held outright, one per age; its **camp** — the unit, the guard's and the raider's scripts, the building, the rewards, the odds — and its **regions**, a table of its own. The `schedules` table goes: a schedule is the age's and has no id of its own. What an age brings — units, scripts, cards, decks, events, capstones, terrains, biomes, buildings, features, improvements — lands in the catalogue's tables, which every age shares.
- **A slice** is what an age's content module exports: what the age owns and what it brings, one field per table it brings to. **The merge** builds the catalogue from the version, the city and the slices in order: each table is the union of what the slices bring, the ages table is the slices' own halves under their ids, and an id two slices bring to one table is refused with the one rejection vocabulary, before the built catalogue is validated as it is today. The city is handed to the merge beside the slices, since it is neither an age's nor a table's.
- **The chronicle names its age** beside its content version; the age is set at the opening and never changes. The camp, the regions and the schedule are read through it: the guards placed on the camps at the opening, the camps' rolls, a raid's door, a camp placed by an answer, a capture's rewards, the tile a camp fills in the city's and the cards' reads, the raid-through-a-camp odds, the timeline's roll and its draws. The **timeline** names no schedule: it is rolled from the chronicle's age's schedule and carries, as today, its generator, the next due turn and the capstone by id and turn.
- **The save** carries the chronicle's age as a field of the chronicle, resolved through the ages table; its `region` beside the chronicle is resolved through the chronicle's age's regions; the `schedule` of a save's timeline is a field the shape no longer names, dropped as any such field. A save written before this line lacks the age and is refused by the reading, dropped, the reason on the console — the user's chronicle in progress goes with it, and the hand-back says so. No version bump: the shape refuses it by itself.
- **The map generator** reads the regions and the camp's building through the age: what it is handed, the age's own half or the age's id, is the implementer's; the trap on the map's content shape is below.
- **Validation per age**, in the one place the catalogue is validated: the schedule's entries at least one, each an event the catalogue holds dealing two answers or more, its capstone held, its spans well-formed; the camp's unit, scripts, building and rewards held, the rewards at least one, both odds within nought and one, the camp's unit standing on every terrain the camp's building names; every region's biomes and features held, its shares dealing biomes, its sized biomes within the disc, its camps kept beyond the centre part's reach plus what the city sees; at least one region; and no deck of the catalogue holding a reward of any age's camp. A catalogue holding no age is refused. Every check that exists today keeps its test on the fixture, now over the fixture's age; the merge's refusal and the no-age refusal get one test each.
- **The launch page** has four rows — age, region, deck, seed — and Launch. The age row lists the ages' ids in the table's order, the first chosen; pressing an age keeps the deck and chooses the first of that age's regions, since the regions are the age's; the region row lists the chosen age's regions. The faces are named `launch-<row>-<option>` as today, the age's `launch-age-<id>`.
- **The address** reads `age`, `region`, `deck` and `seed`; an age it names is resolved through the ages table, a region through the named or first age's regions; `schedule` is no longer a key it reads, so an address naming it alone is bare. An address naming what the content does not hold throws before Phaser, as today.
- **The coherence test** is the catalogue's, in `src/content/`: every id of every table through the screen's lookups, and every closure its cheapest answer on a chronicle launched and settled on each age of the catalogue, on that age's first region and the catalogue's first deck; the events and the capstone of each age's schedule on that age's chronicle; every card on every age's chronicle, since a deck holds cards of any age. The Nomadic test's checks carry over, looped over the ages table where they looped over the schedules.
- **The fixture** keeps every schedule it holds as an age of its own, each over the fixture's one camp and one regions table; a fixture chronicle's age agrees with the timeline it carries, where today the timeline carried the schedule on its own. How the fixture spreads the shared halves is the implementer's.
