# A staleness pass over the tests

**Line:** A staleness pass over the tests — the rules and content tests carry no fixture piece that no longer acts, both coherence tests ask every script of their catalogue its move and its attack on a settled launch, and `text()` refuses an unfilled placeholder so every spec that walks a placeholder surface proves its filling; `npm test` green. Doc-impact: none.

**Spec:** `DOGMAS.md` _Testing_ — "A catalogue is coherence-tested, never trusted … asks every closure it carries its cheapest answer on a launched chronicle" and "A fixture goes through the transform production uses" — and _Code_ — "Data coherence is never deferred … One rejection vocabulary across all validators". No sentence is added or changed; the line brings the tests up to the dogmas as written. No player-facing sentence: the refusal `text()` throws is a developer error, never shown.

**Doc-impact:** none — the tests' rules are `DOGMAS.md` lines that already say this, and no `docs/` page describes a test.

**Scope:**

Three parts, one commit.

_1. The fixture sweep._ In: pieces a fixture sets up that nothing in its test depends on — a unit placed to draw a script that no longer follows it, a stat set on a unit whose test never lets it act, a stock set where only the blocked list is read, a pile no step touches, camps placed for a raid a walk never takes, an export nothing imports. Each candidate below is removed and its file rerun; a removal that turns a test red means the piece acts, so it stays and the report names it under _Deviations_. Out, decided here: `NO_GROWTH` on a fixture whose food never reaches the threshold stays — it states what the test is not about, independent of the fixture's yields. A full draw pile stays everywhere: a turn that runs to its draw draws from it, and a turn that stops before the draw makes it the sentinel behind an asserted hand — `hand` equal to `[]` proves no draw ran only while there were cards to draw. A stat written out where it equals the fixture's default (`move: 2 * MOVE_POINT`, `action: 1`, `population` equal to the tiles held, `tiles: field(n)` covering nothing the test touches) is readability and stays. The camps at `schedule.test.ts` "a chronicle waiting on a deal takes no command but the take" and the third camp in `enemies.test.ts` "two camps captured the turn before an event is due" are the door a taken raid enters by: they act and stay.

The candidates, by file, each named by its test title, every one read at intake:

- `src/rules/fixtures.ts`: `QUIET` is exported and nothing outside the module imports it; the `export` goes, the constant stays.
- `src/rules/schedule.test.ts`: "the siege's own camps are reinforced as the camps standing are" — the `unkillable(CITY)` unit, the shape the `LURE` worker had (see `git show 44fea8e`): the siege's raiders walk for the city whatever stands on it, and from camps three or more tiles off none reaches it in the one turn the test ends; no assertion reads a unit of the player's. "the schedule keeps dealing past the landing of a capstone no span passes" — `camped(field(4), CAMPS)`: the walk takes the famine at every deal and the camp rolls no guard at odds of nought, so no raid ever enters and nothing reads the camps.
- `src/rules/chronicle.test.ts`: "a phase the turn always has is staged every turn, empty or not, and an empty hand discards nothing" — `discardPile: ['PH_Harvest']`: the full draw pile fills the hand without a shuffle, and no assertion reads either pile.
- `src/rules/cards.test.ts`: the `resources: FOOD` on "a unit card is refused for the population when only the city's last population is left", "… refused for the population and for the idle population names both", "… refused for the city while a unit of the player's stands on it", "… refused for the population and for the city names both": only `.blocked` is asserted, and a unit card's blocks read population, idle and units, never the stock. The `production(3)` on "a building card with nowhere to stand is playable all the same …": only admitted tiles, the refusal's blocks and `refusedFor` are read. The `tiles: field(2)` and the worker on "a card the city falls short for is refused for the resource it is short of": only `refusalOf` is read, and the test before it gets a building card's empty blocked list with no worker on the map.
- `src/rules/city.test.ts`: "a claim costs twice the tiles the city holds, its own counted" — `population: 40`: the claim's cost reads the tiles held and never the population, and a claim with no idle population still holds the tile.
- `src/rules/units.test.ts`: "the other faction stops a unit where it stands" — `damage: 0` on the player's unit: a move never attacks. "the same move on the same chronicle gives the same chronicle back" — `damage: 2, range: 1` on the player's unit and `health: 5` on the enemy: a move reads none of them.
- `src/content/scripts.test.ts`: "a raider with neither the city's tile nor a unit in reach moves toward the city by the cheapest way …" — `damage: 0` on the raider: no unit of the player's stands on that map, so nothing can be struck.

_2. The scripts._ In: one test in `src/content/nomadic.test.ts` and one in `src/content/stand-in.test.ts`, on the chronicle `settledLaunch` leaves on the catalogue's first schedule: for every id in the catalogue's `scripts`, the script's `moveTo` and `attacks` are asked for an enemy standing on the chronicle and neither throws. The enemy is any enemy unit standing — the settle opens with a guard on each camp the map was dealt — and a chronicle with none standing throws, since a coherence test that silently asks nothing is the shape this line removes. Out: any assertion on where the script moves or whom it strikes; the scripts' decisions have their tests beside the script.

_3. The placeholders._ In: `text()` refuses a placeholder its caller handed no value for — a `{name}` in the entry whose value is `undefined` — by throwing, in the vocabulary its neighbour `named` already uses for an entry no id names. One Vitest test beside it, `src/ui/text.test.ts` (a pure function on the UI side, as `console-line.test.ts` is), stating the rule on the table's own entries: a placeholder filled reads its value, a placeholder unfilled is refused, an entry with none reads as it stands. The two coherence tests' `UNFILLED` constant and every `.not.toContain(UNFILLED)` go: under the refusal the same proof is the reader called and not throwing, and the assertions are rewritten so, weakened nowhere. Out: a test per screen surface — every e2e spec that walks a surface holding a placeholder now proves its filling through the page-error watch, and any typing of the placeholders at compile time, which would be a second mechanism beside the one refusal and would not reach `named`'s dynamic keys.

**Traps:**

- The e2e specs read the screen and compare it to the same `text()` call (`refuse.spec.ts`, `press.spec.ts`, `recall.spec.ts`, `city-mode.spec.ts`, `inspect.spec.ts`, `deal.spec.ts`, `worker-instants.spec.ts`), so a `String(undefined)` on the screen passed on both sides; the `watch` in `e2e/chronicle-screen.ts` collects `pageerror`, which is what makes the throw a proof. Do not add screen assertions for it.
- Every `text()` call in `src/ui/` was read at intake and hands every value its entry names; the one guarded call is the panel's movement line, which branches to `panel.no-movement` where the cost is absent. A call found unfilled at the ship is a defect, reported under _Deviations_, not silenced.
- `named` builds its key from a prefix and an id and calls `text` with the values it was handed: the refusal must sit where both paths pass, so one throw serves the readers and the coherence tests alike.
- Removing an inert piece is not weakening a test: no assertion changes. A candidate whose removal turns a test red is not inert and stays, named in the report; the dossier's list was read, not run.
- `settledLaunch` may leave the chronicle waiting on a deal; asking a script is a read and needs no command, so that does not matter to part 2. The enemy asked is taken from `chronicle.units`, never entered by the test: the guards the settle opened with are the launched chronicle's own.
- `docs/PHASER.md` is not touched: nothing here reaches the screen.
