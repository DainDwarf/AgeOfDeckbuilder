# The fall at its sources

**Line:** The fall at its sources — the population fall is raised by the helper that takes a person when it takes the player's city's last, the removal fall by the helper that lays a tile over when the player's city's tile comes out without its building, and the recorder of a change records it alone; the victory walk reads no fall; the save reader refuses a chronicle whose city stands with no population or without its building; the rules tests pass with the same stage sequences; `docs/CHRONICLE.md` _The capstone_ no longer reads the condition "as the fall is read".

**Spec:** `docs/CHRONICLE.md` _Units and combat_, the _Defeat is capture_ bullet, unchanged: "Population reaching zero is another defeat, and so is the building of the player's city removed, whatever removes it: the player's city falls on the spot, and nothing of play resolves after it." `docs/CHRONICLE.md` _The capstone_, third paragraph, its first sentence loses the clause ", as the player's city's fall for its population is read" and becomes: "**A capstone names what passes it**: a condition of its own, read on the chronicle after the capstone's landing and after every change from then on; the chronicle ends in victory on the spot, on the first change after which the condition holds, nothing of play resolves after it, and it carries the ending as it carries a defeat." The rest of the paragraph stands. No player-facing sentence: the player sees the same `ended` right after the same change.

**Doc-impact:** `docs/CHRONICLE.md` _The capstone_, the one clause above.

**Scope:**

- In: `landedAs` records the one change it is handed and reads nothing. The population helper that takes one person raises the `ended` with cause `population` when the person taken is the player's city's last; the neutral's last is no fall. The tile helper that lays a tile over raises the `ended` with cause `removal` when the tile is the player's city's and comes out without the city section's building. Both go through the standing door the capture's fall goes through, `fall(chronicle, cause)` into an `ended` change, the capture's own raising in the enemy phase's prepares being the precedent. The victory walk in `chronicle.ts` drops its fall guard and the comment over it: a capstone condition holding on the change that takes the last person or the building wins over the fall, the user's call ("nothing currently falls into the cracks between event sequences; we'll see then"). The save reader refuses a chronicle whose city stands with no population, and one whose city's tile holds no city section's building, in its own rejection vocabulary beside its "has ended" refusal; a settle-phase save, no city and no population, stays accepted.
- Out: the capture's fall, already at its source; any change to what the screen plays; any performance measurement.
- Corner cases decided: the settle lays the city's building on the tile before the chronicle's city is set, so the retile of the settle raises nothing, and the removal test reads the chronicle the helper was handed. A fixture planted at zero population with a city standing falls on nothing once the line lands: the fall is raised by the take. The test "a city with no population left falls on the first change a command makes, whatever the command was" (`chronicle.test.ts`) promises a state no path reaches and is deleted, the user's decision; "a strike taking the city's last population falls on the strike, and no step of the end of turn follows it" (`cards.test.ts`) carries the promise. The other three planted-zero fixtures are reshaped to take the last person through a take: "a city whose food stock of nought would grow at no population falls on the food spent, and grows nobody" and "the fall's ending is read as any change…" (`chronicle.test.ts`) through a food strike on a city of one person, as `hungry(1, { population: 1 })` does; `hoardedDefeat` and "a chronicle that ends having reached no achievement pays nothing" (`campaign.test.ts`) must fall with the hoard's food standing, and every fixture strike takes food, so they take the removal: a city of one person holding its tile alone with nothing else inside the border, `removedInside` by one through `chartedAndRead`, which removes the city's building and ends in cause `removal`, the achievements recorded on the walk. A test's title that names the cause moves with it.
- Reconcile: the raising goes through the standing door, `fall` into an `ended`, as the capture does; the save reader's two refusals join its standing ones as the standing `count` with a least and a slot's `refused` do; nothing becomes one, nothing stays apart for a meant difference.

**Traps:**

- The population helper serves both cities by its `whose`; the fall is the player's alone.
- `followed` resolves nothing after a sequence whose chronicle has ended, so a source that appends the `ended` as the second change of its landing stops what follows on its own; `chartedAndRead` and the victory walk cut what follows a victory the same way, dropping a defeat `ended` that would have come after, which is the accepted reading.
- The save reader's convention is one refusal per slot with a reason naming the value (`count` with its `least`, `refused(catalogue, slot, reason)`); a settle-phase chronicle has no city and zero population and is refused by neither new check.
- Rules tests author synthetic fixtures freely, but a fixture that plants a fallen state unrecorded tests nothing after the line; the take is the way in.
- A hook flags a comment block longer than three lines under `src/`; the comment over the victory walk is cut, not reworded.
- The chronicle-level helpers live in `chronicle.ts` and `schedule.ts`; `state.ts` imports the `Unit` type, and a helper on the chronicle cannot move there.
