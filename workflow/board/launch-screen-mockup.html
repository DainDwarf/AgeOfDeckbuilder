The launch screen

The screen at the game's size, 1280 by 720, under the navbar and the bar as they stand today. Press an age, a region or a civilization to choose it. The controls under the screen change what the campaign holds and how the choices are drawn.

Real today: the Nomadic Age and its ground colour, the Temperate region and its shares of sea, mountain, woodland and land, the Nomadic civilization with 17 cards and 2 settle cards, every colour of the navbar, the bar, the buttons and the terrain.

Made up for the mockup: the other ages' colours, the other regions and their sand and ice, the other civilizations' names and counts, the turn and the achievement Continue reads.
