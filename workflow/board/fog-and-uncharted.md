# The fog and the uncharted

**Line:** The fog and the uncharted — a tile in fog is drawn with every painting and mark of it darkened by the fog's one tint, the art rising above its hexagon included; an uncharted tile wears his void; the disc's rim line is gone; and the fog spec proves it. **Spec:** [`docs/LOOK.md`](../../docs/LOOK.md) _The map_, [`docs/INTERFACE.md`](../../docs/INTERFACE.md) _What stands over what_, [`docs/CHRONICLE-SCREEN.md`](../../docs/CHRONICLE-SCREEN.md) _The veils and the infopanel_ (unchanged: a tile out of sight is still darkened), [`docs/GLOSSARY.md`](../../docs/GLOSSARY.md) (fog, charted, uncharted; "dimmed" is forbidden for the fog, the pages say "darkened"). **Scope:**

- In: the fog scrim per tile goes, and the `fog` stratum with it, nothing standing on it any more. A tile in fog has every image it carries tinted by the fog's entry — its face, its beach, the pond and the herd's copies, the feature's icon, the river pieces and the mouth's pressed straight at its corners under the corner rule — and every shape it carries filled and stroked in its colour multiplied by that same tint: the feature's disc and its rim (their strengths kept), the building, the improvements, the snapshot's unit mark, the border ring (the ring is the holder the snapshot keeps, as today). The outline black is multiplied too, which leaves it black. One small colour function in the look multiplies a colour by a tint, beside the two that already darken colours, which stay as they are (the reconcile: the fog is a multiply, the stack's is a lay-over on the page).
- In: a kept void image per tile beside the face and the shore, named `void-<key>`, wearing one of his four void paintings picked by the tile's place through the pick every painting goes through, standing exactly while the map does not draw the tile and hidden otherwise; the four void files enter the face sheet. The void stands under the row's faces, so a face fading in on a charting fades in over it and a tile behind's risen art stands over the void of its own row behind.
- In: the rim's stroke, its graphics object and its look entry go; the corner function stays for the rivers. The disc's edge is where the void meets the page.
- In: the river rule. A corner's piece is drawn where any tile at the corner is drawn, as today, and tinted where none of them is in sight; a mouth and its pressed straight follow their corner the same way.
- Decided here: the glows are not darkened — a landing lit in fog, an aimed tile in fog and the enemy's target glow stand at full strength over the darkened tile, which reads better than today's scrim over them. The yield glyphs, the culture threshold and the selection ring stand over the fog as they do.
- Decided here: the fog does not move. A tile falling into fog takes its tint at the render after the change has played, as the scrim appeared today; a tile charted fades in as today. Motion is the Motion rung's.
- Decided here: the three scrims that share the overlay's dim entry — the yield dim, the city-mode dim on a held tile nobody works, the spent unit's dim — are untouched; the rings-and-marks line re-reads them.
- The veils keep their meaning: the fog veil off tints nothing; the uncharted veil off draws every tile, so no void shows anywhere, and the void images are hidden with it.
- Out: the far view, the shore along the uncharted (stays bare, as `docs/LOOK.md` says), any change to the pieces' polygons.
- Reconcile, chosen: the fog's entry stays apart from the overlay's dim; the colour function stays apart from the lay-over and the wear; the void stays apart from the face; the tint stays apart from the sheet's boot-time frames.

**Traps:**

- A Shape has no tint component; only an Image does (the `docs/PHASER.md` line above, written by this line). A Container of polygons (a unit's mark) is darkened part by part through its colours.
- A pooled object comes back as its last use left it: `asPainted` in `src/ui/pool.ts` resets visibility, alpha and scale, never a tint or a colour. A face from the crossfade pool, a lying image, a river piece, the straight's container and a fog unit mark taken for a lit tile after a fogged one carry the tint. Every paint sets the tint or clears it, both ways, every time; nothing relies on a fresh object.
- The face sheet is 4096 square and a painting 256 by 384, so it holds 160 paintings in rows of 16; it holds 154 today (28 faces, 126 beaches) and the four void files make 158. `drawSheet` throws at boot when a sheet runs out of room; the feature sheet is separate and unaffected.
- The e2e readings. Four spec files read the scrim by name and by count: `e2e/fog.spec.ts` (three tests, `fog-<key>` standing, `marksIn(page, 'fog')`, and the attack test reading the scrim's standing inside the page), `e2e/archer.spec.ts:99`, `e2e/population-badge.spec.ts:77-79`, `e2e/console.spec.ts` (`fogMarks` in the screen reading at 151, the counts at 237, 246, 264). The scrim and the `fog` layer gone, these read the face's tint instead: a reading added to `readName` in `e2e/chronicle-screen.ts` for an Image's tint (its `isTinted` or its `tint` against the fog's entry), and the count becomes the tinted faces among the `terrain` group's `tile-` names, where `facesShown` already counts them. `DOGMAS.md` _Testing_: a reading the specs cannot make is a helper written in `e2e/`, never an object kept for it.
- `facesShown` counts the `terrain` group's names starting `tile-`; the void image is named `void-<key>` so the count stays the snapshots'. Six specs assert `tile-<key>` not standing for an uncharted tile; the void standing there does not change that.
- The map's `Drawing` already carries `drawn`, `live` and the snapshots; `faceIn` answers whether a face is the tile as it stands. The river pieces are read through `riverPieces(catalogue, rivers, drawn, faces)` in `src/ui/map-rivers.ts`, which knows each corner's tiles: the in-sight set goes in beside the drawn set, the piece answering whether it is in sight.
- A Layer draws by depth then add order (`docs/PHASER.md`): the void image takes its tile's row depth and is added before the face.
- The motion fades in what `paintTile` answers as painted; the void image is not painted by it, it is shown and hidden by the render alone.
- The look dogma: the fog's entry is named for its meaning and holds a tint, not a wash; `mapDim` keeps its three scrims.
