# Embarked units

**Line:** **Embarked units** — a unit may be embarked: it enters the tiles whose terrain names a movement cost for embarked units and no other, attacks nothing, and is attacked only by a unit whose range is above one; a card embarks or disembarks a unit standing beside the tile it is aimed at, spending that unit's action, and where several units could be the one the play names which; the guard's script passes over a unit it could not attack; the rules are held by tests on the fixture and no content of an age uses them yet. Doc-impact: `docs/CHRONICLE.md`, `docs/GLOSSARY.md`.

**Spec:**

- `docs/CHRONICLE.md` → _Units and combat_.
  - In the bullet **A unit moves on its own move points**, "A terrain may name no movement cost at all, and is then crossed by nothing." becomes "A terrain names the movement cost a unit enters it for, the one an embarked unit enters it for, or neither: a tile is entered by the units its terrain names a cost for, and a terrain that names neither is crossed by nothing."
  - A new bullet after **A unit attacks on its own action**: "**A unit embarks and disembarks through a card.** To **embark** is to step from the tile a unit stands on onto a tile beside it that embarked units enter; to **disembark** is the step back, onto a tile beside it that the unit stands on ashore. Either lands only on a free, charted tile and spends one of the unit's action, so it is the last thing that unit does that turn. An **embarked** unit is itself: it keeps its health, its sight and, a worker, the cards played through it. Its move is the one the card that embarked it names, and it attacks nothing; disembarked, its move, its damage and its range are its own again. A unit whose range is one attacks no embarked unit, and a unit of a longer range attacks one anywhere within its range. An embarked unit that attacks was rejected: out of every range-one unit's reach, it kills along a shore at no risk."
  - In the bullet **A unit attacks on its own action**, after "A worker attacks nothing because it is a worker, whatever its range or damage;" nothing changes: the embarked unit's sentence stands in its own bullet.

**Scope:**

- In: the embarked state on a unit; a terrain's movement cost for embarked units; the one answer to "what does entering this tile cost this unit", asked with the unit by every path, landing, script and standing check; the two attack rules; the two steps, embark and disembark, as helpers a card composes; the play that names its unit on a tie; the guard's script; the fixture's content and the tests.
- **The unit is itself, embarked** (the user's choice over one raft kind for all): it keeps its kind, its health, its sight and, a worker, its being a worker, so a card played through a worker is played through an embarked one wherever that card's own reasons admit the tile.
- **Its move** while embarked is a number the embarking card's content hands the helper; its damage and range read nothing while embarked. Disembarked, its move, damage and range are its own again, health untouched.
- **Embark asks of the tile**, in this order: charted; a terrain embarked units enter; no unit standing on it; a unit of the player's that is not embarked standing beside it; one of those holding an action. **Disembark asks**: charted; a terrain the unit stands on ashore; no unit standing on it; an embarked unit of the player's beside it; one of those holding an action. A camp's tile with no unit on it is landed on like any other.
- **The step spends the unit's action** as a card played through a worker does, so the move points it had left go with it; it is no walk, spends no move points of its own and reads no river.
- **The tie:** the units that can be the one are those beside the tile that the checks above admit. One: the play goes through it and names none. Several: the play names one of them, and a play that names none, or one that is not among them, is refused.
- **No attack from an embarked unit, and none on one at range one**, whatever the faction: one rule beside "a worker attacks nothing", in the one place both the player's attack and the scripts' target ask.
- **The guard** (`guarding` in `src/content/scripts.ts`) reads the units it closes on by distance alone today; it reads them through the rule, so a guard whose range is one neither closes on an embarked unit nor walks towards it. The raider already asks the rule.
- **Enemies embark nowhere** in this line: no script embarks. The rules are faction-blind all the same.
- **A unit killed while embarked** leaves whatever card embarked it as it lies; nothing is done about it, by the user's decision.
- The reconcile's choices: played through a unit stays apart from played through a worker (which unit, and the tile beside against the tile under, are meant; the spending of the action is the shared act); what entering a tile costs becomes one answer asked with the unit; who attacks whom becomes one rule; the tie's pick goes through the standing aim at a unit.
- Out: any terrain, card or text of an age; the screen. `src/ui/` changes only where a call no longer typechecks, and reads the cost a unit ashore pays, as today.
- Saves from before this line need no care.
