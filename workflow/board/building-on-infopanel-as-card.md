# A building on the infopanel as its card

**Line:** A building on the infopanel as its card — an inspected building, with its tile's improvements, is shown on the infopanel as the face of its card. Settled at intake: the improvements leave it for the terrain card, and the buildings no card places wear a face of their own.

Done-condition: an inspected tile's building card is the face of the card that places its building, drawn as that card is drawn in the hand; a site, a camp and the neutral's city wear a building face of their own; a building named in a text or marked at a window's foot shows that same face small at a rest and large at a right click; the tile's improvements read as rows on the terrain card; `docs/MAP-SCREEN.md` _The infopanel_ and `docs/LOOK.md` say so; the catalogue's coherence test holds every building to one face; `inspect.spec.ts` proves it.

**Spec:** `docs/MAP-SCREEN.md` _The infopanel_; `docs/LOOK.md` _The pieces on the map_ (the building's and the site's paragraphs) and _The cards_ (the art box, what a card goes on, what the thing placed gives and takes); `docs/INTERFACE.md` _A card's names and its label_, unchanged, whose "anything else as the card the infopanel reads it by" now hands a named building its face.

- Out: the terrain card as a face (its own line, after the unit's); the unit card (its own line); an improvement named in a text, which keeps today's grey card until the terrain line.
- Which card a building wears: the card whose play places it — Farm, Tannery, Fishery, Megalith, Shelter, Foundations, Great stones, and Ornaments for `monument`; a city section's building (`hearth`, `village`) wears the civilization's city card, Settlement, on its decorated front, holding the building standing on the tile (or named) and not the chronicle's city section. - Which text a false face reads is the building's role in its age's content — a site's building, the camp's building, the neutral's city's building — never its id; a building that is none of the three and that no card places is a defect the coherence test refuses, and so is a building two cards place.
- The infopanel's terrain card takes the improvements' rows after the feature's and before the river's, each in the order it was placed. A tile with an improvement and no building has no building card from now on: its cycle is the unit, if any, then the terrain card.
- The infopanel's ghosts stay today's grey paper, each the size of the card standing in front (124 × 182 behind a face, 130 × 182 behind grey paper), offset as today. The panel stands where it stands today, the face's top the grey card's top; the oval rim and the cost disc rise over it as on any face.
- The face on the infopanel reacts as any face does: a rest on a bracketed name or a window-foot mark raises its small card, a right click shows it large, the kind label raises its tooltip. A press on it anywhere else does what a press on today's infopanel card does.
- Fog: the building card reads the building the drawn face holds, as last seen, as today.
- Today's capstone rules leave a building's yield alone (the Long Winter's takes food from terrains and features only), so the face never disagrees with the yield overlay; the docs sentence states the face reads the card, not the tile.
- The LOOK pool's fit "the infopanel building card's 'No yield' drops under a site's name" ends with this line.
