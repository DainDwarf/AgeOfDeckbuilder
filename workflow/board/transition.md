# The transition

**Line:** **The transition** — every region's schedule is its own, every entry weighing one plain number on every turn: the Stone temperate schedule gets its own spacing and window and deals A rival band, Wildfire, The herd and Departure, Lean season staying the Nomadic Age's alone; the archipelago gets a schedule of its own that reads as the temperate's; the Nomadic schedule loses the turn its two harshest events waited for. Done when a schedule's entry is a number the catalogue refuses at nought or below, no region names another's schedule, `npm test` is green with the coherence test among it, and the pages below say so.

**Spec:** [`docs/CHRONICLE.md`](../../docs/CHRONICLE.md) _The schedule_, [`docs/ages/STONE.md`](../../docs/ages/STONE.md) _The events_, [`docs/ages/NOMADIC.md`](../../docs/ages/NOMADIC.md) _The events_. The sentences to land, written out:

The rest of the paragraph stands.
- `CHRONICLE.md` _The schedule_, second paragraph: "On a due turn, and no sooner, the Events phase deals one event, seeded and weighted among those that weigh anything on that turn and whose need is met: an event may name what it needs of the chronicle, and one whose need is not met on its turn weighs nothing, so no event is dealt that does nothing." becomes "On a due turn, and no sooner, the Events phase deals one event, seeded and weighted among those whose need is met: an event may name what it needs of the chronicle, and one whose need is not met on its turn weighs nothing, so no event is dealt that does nothing. A weight not above nought is a defect of the content, refused before any chronicle is dealt." The rest of the paragraph stands.
- `CHRONICLE.md` _The schedule_, last paragraph: "The schedule escalates in what lands, never in how often: an event dealt late is harsher than one dealt early, whichever answer is chosen, and the harshest carry no weight at first." becomes "The schedule escalates in what lands, never in how often or in what it draws: every event weighs the same on every turn, and an event dealt late is harsher than one dealt early, whichever answer is chosen. A weight shifting with the turn was rejected: the turn already escalates what lands, and a schedule that also shifts what it draws is a second escalation on the same deal, one the player cannot read."
- `ages/NOMADIC.md` _The events_, the opening paragraph: "The escalation is the event's, whichever answer is chosen: a later Lean season adds a Hunger that takes more, a later rival band is larger either way, and Wildfire weighs nothing early." becomes "The escalation is the event's, whichever answer is chosen: a later Lean season adds a Hunger that takes more, and a later rival band is larger either way; the four weigh the same from the first turn, The herd rarer than the three."
- `ages/STONE.md` _The events_, the opening paragraph's first and last sentences: "The temperate region's schedule opens on the Nomadic events, which weigh less as the turns pass while the age's own weigh nothing early and take over: Lean season, scaled for a larger city, A rival band, Wildfire and The herd, and Departure, this age's for good, culture being its answer's price." becomes "The temperate region's schedule is its own: its deals come closer together than the band's and its capstone later, and every event weighs the same from the first turn. It deals three of the Nomadic events as the band knew them, A rival band, Wildfire and The herd, and Departure, this age's for good, culture being its answer's price; Lean season is the band's alone." and "The archipelago's schedule copies what suits it and holds its own." becomes "The archipelago's schedule is its own, and deals what the temperate's deals, on the temperate's tempo." The two sentences between them stand.

No player-facing text changes: no event, answer, card or lore entry is added or renamed.

**Doc-impact:** `docs/CHRONICLE.md`, `docs/ages/STONE.md`, `docs/ages/NOMADIC.md`.

**Scope:**

- In, the mechanism: a schedule entry's weight becomes a plain number (`Schedule` in `src/rules/catalogue.ts`); the catalogue's schedule check refuses a weight not above nought, as it refuses a unit table's; the draw on a due turn weighs the entries whose need is met and keeps the step it takes where none is. The branch for a due turn on which no entry weighs anything goes with the type, and the rules test that proves it (`src/rules/schedule.test.ts`, the one on a weight that rises on turn 3) goes with the branch, its state being one the catalogue now refuses. One catalogue test on the fixture holds the refusal: a region's schedule weighing an entry at nought is refused. The fixture's schedules (`src/rules/fixtures.ts`) lose their turn functions.
- In, the content: the Nomadic schedule's A rival band and Wildfire weigh 1 from the first turn; Lean season 1 and The herd 1/3 as they are; spacing 6 to 9 and window 12 to 18 as they are. The Stone temperate schedule, written out in `src/content/stone.ts` and sharing no object with the Nomadic one: spacing 5 to 8, window 26 to 34, the capstone `first-shelter` as the stand-in it is until the capstone lines land, entries A rival band 1, Wildfire 1, The herd 1/3, Departure 1. The archipelago's own schedule constant, the same values, named by that region alone. Departure's answers and prices stand as they are.
- Out: Lean season on any Stone schedule, and any Stone copy of it; any fade, rise, floor or start turn, in either age; the archipelago's own numbers, its line's; the capstones, their lines'; every number above, tuning the balance pass and the simulator revisit; the `hunger` hazard and the Nomadic answers' sizes, unchanged.
- Corner cases decided here: a Stone chronicle's first deal is due on turn 5 to 8, and Departure needs nothing of the chronicle, so a due turn on a Stone region always has an entry to draw from; the step the generator takes where no need is met stays, so a seed deals on the same turns whatever is played. A Nomadic chronicle's first deal, due on turn 6 to 9, may now be A rival band or Wildfire where the tile they need is there.
- Reconcile: the schedules stay apart by a meant difference — the user's rule that regions share events and never a schedule — so nothing is derived from anything: the Stone table is written in full, and the archipelago's constant too, though its values are the temperate's today. The Nomadic and the Stone tables share three event ids, which the catalogue allows and the rule intends.

**Traps:**

- The merged catalogue refuses an id two slices bring and an answer id two events deal (`src/rules/catalogue.ts`); the three shared events are brought by the Nomadic slice alone and named by both schedules, which is allowed, and nothing here brings a new id.
- The fixture age `QUIET` (`src/rules/fixtures.ts`) deals nothing before `FAR` through a weight that rises on that turn, and some fifty tests across the rules and the ui launch in it; it must keep dealing nothing before `FAR` by another device once weights are numbers, and the per-event fixture schedules already show one, a spacing of `FAR`. The fixture's timeline constant also sets the next deal at `FAR`.
- `src/rules/schedule.test.ts` names `runtime-error` on a deal in several tests; only the one on a weight rising on turn 3 depends on a turn-gated weight, and the others, on an unmet need, stand.
- `src/rules/catalogue.test.ts` builds reschedule fixtures with `() => 1` entries in its refusal tests; they follow the type.
- `src/content/catalogue.test.ts` rolls every region's timeline and lands every answer of every entry of every schedule on a chronicle settled in that age; with Lean season off the Stone schedules, its answers are proven on the Nomadic chronicle alone, which is the age that deals it.
- `e2e/chronicle-screen.ts`'s `leanSeason()` searches Nomadic seeds for a first deal of Lean season alone and keeps finding one; `firstDealt(1)` is read by `hover` and `resume`, which take whatever event seed 1 deals first and may now meet a rival band or a wildfire there. `e2e/landing.spec.ts` searches seeds for The herd alone. `e2e/capstone.spec.ts` ends turns on Nomadic seed 1 up to the capstone's turn, which the Nomadic spacing and window keep where it was.
- Specs settling on a campaign from `campaignWith` launch in the Stone Age, and the ones that end turns past turn 4 — `neutral`, `fishery`, `calendar`, `heal` among them — may now meet a deal one turn sooner than before; each one that stops on a deal where it did not is a spec to look at, and CI proves them on the push.
- A `Chronicle` holds the timeline rolled, never the schedule; no save field changes, and no save compatibility is owed before the Bronze Age.
- Comments are for traps only; the pages hold the why.
