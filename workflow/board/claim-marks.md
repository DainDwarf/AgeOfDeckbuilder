# The claim marks

**Line:** **The claim marks** — in city mode the tiles the player's city may claim wear a ghost of the border, the border's own line and band in culture's colour kept to the claimable tiles and drawn under the border and the pieces, and the culture threshold the selected tile wears stands on the feature disc's plate; the city-mode spec reads the ghost's tiles against the rules' claimable. Doc-impact: `docs/LOOK.md`.

**Spec:** `docs/LOOK.md`, _The map_, one paragraph added after _The reach_, verbatim:

> **The claim marks.** In city mode the tiles the player's city may claim wear a ghost of its border: the border's own line and band, in culture's colour, run round the tiles the city holds and the tiles it may claim together, and kept to the claimable tiles, so a held tile's edge wears the border and nothing more. Where the ghost's line leaves a claimable tile's edge for a held tile's it runs on to the middle of that edge, where the border's line passes over it, so the two meet. It is drawn under the border and under the pieces as the border is, the yield overlay's dim covers it as it covers the border, and it is never darkened by the fog, being the screen's answer as the selection is. The culture threshold the selected tile wears stands on a plate: the feature disc's dark plate with its pale rim, a pill round the number and culture's glyph.

`docs/CHRONICLE-SCREEN.md` changes nothing: its city-mode paragraph already says the map marks the tiles the city can claim while the mode is on and that the selected claimable tile wears the threshold as a minus, the number and culture's glyph. No player-facing text changes; the threshold's entry `threshold.culture` stays.

**Doc-impact:** `docs/LOOK.md`.

**Scope:**

- In: the ghost in place of today's 2-unit ring in culture's purple; the plate under the threshold in place of its dark text stroke; the city-mode spec's reading of the marks.
- The ghost's drawing is the border's, with the border's own numbers read from where the border reads them: the line 1.5 units wide, the band at the border's strength, 60%, fading bell-shaped over the border's depth, 7 units. The ghost has no numbers of its own; its colour is culture's reading, as the ring's is today. If the border is tuned later the ghost moves with it, which is meant.
- The loops the ghost runs on are the loops of the held tiles and the claimable tiles taken together, holes included: a camp's tile, the neutral's land, a tile another faction's unit stands on, an uncharted tile. The band is painted inside the loops and clipped to the claimable tiles' hexagons, as the border's band is clipped to the held tiles. The line is the loops' stretches whose tile is claimable, and, at a corner where a claimable tile's stretch meets a held tile's, the held tile's half of that corner's curve too, so the purple runs to the middle of the held tile's edge and meets the gold line there; the border's line is drawn over it.
- Under the pieces, under the border: the ghost stands on the map's terrain stratum, added before the border so the border draws over it; today's ring stood over the units on the city marks stratum. Under the yield overlay's dim, as today and as the border.
- A claimable tile in fog, charted and out of sight, wears the ghost as one in sight does: no darkening.
- The ghost is painted when city mode is on, and repainted when its tiles change, a claim, an assign, a unit moving beside the border, a charting, a capture or the neutral's claims among them; a render repaints the city marks today, which is enough. Off city mode it is hidden, as the ring is.
- The plate: the threshold's number, 12 bold in the threshold's white ink, and culture's 8-unit glyph 3 units after it, stand on a pill 16 units tall with 4 units of room each side of the pair, centred on the tile's middle as the pair is today; the pill is filled in the feature disc's colour and strength, 72% dark, and rimmed 1 unit in the feature disc's rim, pale at 55%. The number loses its dark stroke. At three digits the pill reads 36 units wide on the 48-unit tile; at two, 30. The pill is drawn as the population badge's pill is drawn, a rounded rectangle as tall as it is round at the ends, widening about its middle with the number.
- The threshold is still on the selected claimable tile alone, replacing that tile's yield glyphs, as today; nothing on the other claimable tiles.
- Out: any change to the held tiles' dim, the population's diamond or the yields in city mode, which are the glyph rung's; anything of the city-mode UI ideas noted in the look pool; the border's own drawing, which this line reads and does not touch.
- Reconcile: the ghost's band and line go through the border's own band and line painters, reshaped to take what the ghost needs as parameters: the loops, the tiles the band covers, the stretches the line draws, the colour, and one group with no fog split where the border paints two. The plate goes through the badge's pill drawing, the fill and the rim its parameters. The threshold's number, glyph and container stay as they are; the name `threshold` the spec reads stays.

**Traps:**

- The border's band at an inward corner runs a few units into the tile not held and leaves a sliver of bare ground between band and line: an accepted look, never a deviation or a review finding, and the ghost's band at its own inward corners falls under the same call. Do not report it.
- `e2e/city-mode.spec.ts` counts the claimable marks by name, one object per tile today (`counted(page, 'claimable')`, three assertions in one test, and once more after a claim). The ghost is one layer: it carries its claimable tiles' keys as data, as the reach's layer carries its landings' keys, and the spec reads them through a helper in `e2e/chronicle-screen.ts` as `litKeys` reads the reach's, asserting them against the rules' `claimable` set and an empty list off city mode. The same spec reads the threshold's text through `thresholdShown`, which reads the container named `threshold`; keep that name and the number as a Text.
- `borderColours` in `e2e/chronicle-screen.ts` reads every Graphics in the group named `border` and counts its line styles against the held tiles' stretches; `e2e/neutral.spec.ts` and `e2e/settle.spec.ts` assert it. The ghost's line must not stand in that group: a group of its own on the terrain stratum, added before the border's.
- `faceCounts` in `e2e/chronicle-screen.ts` reads the terrain stratum's children named `tile-…`; a layer added there is skipped by name, so nothing to do, but the stratum is walked by specs.
- The border's painters in `src/ui/map-border.ts` paint on a canvas over the disc at a pixel to the unit through `paintFaded` in `src/ui/map-fade.ts`, which fades a path inward from its line, bell-shaped, kept inside the path; the line is a Graphics stroked one `lineStyle` per stretch. The reach in the same file is the second user of both and the pattern to follow for a region of its own: a disc canvas plus a Graphics in a layer, repainted only when the keys it was drawn from change. The stretches `src/ui/border-loops.ts` hands out each carry their tile, and a corner's curve is two half-stretches, one per tile, split at the point nearest the corner: the cut to the claimable tiles is a filter on the stretch's tile, and the join to the border is the adjoining held half-stretch kept.
- A Shape carries no tint and a canvas texture is painted in its colours; the ghost darkens nothing, so no fog split (`docs/PHASER.md`). A Graphics joins its stroke's segments only above a width of 2, and the border's 1.5 draws each stretch as its own quad, which is why a cut line needs no special end (`docs/PHASER.md`).
- A canvas texture of a power-of-two side gets mipmaps, as the border's does through `sceneCanvas`; a texture key is the game's and is removed at the scene's shutdown there (`docs/PHASER.md`).
- The map's lattice is squashed, rows three quarters of a tile's width apart: every corner goes through `cornerAt` in `src/ui/map-marks.ts`, as the border's loops do.
- `src/ui/look.ts` holds one entry per meaning: the plate reads `featureDisc` and `featureDiscRim`, the ghost reads `reading.culture`, `borderBand` and the border's own widths; no new entry, and no value typed twice.
