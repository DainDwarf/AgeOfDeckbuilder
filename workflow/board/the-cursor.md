**Scope:**

In: the nine states on every screen, the meta screens, the menu and the windows among them, read by the one function that sets the cursor today; the eighteen files and the licence document in the store, pushed there first; the licence record's entry and its credit, which the Credits window and the licences' page read by themselves; the three `docs/` pages; the specs that read the cursor; the proof spec.

Out: a pointer larger than 32 CSS pixels, at any interface size; a pointer drawn in the canvas; an animated pointer; the pack's arrow-with-sign twins (`walk002`, `swords001`, `question001`, `denied003` and their like), which are what to reach for if a sign standing on the middle of a tile hides too much of it in play, the user's to say; the pack's 111 other cursors, which stay on the user's disk; a line of `CHANGELOG.md`.

Corner cases, decided:

- **A thing that reacts to a left click and to a rest** wears the click's picture. The help sign is for a thing a left click does nothing on: a stat of the summary, a name in a goal's line.
- **A dead button** wears the arrow, whether or not it names itself at rest.
- **A card being aimed at the hand** admits every other card of the hand: each wears the aim sign, and the card being aimed itself the open hand.
- **A card of the hand being aimed, or selected,** still wears the open hand, or the not-allowed sign where it is unaffordable: a held press lifts it either way.
- **A unit of the player's on a tile the card being aimed admits** wears the aim sign, and on a tile it does not admit the not-allowed sign: both stand before the open hand in the order.
- **An uncharted tile** wears the arrow under every aim: a press there lands off the map, and nothing answers it.
- **A click on an admitted tile of an unaffordable card** is refused for the cost, so the whole drawn map wears the not-allowed sign while that card is being aimed (the user's call).
- **A card or a unit being dragged** wears the closed hand everywhere, over a marked unit and over the hand's lane too: letting a card go clear of the hand selects it and plays it or leaves it being aimed, and never plays it on the tile under it.
- **In city mode**, a tile holding an assigned population wears the open hand, the selected tile whose click assigns, takes off or claims wears the pointing hand, and every other tile the arrow.
- **Under a minimized window** each thing is read by the same order: nothing reacts to a left click and a held press takes hold of nothing, so a thing that still raises something at rest wears the help sign, the rest the arrow, and the map panned the closed hand.
- **The closed hand** comes at the moment a refusal's note comes down for a drag: once the held press has travelled the click's slack, the one measure for every thing dragged.

What the reconcile chose:

- The cursor and the hovers go on being read by the one function that reads the thing under the pointer once a frame; it gains the states, and no second reader is made.
- The step, attack and aim signs read the tile the path, the attack's arrow and a card's yield plates already read under the pointer each frame, and the not-allowed sign reads the refusal a card's red cost and an answer's darkened plate are drawn from: the same answers, asked once.
- The step sign and the cast's Move stay two pictures, a difference that is meant; the not-allowed sign keeps the pointer's gold beside the pale crossed circle on its red disc.
- The pack enters as the other bought packs did: the store, the record, the credit.

**Traps:**

- **Nobody opens the pack's pictures with a model.** The licence forbids feeding the assets to an AI model, and its words are broad: the implementer, the reviewer and any agent copy, list and measure the files by commands and never read one as an image. Everything the work needs of them is in the table above, measured by a script. A CSS cursor is never in a Playwright screenshot, so no shot shows them either.
- **Never over 32 CSS pixels.** Chrome and Firefox swap a custom cursor larger than 32 CSS pixels for the system arrow wherever its outline touches the window's bottom or right edge. The 64 px files are for dense screens only, drawn across 32.
- **The cursor is one style on the canvas, written only at an edge**, and Phaser never works it out from what lies under the pointer; the game's own reading once a frame is what is right under a resting pointer (`docs/PHASER.md`, the two paragraphs on what the pointer is over and on the cursor). That reading is never taken inside Phaser's input dispatch, where a hit test refills the array being walked.
- **`reactsToPress` says "a left click or a rest" in one word today**, at nineteen sites across `src/ui/`; the help sign needs each to say which of the two it is.
- **Every way a drag ends ends the closed hand:** the release, a release off the canvas, a scrim rising under the press, the window losing focus. A closed hand left standing after one of them is the bug to look for.
- **A browser fetches a cursor's picture when it first shows it**, so a state seen for the first time flashes its fallback: the pictures of the screen's density are fetched before the first screen stands.
- **A browser that does not take `image-set()` in a cursor leaves the style empty** when it is written: the mockup writes the plain 32 px picture then, and reads the style back to know.
- **The Credits window gains a heading and a line**, and `src/ui/credits.test.ts` and `e2e/credits.spec.ts` read the record: a list that no longer fits the window at 1280 by 720 is reported, not laid out again here.
- **Sixty-one reads of the cursor across fourteen spec files** go through `cursorAt` and `HAND` in `e2e/chronicle-screen.ts`; what `canvas.style.cursor` holds is no longer the word `pointer`.
- **The store is pushed before the pointer moves on `main`** (`DOGMAS.md`, _Assets carry a licence record_); the pack is at `C:\Users\daind\OneDrive\Bureau\Bought Assets\fantasy_rpg_cursors_gold`, its `32` and `64` folders and `Standard_Asset_Licence.docx`.
- The throwaway spec that built the mockup's five screens is at `.playwright/cursor-src/zz-cursor.spec.ts`, outside the tracked tree: a Nomadic turn with an unaffordable card, a unit with a lit tile and a marked enemy, a card being aimed that admits one tile, city mode, a deal with an answer the city cannot pay. A source to read, never a file to commit.
