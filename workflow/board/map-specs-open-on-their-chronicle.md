**Traps:**

- The boot reads the save only on a bare address (`src/main.ts`): the opener goes to `/`, never to an address naming content or a seed. The save is planted through `addInitScript` before the navigation; the entry key is `SAVE_ENTRY` in `src/ui/save-entry.ts`. The shared module already holds the opener, `openSaved`, the Nomadic fixture builder, `settledOn`, the seed search, `firstSeed`, the stepped-worker search, `workerStepped`, and the headless turn, `endedTurn`; every one takes the catalogue from the chronicle or as an argument. A chronicle saved with a deal standing opens on that deal's window once the capstone's window is closed.
- A rules helper called outside `apply` charts nothing, and answers a stage, `{ chronicle }`, not a chronicle: `entered` in `src/rules/catalogue.ts`, `campsPlaced` in `src/rules/schedule.ts` (its `placed` holds the tiles), `chartedAt` in `src/rules/sight.ts`, `campUnit` in `src/rules/enemies.ts`. The fixture is passed through `charted` after them, as the victory fixture is; `writeSave` validates by reading back, and a fixture the reading refuses throws at the plant, which is the finding.
- `chartedAt` takes the snapshot with whoever stands on the tile: the enemy entered before the charting is the mark in fog. A unit entered on a charted tile out of sight leaves the tile's snapshot as it was, so the raider three off is drawn nowhere until its crossing brings it into sight, which is the crossing the test watches.
- `campsPlaced` draws its tile from the chronicle's generator and enters no unit; a placed camp with nobody on it is captured by a player's unit standing on it through the enemy phase (`captures` in `src/rules/chronicle.ts`). The guard route was measured and rejected: the camp's own roll enters a second guard mid-fight.
- Every enemy script in the Nomadic content is the camp's unit, a warrior; `campUnit(catalogue, tile, 'raider' | 'guard')` names the script through the catalogue's camp. A raider on the city's tile stays there and the city falls: the raider three off lands at distance one or two on the fixture and never on the city.
- The Nomadic city holds its own tile alone at the settle, sight two; the scout sees three and moves four; the worker sees two and moves two; forest and hills cost two move points, plain one. The camps keep at least seven from the centre, so no camp is charted at the opening.
- The Nomadic first entries are never the do-nothing answers: Lean season's first lays a Hunger that kills the city's last population when the stock falls short, Departure's first takes it outright. No fixture here ends a turn through a deal: the herd fixture stops on its first deal, and every other fixture is turn 1.
- The bindings' storage: `bound`, `DEFAULTS`, `parseBindings` and `serialiseBindings` are exported from `src/ui/bindings.ts`; the storage key `STORED` is module-private, and how the spec reaches it — the key exported, or a planting helper beside `keep` — is the implementer's call. The zoom-in control's second slot is empty by default; a key's `code` is the DOM code, `KeyZ` for `z`. `keysReach` in `src/ui/map.ts` withholds the pan and zoom keys while a menu window stands, not while the deal window does.
- The map's bounds keep `KEPT` design pixels of the map in the frame (`src/ui/map.ts`), and at zoom one the frame holds the whole disc's width: a tile near the centre leaves the frame only zoomed in, which is why the landing test zooms two notches first. `pushOut` in the landing spec pans by the keys and stops when the map stands against its bounds.
- The chronicle screen raises the capstone's window on its first render, a resumed chronicle included (`src/ui/overlay.ts`); its card closes it, and the back key does not, being rebindable. Every fixture here is turn 1 or before its first deal's take.
- The infopanel's unit card lays its rows in `STATS` order, damage second (`src/ui/infopanel.ts`), and the damage label is the widest; the tooltip test runs on a thin margin the user accepted, and a CI failure of it is a removal on the user's say, never a re-budget.
- `counted` and `marksIn` read the map's scene through `window.named`, which `openSaved` installs by `readNames`; the map's containers are named `terrain`, `fog`, `units`, `buildings`.
- CI runs the suite split by spec across four runners: press is the slowest spec and sets the slowest shard on its own; map at three minutes is the second.
- A spec's duration is measured with nothing else running on the machine: a run beside the user's play or another suite reads contention, not the spec.
