# The neutral's workers

**Line:** **The neutral's workers** — the neutral opens with one worker of its own on its city's tile and enters more through its ratio; where the neutral acts, a worker of its plays an improvement or a building card its content names on the tile its city holds that it stands on, through the one door the player's play through a worker goes through, the cost paid out of the neutral's stocks, under one test on the fixture; the villager's worker goes for the tile where a card adds most to the yield, under one test beside it; a pillager goes for either city's workers and builds, the neutral's city's own tile spared; the pages say so. Doc-impact: `docs/CHRONICLE.md`, `docs/GLOSSARY.md`, `docs/ages/STONE.md`.

**Spec:**

  > the chronicle opens with it standing, its tile held with one population on it and one worker of its own standing on that tile, of the kind its content names; a neutral naming none opens with none and builds nothing.

  Its sentence "A card played through a worker lands on no tile the neutral holds, its city's included." becomes:

  > A card played through a worker of the player's lands on no tile the neutral holds, its city's included, and one played through a worker of the neutral's on no tile the player holds.

  After the sentence ending "a unit entered waits for the next enemy phase, as an entered enemy does." add, in the same paragraph:

  > Its workers build as the player's do: where its units act, a worker of its plays an improvement or a building card its content names through itself, on the tile its city holds that it stands on, as the player plays one through a worker — the card admits the tile as it admits the player's, the cost is paid out of the neutral's stocks, the worker spends its action, and a card the neutral cannot pay for is not played; the neutral has no deck, so the map is the only cap on how many of a building it builds. A pillage prepared on a tile the neutral holds lands as on the player's, and never on its city's own tile, where an enemy prepares nothing.

  Its sentence "What the neutral does with what it has is its script, content: which tile it claims, which tiles its population works, which unit it enters out of its population and its stocks, and where its units go, chosen on the chronicle as it stands; it never draws a hand." becomes:

  > What the neutral does with what it has is its script, content: which tile it claims, which tiles its population works, which unit it enters out of its population and its stocks, which card a worker of its plays and where, and where its units go, chosen on the chronicle as it stands; it never draws a hand.

  > Its units are the age's own, warriors, archers and workers, entered as the player's unit cards enter them and kept near a ratio of warriors to archers to workers its content names, the kind whose share falls shortest entered first and the warrior of equals, and it opens with one worker;

Hostile it works on, and of no city it stands where it is.

- `docs/ages/STONE.md` _The camps_: "**Pillager** goes for the nearest, by its own walk, of the player's workers and of what they built, the improvements and the buildings, the player's city's tile and a camp's never, and no tile it would stand on only embarked;" becomes:

- No player-facing text: the worker is the Nomadic kind, named already; a unit card of the neutral's reads its kind as today; the neutral's buildings and improvements read as any tile's layers do.

**Scope:**

- In: a fourth act a unit's script may answer, a card played on the tile it stands on; the rules resolving it for a unit of the neutral's — the card among those its content names, else a `runtime-error`; the card's aim admitting the tile for the neutral and its stocks paying, else nothing; then the cost out of the neutral's stocks, the worker's action spent and the tile relaid, through the card's own aim and effect, under a group of its own; the three reads a card played through a worker makes of the player by name — the worker's faction, the other city's border, the inside of the border — widened to the faction, so the aim admits a tile for the neutral as it admits one for the player; the neutral's content naming the cards its workers play and the script its workers carry, the catalogue refusing a card a worker's action does not go to and cards named with no worker kind in the ratio; the opening entering one worker of the neutral's on its city's tile, of the first worker kind its ratio names, and the entry giving a worker kind the worker script in place of the guard's; the Stone Age neutral's worker row, its five cards and its worker script; the pillager going for either city's workers and builds; the pillage sparing the neutral's city's own tile as it spares the player's; the fixture's second neutral for the new tests; the coherence test widened; one proof spec and the two standing neutral specs that put a unit on the neutral's city's tile adapted to the worker standing there at the opening.
- Out: a cap on how many of a building the neutral builds (the map is the cap until the balance pass); the neutral's Fishery, which needs a worker of its embarked (an idea); drawing anything new on the screen; the ratio's numbers beyond the worker's share of one against the warrior's two and the archer's one, a balance-pass matter; why six of sixty temperate neutrals enter no unit by turn 60 under today's rules, a balance-pass measurement.
- Corner cases decided here: a neutral naming no worker kind opens with no worker and its content names no card, as a camp naming no wave sends none; a worker of the neutral's with nothing to play rests by the guard's rule, off its city's tile while another held tile is free, wandering among the held tiles on the seed, so the entry's tile stays free; hostile, the worker keeps building, and of no city it stands where it is and plays nothing; the worker's script answers the play only where the card admits the tile on the chronicle as it stands when the act is asked, so a stock spent or a tile taken since the move leaves it playing nothing; a play answered by an enemy, by an embarked unit, or on a tile the unit does not stand on is a `runtime-error`; the neutral's city's own tile takes an improvement from its worker as the player's takes one from theirs, and is never pillaged, so a pillager standing there with nothing to attack prepares nothing; the wave counts guards by their script, so a worker is never sent; a pillager goes for a worker of the neutral's as for one of the player's, and the neutral's guards answer it as they answer any enemy; a hazard, an event or the fog read nothing new.
- Reconcile: the neutral's play goes through the standing worker-card door with the faction as its parameter, and is staged as a group of its own, never `played`, which the hand animates and the achievements count; the neutral's entry and the neutral's play become one door inside the rules, what resolves being the parameter; the worker's rest goes through the guard's resting rule, shared; the worker's reading of a tile's worth is the assign's, food first then everything together, on the tile as it would stand after the card, and the claim's own worth stays apart; the opening worker enters through the one way a unit enters.

**Traps:**

- The `played` group (`src/rules/stages.ts`) carries a card and an aim: `src/ui/hand.ts` plays it as a card flying out of the hand, and `plays` in `src/rules/stages.ts` feeds every tally in `src/rules/chronicle.ts` (`playsOn`, `terrainsPlayedOn`, `turnsPlaying`). A neutral's play staged under that name would fly a card the hand does not hold and count toward the player's achievements; it is a group name of its own, and the group names are the closed set `PlainGroup` there, which `src/ui/map.ts`, `hand.ts` and `piles.ts` each switch over without a default.
- A tile aim's `refuses` and `effect` (`Aim` in `src/rules/catalogue.ts`) take no faction; the unit card's `blocked` and `effect` (`UnitEntry`) already take `whose`, the precedent. Three reads name the player: `worked` and the other-city check inside `throughWorker`, and `inside`, all in `src/rules/cards.ts`; every other check a worker card composes — the ground, the slot, the improvement — reads no faction. The screen lights and refuses tiles through `refuses` in `cards.ts` and `admittedAt` in `chronicle.ts`, which read for the player.
- `scriptedActs` (`src/rules/chronicle.ts`) asks a unit's acts again while it holds action; a play spends it through `actionSpent`, so the loop ends. `ScriptAct` is switched there and in `readOf` of `src/content/scripts.test.ts`; `npm run check` names both once the fourth act lands.
- `cityUnit` in `src/rules/cards.ts` hands every unit the neutral enters its guard's script; a worker kind is told by `stats.worker`, the way `canAttack` and `worked` already tell one. `neutralEntered` in `src/rules/city.ts` is the cost-and-refuse shape the play shares.
- `beginChronicle` (`src/rules/chronicle.ts`) enters the camps' opening guards through `enteredOnCamp` after the chronicle is built and charts after; the neutral's worker enters there through `entered` on `neutral.city`, before the charting. Its number is the first dealt on a chronicle holding the neutral, so every fixture and spec reading unit numbers on such a chronicle shifts by one where the neutral names a worker.
- `FIXTURE_NEUTRAL` in `src/rules/fixtures.ts` is every fixture age's neutral and is built on by some seventy-five test uses that stand units on `NEUTRAL_TILE` or expect the entry there; it names no worker kind and no card, so nothing enters at the opening and every standing fixture stays where it is. The new tests lay a second neutral over the first age through `aged`, naming a worker row (`PH_Worker`), cards (`PH_Farm`, `PH_Mine`) and a fixture worker script that plays the first card of the list that admits the tile it stands on and otherwise stands still.
- `goesFor` in `src/content/scripts.ts` reads `standing?.faction === 'player'` for the worker it goes for; `pillaged` in `src/rules/enemies.ts` spares the player's city's tile outright and spares only the building on the neutral's, so a worker's improvement on the neutral's city tile is pillaged today; `prepared` in `chronicle.ts` reads `pillaged` to allow a prepare.
- `NEUTRAL_GUARD`'s resting branch in `src/content/scripts.ts` — the free held tiles off the city, `wanderedTo` among the resting landings — is the rule the worker shares; the yield a tile would have after a card is `tileYield` on the tile relaid, with `tilesBeside(chronicle.tiles)` as the claim and the assign read it.
- In `e2e/neutral.spec.ts`, two specs put a unit on the neutral's city's tile at seed 1's turn 1 through `unitEntered`, which checks no occupancy: the guard entered on its city, and the warrior that captures it. With the worker standing there from the opening each needs the tile free: the worker steps off at the first enemy phase after a claim (`neutralClaimed` in that file ends turns until one lands), or the unit enters beside it. The proof spec builds its chronicle through the rules' own helpers: `bordered` for the neutral on a plain beside its city, `yielded` for the neutral on a production tile as often as the card costs, the worker entered on the plain, then End turn pressed with both veils off, the building's mark rising on the tile.
- `docs/PHASER.md` for anything the proof spec waits out before it measures.
- `DOGMAS.md` _Testing_: the villager's worker is content that decides, one test beside the script on the fixture's ground; the play is a mechanism, one test on the fixture through the end of turn; the Stone Age's cards get the coherence checks alone.
