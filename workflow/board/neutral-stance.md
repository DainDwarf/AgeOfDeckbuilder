# The neutral's stance

**Line:** The neutral's stance — the neutral's relations with the player are at peace or hostile: a unit of the player's landing on a tile the neutral holds or attacking a unit of its declares war, through the Declare War warning while at peace, and at peace crosses no tile it holds; hostile for the rest of the chronicle, its units attack the player's, read the whole map and capture the player's city as enemies do, and its unit card reads Hostile. Done when: the rules tests hold, on the fixture, the declaration by the landing and by the attack, the crossing refused at peace and open hostile, the hostile unit's attack on a unit of the player's and its capture of the player's city, and no wave sent while hostile; the two Stone scripts' hostile walk has its test beside the script; the proof spec sees the warning rise on the press, Back move nothing, and the press gone through land the unit with the neutral hostile; `docs/CHRONICLE.md`, `docs/GLOSSARY.md`, `docs/ages/STONE.md`, `docs/CHRONICLE-SCREEN.md` and `DOGMAS.md` say so.

**Spec:** `docs/CHRONICLE.md` _The neutral_, `docs/GLOSSARY.md`, `docs/ages/STONE.md` _The neutral_, `docs/CHRONICLE-SCREEN.md` _The windows_ and _The map's presses_, `DOGMAS.md` _Design principles_ and _Architecture_. The sentences, written out:

**Scope:**

In:

- A relations row on the neutral's city, at peace at the opening, written into the save and read back; one new change, `relations`, the one row it moves, raised inside the move's or the attack's own sequence when the press declares war.
- At peace, the player's walk offers a tile the neutral holds as a landing and routes nothing through it: a tile beyond one is reached only by another way. Hostile, the walk is open. A landing on a held tile, its city's own included, and an attack on a unit of its, the one that kills it included, each declare war; any unit, a worker among them.
- The warning: the chronicle screen, on a click or a drag's release that would land on a held tile or attack a unit of the neutral's while at peace, raises the menu's warning as the launch screen raises the launch warning, the through press issuing the command and Back, the back key or the scrim taking it down with nothing moved, the unit still selected and lit; a dragged marker comes home as it does when let go anywhere else, and the move's slide plays from home once gone through. Which presses declare war is read off one rules helper; the screen reflects it and decides nothing.
- Hostile: the neutral's unit attacks the player's, is shut out of no tile, occupies the tile it stands on, prepares on the player's city as the raider does and captures it at the next enemy phase, which is defeat by capture; a tile it prepared on wears the neutral's colour and its card reads Capturing as an enemy's does; the neutral enters its units as at peace and sends no wave while hostile; the enemies and its units still attack one another.
- The Stone scripts, content: guard and wave each act as the raider while hostile, in their walk and their act alike, as the pillager falls back on the raider; one test beside each on the fixture.
- The unit card's Hostile line, the defeat text, the glossary rows and the dogma's reword.

Out, decided here:

- The capture of the neutral's city and what it deals, the fall line's: a unit of the player's standing on its city's tile through the enemy phase captures nothing yet, as today.
- A return to peace, and the clause letting an event's answer declare war, cut from the page: the helper the attack goes through is there for content that later composes it.
- An enemy capturing the neutral's city, the idea's.
- The hostile units pillaging: the raider prepares the capture alone, and which script pillages is content.

Corner cases decided:

- A landing on the neutral's city tile is a landing on a tile it holds: war is declared and the unit stands there.
- A unit of the player's whose every way out runs through tiles the neutral has claimed around it is shut in at peace: it leaves by declaring war or not at all, and the ring says so.
- A unit of the player's standing on a tile the neutral claims around it at peace, or entered there through the console's `unit` entry, declares nothing by standing: the declaration is the landing.
- The neutral's unit entered while hostile enters under the guard script, which acts as the raider until the chronicle ends; the wave script a unit carries from a wave sent at peace acts as the raider too.
- A hostile unit of the neutral's standing on a camp or a site through the enemy phase still captures it, dealing nothing, as at peace.
- Hostile, the neutral's city still claims and still grows; a tile a unit of the player's stands on is no claim of its, as today.
- A save written while the warning stands holds nothing of it: the warning is the screen's. A save from before this line holds no relations and no `prepared` on the neutral's units; it needs no care.
- A camp's roll or a raid during the end of turn changes none of this; the proof spec ends no turn.

Reconcile, the likenesses and what was chosen:

- The warning goes through the launch warning's door as it is: the menu's warning window, which already takes a title, lines and the through press's label as its parameters, raised from the chronicle screen as the launch warning is from the launch screen. A window of the chronicle screen's own was rejected: two windows for one shape.
- The Hostile line goes through the unit card's state line as it is, beside Raft and Capturing.
- The hostile walk goes through the raider's script, the content delegating as the pillager does; no script key is added to the neutral's content.
- The hostile capture goes through the enemy's prepare and capture as they are, every read of "an enemy" that decides occupy, prepare and capture widened to "an enemy, or the neutral's unit while hostile" behind one predicate; a capture of the neutral's own was rejected: two doors for one defeat.

- The defeat's reading: one entry, the cause unchanged.
