# Predators

**Line:** **Predators** — the Stone Age's wolves, dealt on every map, no need named: _Abandon a herd_ removes deer and cattle inside the border, one and one more for every ten turns, drawn seeded among those standing, with what stands on them, and is refused while no deer or cattle tile stands inside the border; _Let the farmers handle it_ adds the challenge **Wolves** to the discard pile, which kills one population at the end of every turn it stays in the hand; _Defend the herds_ pays military for each herd the wolves would take, whatever the border holds. An answer may name what refuses it on the chronicle, as a card's aim does. Done when the event stands on both Stone schedules, `npm test` is green with the coherence test and the two mechanism tests among it, `e2e/predators.spec.ts` passes, and the two pages below say so.
