# Influence cards

**Line:** **Influence cards** — two kinds of card no deck holds, the trophy and the challenge, each declaring the influence it gains, paid into the campaign when the chronicle ends with the card still in it, a trophy's on any ending and a challenge's on victory alone; the camp's and the neutral's rewards of both ages promoted to trophies; the ending screen's ledger, the launch warning and Continue reading the cards kept. Done when `paidInto` pays the trophies and the challenges kept in any pile, proven on the fixture; the Stone camp's and the neutral's rewards are trophies gaining 1 influence each and the Nomadic camp's trophies gaining none, the catalogue coherence test passing; `e2e/ending.spec.ts` reads a trophy's row in the ledger; and the pages below say so.

**Scope:**

- In: the two kinds in the rules, each card of them declaring `influence`, an integer of nought or more, refused otherwise when the catalogue is built; a card of either kind is held by no deck by its kind, carries no keyword and is of no civilization's section; a trophy is played through the aim its content declares, as an instant is, and goes nowhere once played; a challenge is aimed at nothing and blocked by a block of its own, so it is drawn unplayable in the hand and its drag raises the note. The ending pays every trophy kept in the draw pile, the hand, the discard pile or the exhaust pile, and every challenge kept there on a victory; the payment carries the cards kept, each with its copies and what they paid, for the ledger. The Nomadic camp's `stores` and `band-joins` become trophies gaining 0. The Stone Age brings `stone-stores` and `stone-band-joins`, trophies gaining 1, the Stone camp's rewards one copy each; `their-land` and `their-stores` become trophies gaining 1, and the neutral deals `stone-band-joins`, `their-land` and `their-stores`, three copies each. The face's foot chip, the ledger rows, the launch warning and Continue's lines.
- Out: the sites' rewards, which stay instants until their own line; any real challenge card, the neutral's grievance line authoring the first; a challenge that strikes. The sorting order of the kinds puts the two after the hazard.
- Corner cases decided here: a trophy gaining 0 stands in the ledger and on Continue with its name and no number, as an achievement that pays none does, and wears no chip; on a defeat a challenge kept stands in the ledger greyed with no number; the ledger lists the achievements first, then the cards in the order of the collection; the hand's Fire discards a challenge like any other card, which then cycles on; a trophy spent is gone and pays nothing. The capture window's count is unchanged, two cards for the camp and three for the neutral.
- Reconcile: the banished-once-played reward instants and the trophy became one, the trophy, for every camp's and neutral's reward of both ages, the Nomadic ones at 0; the hazard and the challenge stay apart, meant; the ledger's achievement rows and card rows are one row shape; the warning's lines and Continue's lines stay the one list they are today, extended.

**Traps:**

- `src/content/stone.ts` builds the Stone camp by spreading the Nomadic camp (`...camp`), rewards included: the Stone camp's `rewards` is written after the spread. The neutral's `band-joins` there is the Nomadic card and must become `stone-band-joins`, or the neutral's Capture would gain nothing.
- `card.kind` is a closed set switched at eight sites (`src/rules/cards.ts`, `src/rules/catalogue.ts`, `src/rules/campaign.ts`, `src/ui/face.ts`); the typecheck lists them.
- `heldByNoDeck` in `src/rules/catalogue.ts` derives "no deck holds it" from the reward tables and the hazard kind; the two new kinds say it by kind, and the table reading stays for the sites' rewards, which are instants still.
- `landed()` in `e2e/chronicle-screen.ts` builds a Nomadic win; a chronicle holds a card of any age, so a Stone trophy added to a pile through `addedToDrawPileTop` before the win is the spec's fixture, and its row reads 1 while the Nomadic trophies read none.
- The ending ledger and the launch screen's Continue each read `chronicle.achievements` on their own today (`src/ui/ending-screen.ts`, `src/ui/launch-screen.ts`'s `reachedOf`); the warning takes the same lines Continue reads (`warnOfLaunch` in `src/ui/menu-scene.ts`).
- `src/content/catalogue.test.ts` resolves every reward id (`capturedIn`); the fixture's `PH_Spoils`, `PH_Cache` and `PH_Relic` in `src/rules/fixtures.ts` are the rewards the rules tests play, and `PH_Relic` is the site's, which the sites line decides for the real content, not this one.
- The glossary lint hook reads code and text for forbidden words; "prize", "loot", "relic" and "keepsake" become forbidden by the trophy row, and `PH_Relic` is a fixture id.
- Anything on the Phaser side: `docs/PHASER.md`.
