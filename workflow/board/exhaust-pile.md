# An exhaust pile

**Line:** An exhaust pile — a card exhausted goes to the exhaust pile, as the card it became where it becomes one, and a card banished is gone for good; the two keywords replace single use on the cards and in the glossary, a building card exhausting and a settle card played and a hazard paid banished by kind, and a card no deck holds carrying exhaust refused by the catalogue; on the chronicle screen the exhaust pile is an upright tab out of the screen's right edge beside the discard pile, reading its count, browsed by the right click, an exhausted card flying to it from the hand. Doc-impact: docs/CHRONICLE.md, docs/CHRONICLE-SCREEN.md, docs/GLOSSARY.md, docs/ages/NOMADIC.md.

**Spec:**

_`docs/GLOSSARY.md`, the table._ The **single use** row is deleted. Three rows are added, in its place and after **become**:

- `| **exhaust** | A keyword on a card, and a building card's own once played: played, it goes to the exhaust pile instead of the discard pile. | single use, one-use, consume, consumed, exile, remove (for a card played), spend (for a card) |`
- `| **exhaust pile** | The cards exhausted this chronicle, out of the cycle and kept in view. | removed pile, used pile, exile pile, void |`
- `| **banish** | To take a card out of the chronicle for good: a keyword on a card, and a settle card's own once played and a hazard's once paid. | gone (for a card), vanish, purge, burn, destroy (for a card) |`

_`docs/CHRONICLE.md` → The settle phase._ The sentence "The section holds settle cards and nothing else, and a settle card played leaves the chronicle instead of going to the discard pile." becomes "The section holds settle cards and nothing else, and a settle card played is banished."

_`docs/CHRONICLE.md` → Cards, the opening paragraph._ From "Every kind cycles:" to "The map's rewards are chronicle-only by nature." the paragraph's tail reads:

> Every kind cycles: played or discarded, a card goes to the discard pile and comes around again. There are two ways out of the cycle. A card **exhausted** goes to the **exhaust pile**, a pile of the chronicle's own that keeps it out of the cycle and in view; a card **banished** is out of the chronicle for good, kept nowhere. Which way a card takes is its own: a building card played is exhausted, a settle card played and a hazard paid are banished, as their entries say, and a card of any other kind carries **exhaust** or **banish** as a keyword, or neither; a card carrying either and discarded unplayed comes around like any other. A card no deck may hold carries no exhaust, so the exhaust pile holds only what a deck could have dealt. The deck's settle cards never cycle: in hand when the chronicle opens, played on the settle phase alone, a settle card is banished once played, and the end of the settle phase banishes the ones left in hand. **Becomes** is a keyword too: a card that carries it names another card, and played, it goes to its pile as that card, made as its content makes it, the exhaust pile where it is exhausted and the discard pile otherwise; discarded unplayed, it comes around as it is. The map's rewards are chronicle-only by nature.

_`docs/CHRONICLE.md` → Cards, the entries._ Settle: "in hand on the settle phase alone, leaving the chronicle once played," becomes "in hand on the settle phase alone, banished once played,". Building: "A building card builds once: played, it leaves the chronicle instead of going to the discard pile, so the copies a deck holds are how many of that building the city can build, and a copy bought in the meta makes the city bigger; it carries no keyword for it, leaving being the kind's own." becomes "A building card builds once: played, it is exhausted, so the copies a deck holds are how many of that building the city can build, and a copy bought in the meta makes the city bigger; it carries no keyword for it, exhausting being the kind's own." Hazard: "and paying its cost is what takes it out of the chronicle." becomes "and paying its cost is what banishes it."

_`docs/CHRONICLE.md` → Cards, the card aimed at the hand._ "The card played goes to the discard pile before it lands, so a card it discards lies over it." becomes "The card played goes to its pile before it lands, the discard pile or the exhaust pile, so a card it discards lies over it."

_`docs/CHRONICLE-SCREEN.md` → The cards → The piles._ After "A left click on a pile does nothing of its own." add:

> The exhaust pile is no stack of cards: it is an upright tab coming out of the screen's right edge beside the discard pile, 32 wide and 16 from the pile, as tall as a resting card and standing on its baseline, its word and its count read upwards, and the discard pile and the end-turn button keep from the tab the margin they keep from the screen's edge elsewhere. It answers the right click with its browse and the left click with nothing, as a pile does, and rests on nothing. A card exhausted flies from the hand to the tab as a discarded card flies to the discard pile, turning over into the card it became on the way, and the tab's count ticks once it has landed; a card banished leaves the hand where it lies, with no flight.

"Single use in both, so a captured camp leaves no dead card cycling through a small deck." becomes "Banish on both, so a captured camp leaves no dead card cycling through a small deck."

Corner cases decided here: the pile's rule reads the card as it lands, so a card that becomes another is judged on the card it became, and no content today crosses that line; a card carrying exhaust or banish and discarded unplayed comes around like any other, as single use does today; a hazard discarded unplayed cycles as today and only a paid one is banished; the order of a play is unchanged, the card leaves the hand for its pile and then its cost is paid and its effect lands, so an effect never meets its own card in the hand; the exhaust pile holds cards at their counters, and its browse stacks them as the other browses do, a copy reading apart standing apart; an exhausted card that becomes another flies to the tab turning over into the card it became, as the discard flight already does; the pointer over the tab is the arrow, as over the draw pile.

Reconcile: the exhausted card's flight goes through the discard flight with one more destination, the tab's place; the browse goes through the pile browse as it is, a third pile kind; the tab's presses go through a pile's, a right click browsing and a left click doing nothing, with nothing to rest on; the count in the tab's word and the piles' pill stay apart, the user's choice until v0.0.6.

**Traps:**

- `src/rules/cards.ts` today answers what a card lies on the discard pile as, and answers nothing where the card leaves the chronicle before it reads becomes: that is the cross this line undoes. Where a card goes and what it goes as are two facts, read apart.
- `src/rules/stages.ts` holds the closed sets of change names, and every listener in `src/ui/` switches over them with no default; the rename and the new name are found by the typecheck, which is the point.
- The hand's lane in `src/ui/hand.ts` is symmetric today, both edges one margin plus a card plus the lane's pad from the screen's edges; the discard pile moving left makes it asymmetric, and the lane's right edge is read from where the discard pile stands.
- The discard pile's place in `src/ui/piles.ts` is where the shuffle lifts from, where the discard flight lands and where the e2e helpers find the pile's top; moving it moves all of them, and nothing else reads the screen's right margin for a card but the end-turn button in `src/ui/chronicle-scene.ts`, whose shown-event strip stands over it and as wide as it.
- The piles' count ticks on a render the discard change triggers once its block has landed, in `src/ui/piles.ts`; the tab's count follows that mechanism, never the change itself.
- A colour is named for its meaning: the tab's paper, edge and ink are entries of their own in `src/ui/look.ts`, borrowing the panel's and the ink's values, as the one-accent rule the user holds until v0.0.6 says.
- The e2e helpers in `e2e/chronicle-screen.ts` address a pile by its top card's name and browse it by a right click on that top; the tab has no top, so the helper that browses takes the tab by its own name for the third pile.
- A text drawn upright, a zone that takes the right click, and when a new object first accepts a press: `docs/PHASER.md`, read before asserting any of it.
- The save in `src/rules/save.ts` reads each pile by its field; a chronicle without the new field is not a chronicle's shape and is refused and dropped at the boot, the campaign standing, which is the standing rule and needs no fallback.
- The forge in `tools/` writes a campaign with no chronicle and reads no pile.
