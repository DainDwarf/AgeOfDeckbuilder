# Board

A shrinking list whose goal is zero, in two sections. **Rungs** are the version's deliverables, in order, each a title and one sentence: they land when the version opens, or through `/todo` on the user's order, and when a rung's turn comes `/intake` cuts it into lines and deletes it, the lines being its trace. **Lines** are one item each; **priority is order; completion is deletion.** A line reaches this file only through `/todo`, on the user's order, or a rung's cut, as a title and one sentence; a passing thought goes to [`IDEAS.md`](IDEAS.md) instead. `/intake` then settles the line's design with the user and gives it a done-condition, machine-verifiable where possible, its doc-impact, and a dossier `board/<slug>.md` holding its contract — spec, scope, traps, plan — written once, on the settled state, and deleted with the line. A line with a dossier link is ready to ship; one without waits for intake. A line that cannot be completed is documentation — it moves to `docs/`.

Before intake: `- **Title** — what it is about.` After intake: `- **Title** — done-condition. Doc-impact: <`docs/` pages, or none>. [dossier]`

## Rungs

- **The stand-ins' doors** — the fixture content that opens a door no real content walks through — the recall, the road, the siege capstone with its second script and its span, and whatever else the search finds — reassessed one by one: the door kept for content the design foresees, or the mechanism and its fixture cut together.
- **The balance pass** — the Stone Age's numbers, measured through the simulator and felt in play, the findings kept in [`BALANCE.md`](BALANCE.md) first among them; Domestication's goal, which a worker standing on one herd reaches, made a focus, Tanning's need of two herds inside the border weighed, Fishing's need of 50 food from coast and the Fishery's gain on the coast around it weighed, and Trapping, Irrigation, Pasture and Clay pit, four improvements of one price and one gain, weighed against one another; a trial of where military comes from: military taken off the Hearth's yield and flint giving it in place of production.

## Lines

- **The change carries the chronicle before** — every stage, a change or a group, carries the chronicle it was raised on beside the one it leaves; the three achievement tallies that walked the stages keeping a chronicle of their own read it off the stage instead; one test on the fixture holds the invariant across a command's stages. Doc-impact: none — the stage's shape is `DOGMAS.md`'s. [board/change-carries-before.md](board/change-carries-before.md)
- **The row** — the row reads the chronicle as it ended and what the flow did on the way: the influence the ending paid, the capstone drawn and its turn, the stocks, the population, the tiles held, the neutral's population, the units standing and the units killed each by faction and kind, the cards played with their count and the cards in the piles never played, every deal with the entry taken and its turn, and every achievement's count and the turn it was reached; the header is fixed per cell, the fixed columns one value each and the variable ones one cell each in `learned`'s shape; the `sim` skill reports the new columns. Doc-impact: [`../docs/SIMULATOR.md`](../docs/SIMULATOR.md). [board/the-row.md](board/the-row.md)
- **The fold** — the second verb: tables from one file of rows, and two files compared seed by seed where their cells pair; the `sim` skill gains the verb.
- **The goal gambit** — `--goal <technology>` on top of any persona: among the commands the persona would take, the one that raises that achievement's count; the `sim` skill gains the flag.
- **The deck's edits** — copies bought and cards added or removed in the cell's campaign, by flags or a deck file, through the door the forge builds its campaign by, so the forge gains them too; the `sim` skill gains them.
- **The builder** — the persona that works the land and the border; its gambits are served as sentences to strike at its intake.
- **The warlord** — the persona that fields units and goes for the camps; its gambits are served at its intake, the floor of population a unit card leaves among them.
- **The profile** — a builder's hundred seeds profiled and what the profile justifies landed: the reachability cache, the end of turn's cost per `apply`, the split of a sweep across processes; a rules change among them is its own commit under its own review.
- **The sweep** — a knob sweep over the cell's own parameters, one cell per value, and the ablation of one gambit at a time, one cell per gambit; the `sim` skill gains both.
