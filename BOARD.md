# Board

A shrinking list whose goal is zero. One line per item; **priority is order; completion is deletion.** A line reaches this file only through `/todo`, on the user's order, as a title and one sentence; a passing thought goes to [`IDEAS.md`](IDEAS.md) instead. `/intake` then settles the line's design with the user and gives it a done-condition, machine-verifiable where possible, its doc-impact, and a dossier `board/<slug>.md` holding its contract — spec, scope, traps, plan — written once, on the settled state, and deleted with the line. A line with a dossier link is ready to ship; one without waits for intake. A line that cannot be completed is documentation — it moves to `docs/`.

Before intake: `- **Title** — what it is about.` After intake: `- **Title** — done-condition. Doc-impact: <`docs/` pages, or none>. [dossier]`

- **Turn-0 cards are settle cards** — every card of a deck's settle section is of the settle kind and the catalogue refuses any other, the stand-in's free claim among them reading its kind as the settle card does, and the turn-0 clause is gone from the page and the rules. Doc-impact: `docs/CHRONICLE.md`, `docs/GLOSSARY.md`, `docs/ages/NOMADIC.md`. [board/turn-0-cards-are-settle-cards.md](board/turn-0-cards-are-settle-cards.md)
- **Events deal their answers** — inert, on the stand-in and the fixture alike: a due turn deals one event and the event deals its answers, two or more, as cards; the take of an answer pays its cost and lands it, and an unaffordable answer is refused; a capture deals the camp's rewards in the same window, before the turn's event, and the one taken is laid in the discard pile; the catalogue refuses an event with no answer costing no stock. Doc-impact: `docs/CHRONICLE.md`. [board/events-deal-their-answers.md](board/events-deal-their-answers.md)
- **The capstone passes on a condition** — inert, on the stand-in: the schedule's capstone names the condition that passes it, read at the end of every turn from its landing, the stand-in's siege passing on its turn count as before and a fixture capstone passing on a building standing; the timeline is rolled a deal ahead from a generator of its own and never runs out, so a chronicle has no last turn. Doc-impact: `docs/CHRONICLE.md`, `docs/GLOSSARY.md`. [board/capstone-passes-on-a-condition.md](board/capstone-passes-on-a-condition.md)
- **The camps roll their own warriors** — inert, on the stand-in: at every enemy phase each camp whose tile is free rolls, seeded, whether a warrior enters on it, the odds content on the camp.
- **The map's scripts** — inert, on the stand-in: a terraform removes the improvements the new terrain does not admit, a settle card that enters a unit is aimed at a tile and takes no inhabitant, and the helpers an answer needs to kill the inhabitant working a tile and to damage the units standing on it.
