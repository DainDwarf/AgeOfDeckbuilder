# The map's scripts

**Line:** The map's scripts — inert, on the stand-in: nothing sees and no unit acts on turn 0, the centre part the whole of its sight; a settle card that enters a unit is aimed at a charted tile the unit stands on with no unit on it and takes no inhabitant; a terraform removes the improvements and the building the new terrain does not admit; and an answer has a helper to kill the inhabitant working a tile and one to damage the unit standing on it, the fixture proving each. Doc-impact: `docs/CHRONICLE.md`, `docs/GLOSSARY.md`.

**Spec:** `docs/CHRONICLE.md` → _The rules_ → _The turn_, _Sight_, _Cards_ and _The map_; `docs/GLOSSARY.md` → the **killed** row. The sentences are written against the pages as the four lines before this one leave them. Written out:

- _The turn_, the opening paragraph. Replace "None of the cycle below runs on turn 0." with: "None of the cycle below runs on turn 0, and no unit moves or attacks on it: a unit entered on turn 0 stands where it entered until turn 1."
- _Sight_, the turn-0 paragraph, whole: "**On turn 0 the centre part of the map is in sight**, and it is the only sight there is: nothing sees on turn 0 — not the city once it stands, not a unit entered on it — so nothing is charted before turn 1 but the centre part, and the settle is chosen on what it shows. From turn 1 the city and its units see for themselves, and the centre part falls into fog wherever they do not."
- _Cards_, the **Settle** bullet. Replace "One that enters a unit takes no inhabitant: the band arrives with its units." with: "One that enters a unit is aimed at a charted tile the unit can stand on with no unit on it, and puts the unit there with its move points and action full; it takes no inhabitant: the band arrives with its units."
- _The map_, the **Building** bullet. After "That slot is the whole difference between a building and an improvement." add: "A building names its terrains as an improvement does, and a terraform that reaches it — an event's, since a worker's is refused on a filled slot — keeps it through a terrain it stands on and removes it with one it does not."
- `GLOSSARY.md` → **killed**, meaning: "What happens to a unit that loses, or to an inhabitant an event kills: the unit leaves the map, the inhabitant the city." The _Not_ column stays.

Player-facing text, one sentence: the refusal a card aimed at a tile answers when a unit already stands there, read by the refusal note as every `refusal.<block>` entry is. The block's key is the implementer's (`standing` suggested); its sentence is: `A unit already stands there.` No other text: the fixture's cards have no text, and the stand-in gains no card.

**Doc-impact:** `docs/CHRONICLE.md`, `docs/GLOSSARY.md`. `docs/ages/NOMADIC.md` holds as written: its settle paragraph and Wildfire defer to the rules.

**Scope:**

- In: `inSight` answering the centre part and nothing else on turn 0 — no city, no unit, no held tile sees before turn 1, so a settle on the centre part's edge and a unit entered there chart nothing until the tick; one rules function owning what a unit of the player's may do by hand — no landing and no target on turn 0, `reachable` and `attackable` from turn 1 — that the `move` command, the `attack` command and the map's lighting all read, a move or an attack on turn 0 being one `refused` stage; one helper that a settle card composes to enter a unit: aimed at a tile, the kind's charted check first, then the tile's terrain the unit's stats stand on (`standsOn`, the `terrain` block), then no unit standing there (the new block), the effect entering the unit through `entered` on that tile, the player's, taking no inhabitant; `terraformed` removing every improvement whose kind does not name the new terrain and the building whose kind does not stand on it, keeping the ones that do, the feature going as today; one helper that kills the inhabitant working a tile — the population one fewer and the tile unassigned, the chronicle untouched where nobody works it; one helper that damages the unit standing on a tile by an amount — its health less the amount, killed at nought or below as an attack kills, whatever its faction, the chronicle untouched where no unit stands there; the new `TileBlock` member with its `refusal.` entry; the fixture catalogue gaining a settle card that enters a `PH_Worker`, held by no fixture deck's section by default, and a fixture event whose answers exercise the two answer helpers and a terraform of a built tile, dealt through `dealing`; the tests in _Plan_; the two `docs/` pages.
- Out: any change to the stand-in's content — its settle section, its cards, its events and its text stay, and no spec sees a unit on turn 0 until the Nomadic content lands; the Nomadic settle section, Wildfire and Departure themselves (the age's content line); a helper that takes an idle-or-working inhabitant (Departure's, the content line's); a unit entering spent or any rule on a unit's move points on turn 0 beyond the refusal — the tick refreshes what is already full.
- Corner cases decided here: a unit entered on turn 0 sees nothing and acts on nothing until turn 1, and the settle is chosen on the centre part alone whatever was entered first; a settle unit card is played before or after the settle alike, on the city's own tile included while no unit stands there, and on a tile with a building in its slot, as a unit stands on a camp; a settle unit card is refused on no count of the city's population, which is nought before the settle; the terraform helper drops layers by the kind's terrains alone, so the stand-in's settle, which terraforms the tile into urban, would drop a mine and keep a road — nothing stands on a tile at the settle, since the generator lays no improvement, so the stand-in plays as today; a camp on a terrain the new one is not among goes with the terraform like any building; the kill helper takes the city's last inhabitant when told to, the population defeat following on the take's last stage as `resolved` has it, and an answer that spares the last one says so itself; the kill helper on the city's own tile kills the inhabitant working it like any tile; the damage helper kills at nought exactly, as `attacked` does, and damages a unit of the player's, an enemy and a worker alike; a unit killed by an answer leaves the map on the `events` stage, with no stage of its own.

**Traps:**

- This line ships after "The camps roll their own warriors" and is written against the code the four lines before it leave: the settle kind takes any aim (`aimOf` wraps the tile aim's refusal behind `chartedTile`), an event is a record of answers taken through the take command, the timeline is rolled a deal ahead, and `dealing` in `src/rules/fixtures.ts` hands in a deal. Where those lines left something else, read the code, not this file.
- `inSight` in `src/rules/sight.ts` adds the units' sight whatever the turn and the city's once it stands; its turn-0 branch today adds the centre part on top of them. On turn 0 the centre part becomes the whole answer. `charted` runs on every stage, so nothing else needs to know the turn.
- `src/rules/sight.test.ts` has the test "the centre part stands in sight through turn 0, and from turn 1 falls into fog wherever the city does not see it"; it settles at the centre part's edge and reads a far tile of the centre part, which holds under the new rule. Any test or spec that reads a tile the settle's own sight charted on turn 0 beyond the centre part changes: `src/rules/chronicle.test.ts` and `e2e/fog.spec.ts` are the two to read for one. The fixture's `opening` reaches two and the city sees two, so a settle on the centre tile charts nothing new either way.
- `lightUnit` in `src/ui/map.ts` reads `reachable` and `attackable` straight from `src/rules/units.ts`, and `move` and `attack` in `src/rules/chronicle.ts` read the same two; the turn-0 refusal is one function all three go through, or the map lights on turn 0 what the rules refuse. The enemy script `ADVANCE` in `src/content/stand-in.ts` reads `reachable` too, and the enemy phase never runs on turn 0; `reachable` takes a `Crossed` that carries no turn.
- `refusal-note.ts` and `card-face.ts` in `src/ui/` read `refusal.${block}` for every block; a `TileBlock` member with no entry throws on screen, and the stand-in's coherence test covers no fixture block. The entry lands with the member.
- `terraformed` in `src/rules/cards.ts` is what the settle card composes on the fixture and the stand-in, wrapped around `settled`, which builds the city after the terraform; the order stays, so the city's building is never dropped by the terraform that precedes it.
- `src/rules/cards.test.ts` has the test "a terraformed tile loses its feature and keeps the improvements on it", which promises the old behaviour on a mine, whose kind names hills alone, under a terraform to urban. It is rewritten to the page's promise, not weakened: the mine goes, and a road, whose kind names urban, stays.
- The `events` stage in `src/ui/map.ts` plays units arriving; a unit killed by an answer must leave the map on that stage with no stale token, checked by reading, since no stand-in answer kills one.
- `withTile` and `builtOn` in `src/rules/fixtures.ts` are how a test lays an improvement or a building on a tile; `standing` and `worker` put units on one, and `settledOn` plays the first settle card of the hand, which is the city's on every fixture deck.

**Plan:**

2. `src/rules/units.ts` or `src/rules/chronicle.ts` — one function owns what a unit of the player's may do by hand on the chronicle, nothing on turn 0; `move`, `attack` and the map's `lightUnit` read it; the precedent is `cityCommand`, the one decision the screen and `apply` share.
3. `src/rules/state.ts` — the new `TileBlock` member, listed in the type's comment; `src/ui/text.ts` — its `refusal.` entry, the sentence in _Spec_.
4. `src/rules/cards.ts` — the helper a settle unit card composes, as in _Scope_, beside `enters`; `terraformed` dropping the layers the new terrain does not admit, its comment saying which stay; the two answer helpers, beside the other helpers an answer composes (`raided`, `laid` in `src/rules/schedule.ts`, or here — the implementer's cut).
5. `src/rules/fixtures.ts` — the settle unit card and the fixture event of _Scope_.
6. Tests — `src/rules/sight.test.ts`: the settle at the centre part's edge charts nothing beyond it on turn 0 and does from the tick; a unit entered on turn 0 at the edge the same. `src/rules/cards.test.ts`: the settle unit card admits every charted tile a worker stands on with no unit on it, refuses an uncharted tile, a mountain and a tile a unit stands on by their blocks, and played puts the worker there full, population unchanged, the card in no pile, before the settle and after it; a move and an attack of that unit on turn 0 are one `refused` stage and the function the map reads answers nothing, then from turn 1 it moves; the terraform test rewritten as in _Traps_, plus a building dropped and one kept through a fixture answer. `src/rules/schedule.test.ts` or `src/rules/cards.test.ts`: the kill helper on a worked tile, on an unworked one, and on the city's last inhabitant ending the chronicle; the damage helper hurting, killing at nought, and touching nothing on an empty tile.
