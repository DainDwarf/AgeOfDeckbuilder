# The cycle is groups over changes

**Line:** The cycle is groups over changes — the end of turn, the take, the city's acts and the unit's commands resolve as groups over the changes they make, income per tile, the tick per unit, each enemy's blow an `attack` over its two changes, a due turn that draws no event a `rolled` then a `runtime-error`, an assign an `assign` group over its `assigned` changes; and the fall is taken on the first change that leaves the population at nought, so nothing after it is computed and the guard in `grow` goes. Doc-impact: `docs/DOGMAS.md`.

**Spec:** `docs/DOGMAS.md` → _Code_, the bullets "The game is one pure function", "The flow is a tree of stages", "A group is a name for why, over stages" and "A rules helper that changes the chronicle answers the changes it raised". `docs/CHRONICLE.md` → _The turn_ for the cycle's order, _Units and combat_ for "Population reaching zero is the other defeat, and the city falls on the spot: nothing resolves after it", _Events and the capstone_ for the second script and the deal. `BRANCH.md` → _The design_, the group list as this intake rewrote it: it is the grain below, sentence for sentence. One sentence of `docs/DOGMAS.md` changes, and there is no player-facing sentence.

**Doc-impact:** `docs/DOGMAS.md` → _Code_, the bullet "The flow is a tree of stages": its sentence "One change moves no row: `runtime-error`, a helper called where the content should never have called it, answered in place of the change it could not make." becomes, verbatim: "One change moves no row: `runtime-error`, a content defect met in play — a helper called where the content should never have called it, a due turn that draws no event — answered in place of the change it could not make, and play goes on wherever it can." Nothing else: the vocabulary lives in `src/rules/stages.ts` by the dogma's own pointer, and every sentence of `docs/CHRONICLE.md` on the cycle's order and on the fall stays true.

**Scope:**

This line lands on the shape the two dossiers before it leave: a stage a change or a group over stages, both carrying the chronicle they leave; the two closed sets; `Landed`; the pre-order walk with its cue rule; every helper of `board/every-helper-answers-its-changes.md`'s table answering its changes. The plan is written against those dossiers, not against the code as it stands before they ship.

In: every step of the cycle and every command not yet grouped, at this grain, settled here:

| What | Resolves as |
| --- | --- |
| the end of turn, from turn 1 | one `strike` per hazard, as the second line has it; `discarded` where the hand holds any card; `income`; `grow`; `enemy-phase`; then, where the chronicle still stands, one `camp-capture` per camp captured, in tile order; then the opening, unless a deal stands |
| the opening | the bare `ended` of the victory where the capstone is passed, the last stage, nothing after it; else `turn`; then `capstone` on the turns after the capstone's own where it carries a second script; then the events phase, `capstone` on the capstone's turn and `deal` on a due turn and nothing on any other; then `drawn`, `shuffled`, `drawn`, each where it moved cards, unless a deal stands. The end of turn 0 is the opening alone |
| `income` | one `stock` per assigned tile no enemy occupies whose yield is anything, in tile order, each carrying the tile it came from; the one `stock` that carries a tile |
| `grow` | `stock` then `population` where the food stock reaches the growth threshold; nothing else. The `threshold === 0` guard goes: the fall keeps a city of no population from reaching it |
| `turn` | the `turn` change, the tick; then `left` on turn 0 where settle cards were still in hand; then one `refreshed` per unit in unit order whose move points or action were short of full |
| `enemy-phase` | the one `ended` of a capture where an enemy stands on the city's tile as the phase begins, nothing else in it and nothing after it; else, per enemy in unit order, `move` where it moved and one `attack` group per attack; then the camps' roll, one `enter` per camp whose draw fell under the odds, in tile order, carrying the camp's tile. The roll draws once per free camp whether or not a warrior enters, and a draw that entered none rides on the group's chronicle |
| `attack` | carrying `attacker` and `target`: `action-spent` on the attacker's tile, then `damaged` or `killed` on the target's. The player's attack by hand is the same group at the top level |
| `camp-capture` | carrying `tile`: `retiled` on it, then `dealt` |
| `capstone` | on its turn: `rolled`, then the landing's changes, nothing where it landed nothing; on every turn after, where the capstone carries a second script: what the script raised, nothing where it moved nothing; no group where it carries none |
| `deal` | `rolled`, then `dealt` where an event is drawn, `runtime-error` where none is — no entry weighted above nought on the turn, or no event's need met — the timeline rolled on to its next due turn all the same and play going on; only on a due turn |
| `answer` | `taken`, then the cost as one `stock`, absent where the answer costs nothing, then the landing's changes |
| `reward` | `taken`, then `discarded` |
| `assign` | one `assigned` on the tile, worked or left; a reassign is the same group over two, the tile left then the tile worked |
| `claim` | the culture as one `stock`, then `held` and, where an idle population stands on it, `assigned`, as `bordered` answers them |
| the move by hand | the bare `move` change, as today |
| a refusal, every kind | `refused` holding nothing, as today |
| the fall | on the first change that leaves the population at nought while the city stands, an `ended` change follows it under the same parent, every group above closes on that chronicle, and nothing after it is computed: not the rest of the group, not the rest of the command. A capture is the other `ended`, under `enemy-phase` |

The change set after this line: the second line's, plus `turn`, `dealt` and `taken`; `stock` carries `tile` where a tile yielded it and nothing elsewhere. The group set: the stands line's, plus `enemy-phase`; `assign` stays, now a group over changes. Every name of the design's vocabulary is raised by something once this line ships. `turn` sits in both sets, the group over the change.

Corner cases: a phase the turn always has — `income`, `grow`, `enemy-phase`, `turn` — is staged every turn from turn 1, empty or not, where today a step that changed nothing is absent; `deal` and `capstone` are staged on the turns the design gives them and no other, so a turn that is not due stages nothing for its events phase; `discarded` at the end of turn is a change and is absent where the hand is empty. A reward taken lands in the discard pile from outside the hand and is `discarded` all the same: the change is the pile's, its origin read off the chronicles. `enemy-phase` is empty on a turn with no enemy and no warrior rolled, and its chronicle still carries the camps' draws, so a group's chronicle is its last child's apart from the generator's position. An answer's landing that takes the city's last population ends the chronicle under `answer` and draws no hand. A strike that does the same ends it under that `strike`, and no phase follows. The victory's `ended` is bare and last, as today. The end of turn 0 stages `turn` over the tick, the settle cards `left`, no `refreshed` since the units entered full, then the events phase and the draw as on any turn. An enemy that neither moves nor attacks raises nothing under `enemy-phase`. `cityCommand` and `cityDrag` stay the one decision the screen and `apply` answer a click and a drag by; a refused assign, reassign or claim is `refused`.

Out: the sight's charting per change is the stands line's and stays as it left it; no helper of the second line's table changes what it answers, `yielded` excepted for the tile it now carries; no content file changes in what its closures do; the e2e harness reads stages as it does today.

**Traps:**

- The stands line and the second line are dossiered, not shipped, when this dossier is written. If either shipped a shape other than its dossier names — the walk, the sets, the `Landed` list, the helpers' grain — the report says so before anything is built on it.
- `resolved` finds the fall today by scanning the top level for the first stage whose chronicle falls, replacing that stage's chronicle and dropping the rest; `falling` needs the city standing and the ending unset. Under this line the `ended` is raised where the changes are sequenced, inside the tree, and nothing after it is built. Where that sequencing lives is the implementer's; that there is one place every change goes through is the dogma.
- `endOfTurn` and `turnOpened` each build a flat list with a `staged` that drops a step whose chronicle is the one before; `drawn` does the same. `enemyPhase` returns the `capture` stage alone; `campsRolled` hands the chronicle back beside its stages because a draw may enter nothing; `captures` builds each stage by hand; `take` resumes the opening after a reward and the draw after an answer through `resumed`.
- `income` walks `chronicle.tiles` in tile order and skips an occupied tile; `yielded` refuses a tile the map does not hold. `grow` guards `threshold === 0`; `arrived` is the population change a card's effect uses. `claim` pays the culture inline and calls `bordered`.
- `events` answers three phases today, one of them tagged `'no-deal'`, an internal tag and not a stage; under this line the non-due turn answers nothing, the tag goes with the phase, a due turn drawing nothing raises `runtime-error` under `deal` on both of `eventDrawn`'s empty paths, and `continued` is the second script, `unchanged` where the capstone carries none. The generator's step on the path where no need is met stays, so the turns a seed deals on do not follow the play.
- The resource bar answers `income` and `grow` by tweening every reading whose painted value differs from the stage's chronicle, which is right at the group cue, since a group's chronicle is the phase's outcome. Left so, the walk then renders it on every `stock` and `population` leaf under them, as it renders every part that answers nothing for a leaf, and the reading tweened to the outcome snaps back to each leaf's in turn. The bar plays the group and claims the leaves under `income` and `grow` with a resolved promise, rendering nothing for them, as the overlay claims a stage that leaves a deal standing: one rise per phase, as today. The end-turn button rolls on the `turn` group and answers nothing for the leaves under it, which renders the turn it already rolled to.
- The map lunges on `attack` reading `attacker` and `target`, bumps the target and shrinks it where the stage's chronicle holds no unit there, and answers nothing for `damaged` today. It keeps answering nothing for `damaged` and `killed`, under an `attack` or not: the bump lives in the attack, and a fire's kill repaints as it does now.
- The scene cuts the play-out at the top-level `capstone` stage, commits its chronicle, raises the window through `overlay.land`, and plays the rest once it closes. Under this line the overlay answers the `capstone` group whose chronicle's turn is the capstone's turn with the window's promise, the children walk once it resolves, and the cut goes; a `capstone` group on a later turn is answered by nothing. The window rises on the screen as it stood before the landing, since a group cue commits nothing.
- The overlay answers a resolved promise, on purpose and without rendering, for any stage whose chronicle leaves a deal standing. The `deal` group and its `dealt` leaf both do; its `rolled` leaf does not and renders. `camp-capture` and its `dealt` leaf do too. Keep it.
- `charting` runs per leaf over the tree and drops the units from the snapshots on the leaf whose chronicle's turn differs from the one before: that leaf is now the `turn` change inside the `turn` group. `chartedOn` switches the change set, `charted` alone answering a tile.
- Every switch over the closed sets — the six UI switches and `chartedOn` — must list `turn`, `dealt`, `taken` among the changes and `enemy-phase` among the groups, and the `stock` case must accept the tile.
- Tests assert stage-name sequences in `chronicle.test.ts`, `enemies.test.ts`, `city.test.ts`, `schedule.test.ts`, `sight.test.ts`, `units.test.ts` and `cards.test.ts`, and through `stagedBy` in `fixtures.ts`, which reads the top level. `enemies.test.ts` filters `attack`, `move`, `camp-capture` and `enter` off the flat list to read what they carry; `sight.test.ts` finds the end of turn's `attack`; `units.test.ts` narrows the attack by hand to read `attacker` and `target`. Each assertion takes the table's grain, the children read through the walk as the stands line has tests read them; none is weakened. One test changes what it promises by the user's decision at this intake: "a stage of the end of turn that changed nothing is left out of it" becomes the phases standing empty and `discarded` absent on an empty hand.
- `e2e/end-of-turn.spec.ts` reads `drawn` and `shuffled` at the top level; `e2e/map.spec.ts` reads the move by hand and `deal` there. Neither changes here. Specs that assert after an end of turn wait on `playedOut`; the play-out holds more leaves, most rendered at once, and a spec that reads before it is a bug in the spec's wait, not a timeout to widen.
- `cause: 'capture'` in a chronicle's ending is a `DefeatCause`, not a stage name.
- `npm run e2e` is refused from a session; the specs run one at a time by name.
