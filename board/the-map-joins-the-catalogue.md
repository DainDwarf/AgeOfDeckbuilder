# The map joins the catalogue

**Line:** **The map joins the catalogue** — the terrains and their numbers, the biomes, the buildings, the features, the improvements and the regions move into the catalogue, the generator reads them through it and a region id, and puts no city on the map; the chronicle's opening takes a map as an argument and settles the centre tile itself, so a test hands in a hand-made map such as a radius 8 all-plain disc; the fixture catalogue carries map content of its own, and the stand-in's coherence test checks every terrain, building, feature and improvement against the UI's names, colours and marks. Doc-impact: `docs/DOGMAS.md`, `docs/CHRONICLE.md`.

**Spec:** `docs/CHRONICLE.md`, _The map_, and `docs/DOGMAS.md`, _Stack_; the whole line lands on the units line's catalogue, and copies its pattern.

- `docs/DOGMAS.md`, _Stack_, the first bullet under the table, as the units line rewrote it: the phrase "the unit kinds, the enemy scripts, what a camp enters, and what the later content brings" becomes, verbatim, "the unit kinds, the enemy scripts, what a camp enters, the terrains and the layers a tile is made of, the biomes and the regions, and what the later content brings". Nothing else on the page changes.
- `docs/CHRONICLE.md`, _The map_: after the paragraph "Biomes are content, like the terrain and feature lists: …" one paragraph is added, verbatim: "A **region** is one composition of all that: the disc's radius, how many biomes it is cut into and which kinds are dealt in what shares, the share of each feature, how many camps and how far they keep from the centre and from one another, and the river layer's numbers; the launch names one, and the generator deals the map it describes." Nothing else on the page changes: the generator's six layers, "the generator puts urban on no tile", the feature deal sparing no tile and the camps keeping from the centre are already written there, and this line is what makes the code agree.
- No player-facing sentence: nothing on screen changes, and no text entry is added.

**Doc-impact:** `docs/DOGMAS.md`, `docs/CHRONICLE.md`.

**Scope:**

In:

- The `Catalogue` type, in `src/rules/catalogue.ts`, gains six tables and one entry, every id an open `string`:
  - `terrains: Record<string, { yields: Partial<Resources>; movementCost?: number; water: boolean; elevation: number; lift: number; river?: Partial<Resources> }>` — one record per terrain, replacing the six parallel tables of `map.ts` (`TERRAIN_YIELDS`, `TERRAIN_MOVEMENT_COST`, `TERRAIN_WATER`, `TERRAIN_ELEVATION`, `TERRAIN_LIFT`, `RIVER_YIELDS`); `movementCost` absent is "named none, crossed by nothing", `river` absent is "a river gives it nothing". Water and movement cost stay two facts though today they agree.
  - `biomes: Record<string, { origin: string; interior: Record<string, number>; rim: Record<string, number>; rimWidths: number[] }>`, as `BIOMES` is today.
  - `buildings`, `features`, `improvements`: as `BUILDINGS`, `FEATURES`, `IMPROVEMENTS` are today, keyed open.
  - `regions: Record<string, Region>`, where a `Region` is today's `MAP_COMPOSITION` with `cityBiome` renamed `centreBiome` and `campFromCity` renamed `campFromCentre`, plus `rivers`, today's `RIVER_FLOW` block whole (`relief`, `roughness`, `perRange`, `climb`, `meander`, `curl`, `edgesPerTile`, `leastEdges`, `draws`). The river numbers live in the region and nowhere else; the user chose that over a catalogue-level block.
  - `camp` gains `building: string`, the building kind a camp is: the generator places it, and every rule that today compares a tile's building to the camp literal reads it here (`enteredFromCamp`, `reinforced`, the siege, `captures`, `claimable`, and every test that writes a camp).
  - `city: { terrain: string; building: string }`, new: what the opening puts on the centre tile until the settle line makes it a card.
- `catalogued` validates the new content, every refusal through the units line's one refusal function: every biome's origin and every key of its interior and rim tables resolve to a terrain, and its `rimWidths` is not empty; every feature's terrain and every building's and improvement's terrains resolve; every region's `centreBiome` and each of its `biomeShares` resolve to a biome and each of its `featureShares` to a feature; `camp.building` resolves and the camp's unit stands on every terrain that building names (this is the check the units dossier parked "until the map line moves it in"; it reads the catalogue's own building now); `city.building` resolves and names `city.terrain` among its terrains. Lookups that throw on an unknown id, for a terrain, a building, a feature, an improvement, a biome and a region — one per table or one generic over them, the implementer's cut.
- `map.ts` keeps the mechanism and loses the content: `MOVE_POINT`, `CITY_TILE`, the axial geometry, the corners, the walks, the six-layer generator. Its four readers of a terrain entry take the catalogue first: `movementCost(catalogue, tile)`, `water(catalogue, terrain)`, `elevation(catalogue, terrain)`, `tileYield(catalogue, tile, rivers)`; `pathCosts` takes it and threads it. `Terrain`, `BuildingTypeId`, `FeatureId`, `ImprovementId` and `Biome` become `string` aliases, and `CITY_TERRAIN`, `TERRAIN_*`, `RIVER_YIELDS`, `BIOMES`, `BUILDINGS`, `FEATURES`, `IMPROVEMENTS`, `MAP_COMPOSITION` and `RIVER_FLOW` go.
- `generateMap(catalogue, region, initial)`: the region id resolves through the lookup, and the generator is city-blind: the centre tile is the centre biome's origin terrain outright and stays so, no tile is made urban, the feature deal spares no tile, and the camps keep `campFromCentre` from the disc's centre. The ten-deal throw stays as it is. It answers `{ rng, tiles, rivers }` as today.
- `beginChronicle(catalogue, seed, deck, map)`, where a map is `{ tiles: Tile[]; rivers: River[] }` and nothing more: the chronicle's generator is `seedRng(seed)` afresh, shuffling the deck and rolling the schedule from it as today. The opening settles the centre tile: its terrain becomes `catalogue.city.terrain`, its building `catalogue.city.building`, its feature gone, whatever the map handed in carried there. A map with no tile at `CITY_TILE` throws. `launched(catalogue, region, seed, deck)`, new beside it in `chronicle.ts`, generates the map from `seedRng(seed)` and opens the chronicle on it: the one place the map and the chronicle share a seed, which the scene and the e2e helpers both call, so a spec's `?seed=` reproduces the screen's chronicle exactly.
- The rules rethread, typecheck-driven as the units line set it: a function takes the catalogue as its first parameter when it, or something it calls, reads it. What reaches the catalogue now: `standsOn`, `reachable`, `inSight`, `charted` (and so `apply`'s charting, `withUnits` and `withTile` in the fixtures), `income`, `claimable`, `tileRefusal`, `cityCommand`, `assign`, `claim`, `captures`, `enteredFromCamp`, `reinforced`, the siege, and the card aims that read `BUILDINGS.PH_Farm.terrains` and the improvements' terrains, through the catalogue the units line already hands every `Aim` closure.
- `src/content/stand-in.ts`: `STAND_IN` gains the seven terrains, the three biomes, the three buildings, the feature, the two improvements with the numbers `map.ts` holds today, one region `PH_Region` with today's composition and river numbers, `camp.building: 'PH_Camp'`, `city: { terrain: 'urban', building: 'PH_City' }`. Its coherence test grows: every terrain has a name in the text table and a colour; every building a name, a mark and a colour; every feature a name, a mark and a colour; every improvement a name and a mark — read through the same lookups the screen uses.
- `src/rules/fixtures.ts`: `CATALOGUE` gains the same ids under numbers of its own, frozen at today's, and one region, `disc`, with today's composition; the generator tests in `map.test.ts` run on `CATALOGUE` and `'disc'`. `cityOf`, `field`, `founded` and the rest write terrains and buildings as they do. Every test that reads its oracle from a table of `map.ts` reads it from `CATALOGUE` instead: the four-layer yield test, the river yield tests, the terraform tests, the siege's `campsApart`, the raid test's radius.
- `map.test.ts`: "the city stands on its tile" becomes "the generator puts urban on no tile, and the centre tile is its biome's origin terrain"; "a feature never on the city's tile" loses its city clause; the camp tests measure from the centre; the camp-terrain test is already gone with the units line, and `catalogue.test.ts`'s check of it reads the catalogue's own building now. `catalogue.test.ts` gains: a catalogue whose biome names a terrain it lacks, whose feature lies on one it lacks, whose region names a biome or a feature it lacks, whose camp's building it lacks, or whose city's building does not stand on the city's terrain, is refused; `generateMap` on a region the catalogue lacks is refused; the opening on a map with no centre tile is refused. `chronicle.test.ts` asserts the opening's centre tile: the city's terrain, the city's building, no feature.
- UI: `ChronicleScene(catalogue, region, seed, deck)`; its `begin` calls `launched`, on the seed asked for or a fresh one, so a new chronicle deals a new map as today. The six colour and mark tables of `src/ui/map.ts` move into the Phaser-free marks module the units line creates, open-keyed, each read through a lookup that throws; `terrainMark`, `buildingMark`, `featureMark` and `improvementMark` wrap them. `cardsOf` in the infopanel takes the catalogue, and the row yields read the catalogue's entries. The yield overlay and city mode's claimable marks pass it on. `src/main.ts` names the stand-in's one region by its id.
- e2e: every `beginChronicle(seed, DECKS.X)` in the specs and `e2e/chronicle-screen.ts` becomes `launched(STAND_IN, region, seed, DECKS.X)` through one helper in `chronicle-screen.ts`; `camps.spec.ts` reads the camp count from `STAND_IN.regions`, `inspect.spec.ts` the feature and the river yields from `STAND_IN`, `worker-instants.spec.ts` the mine's yield from it, `move.spec.ts` and `inspect.spec.ts` hand `movementCost` the catalogue.

Out:

- The cards, the decks and the schedule's entries stay module constants; their lines follow. `PH_Urbanisation`'s terraform literals stay in `cards.ts` as the unit cards' kinds do until the cards line.
- The settle stays the opening's: the city lands on the centre tile, and `CITY_TILE` stays the rules' constant for it. The settle line makes it a command over a small centre part of the map, and retunes `campFromCentre` then.
- `CITY_SIGHT` stays where it is; it is the city's, not the map's.
- The chronicle carries nothing new: a save is its tiles, and the region it was launched on is the meta's to record.
- No number changes. Every rules test yields the same stages on the fixture catalogue as on the constants, with one exception the line owns: the generator is city-blind now, so every generated map differs from today's — the centre tile keeps its origin terrain until the opening, the feature deal covers it, and the shuffle it draws from lands differently. A rules test or a spec pinned to a seed for what its generated map held is re-pinned, never weakened; the specs that search seeds find them afresh.

Corner cases decided: the map's first roll and the chronicle's first roll come from the same stream position, since both start from `seedRng(seed)`; accepted, and stated nowhere else. A map handed to the opening is not validated against the catalogue: it is state, as a save is, and the terrain lookup throws at the first read of an id the catalogue lacks. The opening overwrites whatever the map carried on the centre tile; a hand-made map need not hold an urban tile. A region's `rivers.perRange` of zero is content, not a refusal. The camp's unit standing on the camp's terrains is the validator's; nothing else ties a building to a unit.

**Traps:**

- The units line ships first and this line builds on its `catalogue.ts`, its refusal function, its `STAND_IN`, its `CATALOGUE` and its marks module; the ship session reads that code once, at the ship, and this dossier names shapes, not lines.
- The scene's `newChronicle` generates a map on a fresh seed, so the scene has to hold the region id; the boot cannot generate once and hand a map over.
- `charted` reads elevation through `inSight`, so it takes the catalogue, and with it `apply`'s `charting`, and the fixtures' `withUnits` and `withTile`: the typecheck reaches far from `sight.ts`.
- `src/ui/map.ts` and `src/ui/design-space.ts` import Phaser for real: the colour and mark tables have to live in the Phaser-free marks module for the coherence test in Node to read them.
- `tsconfig.json` sets `noUnusedParameters`: a closure that takes the catalogue and ignores it names the parameter with a leading underscore.
- The camp literal is compared in five rules modules and written by three fixture helpers and several tests; the one reading is `catalogue.camp.building`, and a test that writes a camp writes `CATALOGUE.camp.building`.
- The rim roll and the feature deal draw from the generator in tile order; removing the centre exemption shifts the feature deal's shuffle, so no generated map of today survives byte for byte, and that is expected.
- `e2e/camps.spec.ts` pins seed 1 for "its map is dealt every camp": every seed holds its camps by the redeal, so the pin survives; `RAID` is computed, not pinned.
- `text.ts` is hook-linted for glossary synonyms; no entry is added, and the terrain keys stay `terrain.<id>`.
- `docs/DOGMAS.md`'s Stack bullet and the new `docs/CHRONICLE.md` paragraph are one line each; `npm run lint` refuses a wrapped one.

**Plan:**

1. `src/rules/catalogue.ts`: the six tables, the `camp.building` and `city` entries, the validator's checks, the lookups.
2. `src/rules/map.ts`: the content out, the four readers and `pathCosts` take the catalogue, `generateMap(catalogue, region, initial)` city-blind.
3. `src/rules/units.ts`, `sight.ts`, `city.ts`, `enemies.ts`, `schedule.ts`, `cards.ts`: the rethread the typecheck names, the camp literal replaced by `catalogue.camp.building`.
4. `src/rules/chronicle.ts`: `beginChronicle(catalogue, seed, deck, map)` settling the centre from `catalogue.city`; `launched`; `charting` and `captures` take the catalogue.
5. `src/content/stand-in.ts` and its test.
6. `src/rules/fixtures.ts`, `catalogue.test.ts`, `map.test.ts`, `chronicle.test.ts`, and every rules test the typecheck names.
7. `src/ui/`: the marks module, `map.ts`, `infopanel.ts`, the scene; `src/main.ts`; `e2e/chronicle-screen.ts` and the specs.
8. `docs/DOGMAS.md` and `docs/CHRONICLE.md`, the phrase and the paragraph written out under _Spec_.
