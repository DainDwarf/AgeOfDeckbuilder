# The capstone passes on a condition

**Line:** The capstone passes on a condition — inert, on the stand-in: the capstone names the condition that passes it, read at the end of every turn from its landing, the stand-in's siege passing on its turn count as before and a fixture capstone passing on a building standing; the timeline is rolled a deal ahead from a generator of its own and never runs out, so a chronicle has no last turn. Doc-impact: `docs/CHRONICLE.md`, `docs/GLOSSARY.md`.

**Doc-impact:** `docs/CHRONICLE.md`, `docs/GLOSSARY.md`. `docs/ages/NOMADIC.md` holds as written: its shelter capstone names no span, which is now the rule's plain case.

**Scope:**

- In: the timeline as the schedule it rolls from, a generator of its own, the next deal — its turn, and its event or none — and the capstone's id and turn, its `last` gone; one roll owning both the due turn from the spacing and the draw on it, run by the opening for the first deal and by the events phase after each deal it deals; the capstone's turn landing the capstone alone and rolling the next from itself, whatever was ahead; the capstone carrying `passes`, a closure over the catalogue and the chronicle answering whether it is passed, in place of the schedule's `span`, so the schedule's capstone entry is the id and the window; one rules function owning the check — nothing before the landing turn, the capstone's condition from it — called where the span check stands today, after the enemy phase and the captures, victory ending the chronicle at that end of turn as today; the second script on every turn after the one it lands on, reading no span; the catalogue dropping the span rule; the stand-in's PH_Siege passing on the sixth turn from its landing, and PH_ShortSiege, landing and continuing as PH_Siege does and passing on the second, named by `PH_ShortSchedule`, so the victory spec's turns hold; the fixture gaining a schedule with no entries, which `NO_DEALS` and `dealing` name so a handed-in deal is followed by nothing, and a capstone that lands nothing and passes once a building the fixture names stands on a tile the city holds, with a schedule naming it; the stand-in's coherence test asking `passes` of every capstone on a settled launch.
- Out: a last turn of any kind — a chronicle lost to time was considered and rejected by the user: the chronicle is infinite in principle; the Nomadic shelter (the age's content line); an ordinary event's second script; any screen change — the announcement, the landing's window and the ending screen read the capstone by its id only.
- Corner cases decided here: the condition holding on the landing turn itself ends the chronicle in victory at that turn's end, since the capstone's card is drawn and may be played that turn. A condition true before the landing passes nothing: the guard is the rules', not the closure's. A city that falls in the enemy phase of the turn the condition would hold is defeated; the check never runs on an ended chronicle. The next deal due on the capstone's turn, or past it, is dropped alike: the capstone's turn rolls the next from itself. A due turn no event weighs anything on deals nothing, raises no stage, and makes the turn after it due with a draw of its own and no spacing roll; a schedule with no entries deals nothing forever and loops nowhere, one turn tried per turn. The timeline holds one deal ahead and no more: a test that wants two hands in the first and a schedule whose entries weigh the second.
