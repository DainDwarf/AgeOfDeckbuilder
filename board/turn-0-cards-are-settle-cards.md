# Turn-0 cards are settle cards

**Line:** Turn-0 cards are settle cards — every card of a deck's settle section is of the settle kind and the catalogue refuses any other, the stand-in's free claim among them reading its kind as the settle card does, and the turn-0 clause is gone from the page and the rules. Doc-impact: `docs/CHRONICLE.md`, `docs/GLOSSARY.md`, `docs/ages/NOMADIC.md`.

**Spec:** `docs/CHRONICLE.md` → _The rules_ → _The turn_ and _Cards_; `docs/GLOSSARY.md` → the **settle** row; `docs/ages/NOMADIC.md` → _The settle_. The settle kind is redefined: it is the kind of every card of the settle section, in hand on turn 0 alone, gone once played, and what a settle card does is content, played through whatever aim it declares. The sentences, written out:

Replace "and the hand holds the deck's settle cards, in the deck's order, played on this phase and no other: today the one that **settles** the city on a charted tile that takes a city. An age may add cards to the section, and any card played on turn 0 leaves the chronicle instead of going to the discard pile." with: "and the hand holds the deck's settle cards, in the deck's order, played on this phase and no other: today the one that **settles** the city on a charted tile that takes a city, and the stand-in's free claims beside it. An age adds settle cards to the section and nothing else, and a settle card played leaves the chronicle instead of going to the discard pile."

**Doc-impact:** `docs/CHRONICLE.md`, `docs/GLOSSARY.md`, `docs/ages/NOMADIC.md`.

**Scope:**

- In: the `Card` type's settle member takes any aim, not the tile aim alone; the stand-in's free claim and the fixture's are of the settle kind; the catalogue refuses a settle section that is empty or holds a card of any other kind, and still refuses a settle card among a deck's cards; the play's turn-0 clause is removed, a settle card leaving the chronicle by its kind alone; the tests that promised the old sentences are rewritten to promise the new ones; the fixture catalogue gains one settle card aimed at nothing, so the widened aim is proven on synthetic content.
- Out: any helper that enters a unit without taking an inhabitant — that is the "map's scripts" line's, reworded on the board in this intake; any unit card in the stand-in's settle section; any change to what the free claim does or to where a settle card's unit enters.
- Corner cases decided here: the catalogue can no longer tell the card that settles the city by its kind, so it does not try — whether a deck can settle its city is what the coherence test that settles each deck and reaches turn 1 proves, per catalogue, and the stand-in has that test already. A settle card aimed at a tile still refuses an uncharted tile before its own reasons, whatever it does; one aimed at a unit, at the discard pile or at nothing asks nothing of the kind. Nothing but the settle section can be in hand on turn 0 — no draw, no deal, and an empty discard pile recalls nothing — so the turn-0 clause has no path left once the section holds settle cards alone, and it goes without a replacement.

**Traps:**

- The fixture `settledOn` in `src/rules/fixtures.ts` and the e2e helpers `launch` and `settle` in `e2e/chronicle-screen.ts` find the card that settles the city by its kind being settle. After this line every card of the hand on turn 0 is of that kind, so that lookup answers the first card of the hand. Every deck of the fixture catalogue and of the stand-in lists the city's settle card first, so the answer does not change today; the implementer picks whether the helpers keep reading the first card or name the card by its id, and says which in the report.
- The test in `src/rules/chronicle.test.ts` named "any card played on turn 0 leaves the chronicle, and the hand holds the settle section in the deck's order" builds a settle section holding an instant, which the widened validator refuses at the fixture's `opening`. It is rewritten, not weakened: the same promise on a settle card aimed at nothing — the fixture's new one — or on the free claim, with the hand order still asserted.
- The test in `src/rules/catalogue.test.ts` named "a catalogue whose deck's settle section holds no settle card is refused" keeps both its cases — an empty section and one holding an instant — under the new reason: empty, or a card of another kind.
- `e2e/fog.spec.ts` and `e2e/settle.spec.ts` narrow the settle card's aim to a tile with a thrown error; they keep compiling, since the tile aim is still one member of the widened union.
- The card face reads the kind's label through `kind.<kind>` in `src/ui/text.ts`; nothing in `src/ui/` switches on the settle kind, so the screen needs no edit.
- Sorting a browse by kind lists settle first; no settle card reaches a pile, so nothing on screen changes.
