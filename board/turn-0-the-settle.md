# Turn 0, the settle

**Line:** a chronicle opens on turn 0 with the city standing nowhere, the centre part of the map in sight and the deck's settle cards in hand; a card of the new **settle** kind, aimed at a charted tile that takes a city, puts the city there; ending the turn is refused until it stands, and its end brings turn 1 with none of the cycle run and the settle cards gone; the stand-in deck settles the city as it founds it today, and every spec opens through the settle.

**Spec:** `docs/CHRONICLE.md` → _The turn_ (the opening paragraph), _Sight_, _Cards_ (the kinds), _The map_ (the generation paragraphs, the region sentence, the camps), _Population_, _The chronicle screen_; `docs/GLOSSARY.md` rows **settle**, **deck**.

The rules, as the spec reads them:

- **The opening.** The map is dealt with its **centre part** — every tile within a radius of the disc's centre the region names, 🔧 three — and the timeline rolled. The chronicle opens on turn 0: the city stands nowhere, it has no population, no tile is held, and the hand holds the deck's settle cards, in the deck's order, drawn from no pile. The centre part is in sight for the whole of turn 0, and nothing else is until the city stands; from turn 1 the city's own sight is the only sight, and the centre part falls into fog wherever the city does not see it.
- **The settle kind.** A fifth kind of card, the deck's settle section's own: it is aimed at a tile, admits what its content admits — a charted tile whose terrain takes a city, its slot free — and its effect is the settle: the city stands on that tile from then on, its building in the tile's slot, the founding holding and staffing what the city's content says. A settle card is played on turn 0 alone, which is the only turn it is ever in hand, and once played it leaves the chronicle.
- **Turn 0's play.** Any card in hand may be played on turn 0, and a card played on turn 0 never goes to the discard pile: it leaves the chronicle. A unit card played while the city stands nowhere is refused: the city is not settled. Nothing else is refused for the turn's sake.
- **Turn 0's end.** The turn is ended as any other, and refused while the city stands nowhere. Its end runs none of the cycle: no strike, no discard, no income, no growth, no enemy phase, no capture; the cards left in hand are gone, the turn ticks to 1, and turn 1's events phase and draw follow as on any turn.
- **The catalogue.** A deck is two sections, its cards and its settle cards. A deck whose settle section holds no settle card is refused, and so is a deck whose cards hold one; neither section holds a hazard or the camp's reward. A region whose camps may come within sight of the settle wherever it lands is refused: the camps keep further from the centre than the centre part's radius plus the greater of the city's sight and the rings it holds.

**Scope:**

- In: the centre part on the region, the map and the chronicle; the opening on turn 0; the settle kind, the settle helper and the two aim helpers (charted tile, unsettled city); the deck's two sections and the catalogue's four new refusals; turn 0's play and end; the screen's dead button and absent ring; the boot's `?deck=` taking a deck id alone; the stand-in's `PH_Settle`; both catalogues; the spec helpers and one new spec; the docs edits above.
- Out: any change to what the stand-in city is once settled — it is urban with `PH_City` in its slot, holding and staffing what the previous line made content; a rolled centre; a settle card that is not the stand-in's; the throwaway selection page, which is the next line and takes the boot over.
- Corner cases decided here: the population is the settle's, so a chronicle standing nowhere never falls for having none — the population defeat reads a settled city only; a unit card in the settle section is refused before the settle and playable after it, on turn 0, and enters on the city's tile as always; a settle-section card of any kind played on turn 0 leaves the chronicle; the settle refuses a tile whose slot is filled, so a camp's tile never takes the city; the hand on turn 0 is the settle section in the deck's order and no shuffle draws from the generator for it, so the seed's draws are the map's, the timeline's and the cycling deck's as today; a second settle card played after the city stands is content's to refuse — the stand-in has one, and no rule stops a second settle; the deal window, the capstone window and the menu stand over turn 0 as over any turn.

**Traps:**
